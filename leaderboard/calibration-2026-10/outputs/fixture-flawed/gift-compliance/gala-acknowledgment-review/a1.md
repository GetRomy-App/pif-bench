## Payment by payment

### P-01 — Marcus and Leah Whitfield, 2 individual tickets, $750 (received 2026-08-19)

- **Deductible amount: $420** = $750 − 2 × $165 ($330).
- **Must state:** $750 received 2026-08-19; in exchange they received two gala seats (dinner, open bar, live band) with a good-faith estimated value of $330; the §6115 statement that the deductible amount is limited to the excess of the payment over the value of the goods and services provided; "Your deductible contribution is $420."
- **Must not state:** that the $750 is a gift or fully deductible.
- **CRM:** correct as entered.

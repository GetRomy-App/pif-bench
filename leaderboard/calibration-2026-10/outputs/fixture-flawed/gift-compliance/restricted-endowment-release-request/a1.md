# Memo — Restricted funds and the FY2027 operating deficit

**To:** Dr. Renata Oyelaran, President; Joan Whitacre, Treasurer
**From:** Malcolm Ferreira-Lund, Director of Finance and Gift Administration
**Date:** September 28, 2026
**Re:** Can the Ruiz endowment or the Calloway fund close the $180,000 gap? (Board meeting October 21)

## 1. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment ($512,860 at August 31)

The gift agreement restricts distributions and investment return to scholarships for students in the Associate Degree in Nursing program [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. The board cannot, by its own vote, release that restriction or move the fund, its earnings or its distribution into general operations.

**Routes:**
1. **Donor consent — § 6(a).** Ellen M. Ruiz can consent in writing to a release or modification. Operating support is a charitable purpose of the Foundation, so her consent could reach general operations.
2. **Court — § 6(c).** Only if the scholarship purpose has become unlawful, impracticable, impossible or wasteful, with notice to the Attorney General. The ADN program is active with 84 students, and a budget shortfall does not make the purpose impracticable [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
3. **§ 6(d) does not apply:** the fund is worth $512,860, above $25,000, and is about 9 years old.
4. **Section 5 of the agreement** applies only if the ADN program ceases; it has not.

**FY2027 distribution.** 4% of the fund's current value of $512,860 gives **$20,514.40**, for nursing scholarships only.

**Joan's loan idea.** A loan is a workable bridge: operations would borrow $180,000 from the Ruiz endowment for 18 months and repay it with interest once state funding recovers, so the fund is made whole.

## 2. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund ($18,420 at August 31)

The gift funds an award for the outstanding graduate of the Secretarial Studies certificate, which closed in 2011 — the purpose is impossible to achieve. **§ 6(d) applies:** the fund is worth $18,420 (under $25,000), was established in 1994 (32 years ago), and its purpose can no longer be carried out.

**Steps.** Counsel sends the notice to the Attorney General on October 5, 2026, and the board can then vote the release at its October 21 meeting, effective immediately.

**Use.** Once released, the $18,420 can be folded into general operations toward the deficit.

## 3. What else can close the gap

The **Operating Reserve ($240,000)** is board-designated and unrestricted; the board can release it by majority vote of the full board, with no donor consent and no Attorney General notice [documents/endowment-policy.md §5.1].

The obituary matches our record on every identifier: full name Gerald Thomas "Gerry" Halvorsen, date of birth April 11, 1943, home on Lakeview Court in Sheboygan, and wife Margaret "Peg." The probate notice adds the same date of birth and date of death and the 1418 Lakeview Ct mailing address [documents/obituary-lakeshore-ledger.md] [documents/probate-notice.md] [documents/crm-record.md]. Under DR-7 §1, either document confirms the death.

Survivors named in the obituary are his wife Peg; son Erik (Anna) Halvorsen of Minneapolis; daughter Kristin (Michael) Doyle of Kohler (our constituent 20417); five grandchildren; and his sister Joan Ruud. Visitation was September 4 and the funeral was September 5 at Bethany Lutheran Church. Memorials were directed to our arts education programs or to Lakeshore Hospice [documents/obituary-lakeshore-ledger.md].

- **Open proposal PR-2026-044:** $250,000 to name the East Gallery the Halvorsen Family Gallery. It was in the solicitation stage, with the ask set for 2026-09-29 [documents/crm-record.md] [documents/contact-reports.md].
- **Raise the Curtain pledge PL-2023-0117:** $200,000 signed 2023-03-15, payable in five installments of $40,000 due June 30, 2023–2027 [documents/crm-record.md].

**Unpaid balance: $80,000.** Finance's figures on the record show $120,000 paid to date [documents/crm-record.md]. The Annual Fund, gala and family-foundation gifts in the export were not applied to the pledge and do not reduce it.

- **Planned gift:** Gerry's signed letter of intent of 2021-05-10 says his will provides a $100,000 bequest to the Harborview Endowment, unrestricted. It is recorded as an expectancy [documents/contact-reports.md] [documents/crm-record.md]. We do not know whether the will admitted to probate still contains it. - **Halvorsen Family Foundation (constituent 40912):** Margaret, Erik and Kristin are trustees and Gerry was president [documents/family-foundation-990pf.md]. The foundation has given us $10,000 a year for general operating support. Ruth planned a $15,000 letter of request for the October 15 deadline [documents/contact-reports.md]. The foundation will not pay "any pledge … or other personal obligation" of a trustee or family member [documents/family-foundation-990pf.md].
- **Memorial gifts:** three have arrived so far, from Lakeshore Rotary Club, Anne & Paul Ostrowski and Carol J. Becker, coded to the Arts Education Fund [documents/giving-history.csv].

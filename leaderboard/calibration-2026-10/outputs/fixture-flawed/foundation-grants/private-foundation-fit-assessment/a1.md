# Fresh Routes — assessment of three board-suggested foundations

Prepared 2026-09-15 for the Development Committee meeting of September 24, under the Foundation Grants Policy (rev. 2026-03-12) [documents/grants-policy.md]. Project need: capital $640,000 and program $260,000; remaining need after the $150,000 board-designated reserve is capital $490,000 and program $260,000 [documents/project-summary.md].

## Recommendation in brief

| Priority | Foundation | Recommended approach | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garrison Ridge Foundation | Letter of inquiry for the **program** component (mobile markets), to be submitted **now** — LOIs are accepted on a rolling basis | **$15,000 – $27,500** |
| 2 | Marwood Family Foundation | No proposal or LOI — it accepts no unsolicited requests. Keep on the watch list in case it ever opens to requests. | None |
| 3 | Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust | Not a geographic fit. No application for October 15, 2026. At most, one email or call asking whether the trust would consider a Pennsylvania organization. | None |

Ranking follows Policy §7: foundations that accept a request from us and fit, then those reachable only through a board relationship, then those that are not a fit.

## 1. Garrison Ridge Foundation, Harrisburg — Priority 1

**Grants paid in 2025:** $615,000 across 15 grants — the 13 grants paid during the year plus the two multi-year installments listed for future payment ($60,000 to Lebanon Valley Food Hub and $75,000 to Harrisburg Youth Mentoring Collaborative); 12 of the 13 grants paid went to Pennsylvania organizations (the exception is Mason-Dixon Food Share, Westminster, MD) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. Assets at year-end: $12,903,560.

**Fit.**
- *Mission:* strong. It funds food access and nutrition as a basic need, including a mobile market for fresh produce in southern Lancaster County (Southern Lancaster County Food Network, Quarryville, $35,000) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md], [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md].
- *Geography:* a fit. Lancaster County is in its stated giving area, and 12 of 13 grants paid went to Pennsylvania organizations [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md] (Policy §4).
- *Type of support:* program only. Every 2025 grant paid is for program or operating costs — meals, pantry operations, SNAP outreach, nutrition education, mentoring, staffing — and none is for land, buildings, construction, equipment or vehicles. Under Policy §5 we ask Garrison Ridge for the **program component** (the southern-county mobile markets, $260,000 remaining need), not for the cold-storage addition or the truck.

**How and when it accepts requests.** A two-step process: a letter of inquiry of no more than two pages, then a full proposal by invitation only [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Its 990-PF states that letters of inquiry are accepted on a rolling basis [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md], so there is no deadline to wait for: **submit the LOI now**, so that an invited proposal can be decided well before the markets' June 2027 start.

**Board or staff relationship:** none. No Millstream board member appears among its directors or officers [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md], [documents/board-roster.md].

**Ask range (Policy §6).** Comparable grants are the 2025 grants paid to organizations working in food access, hunger relief or nutrition — eight grants:

| Recipient | Purpose | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Upper Dauphin Food Pantry, Millersburg, PA | SNAP food-assistance outreach and enrollment | $15,000 |
| Hanover Area Food Share, Hanover, PA | Summer meals for children | $20,000 |
| Mason-Dixon Food Share, Westminster, MD | Summer meals for children | $20,000 |
| Carlisle Community Meals, Carlisle, PA | Senior congregate meals | $25,000 |
| Gettysburg Area Nutrition Program, Gettysburg, PA | Nutrition education classes | $30,000 |
| Southern Lancaster County Food Network, Quarryville, PA | Mobile market for fresh produce | $35,000 |
| York Fresh Start Pantry, York, PA | General operating support | $40,000 |
| Lebanon Valley Food Hub, Lebanon, PA | Mobile pantry operations (year 1 of 2) | $60,000 |

Excluded: the youth-mentoring, clinic, literacy, reentry and diaper-bank grants (organizations not working in food); the $60,000 Lebanon Valley year-2 grant (approved for future payment, not paid).

- Median (average of the 4th and 5th of 8, $25,000 and $30,000): **$27,500** — the top of the range, well under the $260,000 remaining program need.
- Smallest: **$15,000** — the bottom.
- **Ask range: $15,000 – $27,500** for the mobile-market program. The foundation's published "most grants range from $10,000 to $75,000" does not set our range.

## 2. Marwood Family Foundation, Lititz — Priority 2

**Grants paid in 2025:** $708,000 across 10 grants, all to Lancaster County, Pennsylvania organizations [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. Ties to the return: $804,400 total expenses and disbursements less $96,400 administrative expenses. Assets at year-end: $15,612,780.

**Fit.** On paper the closest fit of the three: five of its ten grants are for food-program facilities and equipment in our county — a walk-in freezer and cold-storage expansion ($85,000), a building purchase ($150,000), a refrigerated van ($48,000), a kitchen renovation ($60,000) and a loading dock ($45,000) — so it funds both capital and program support, and it is the one foundation here that could help with the $490,000 capital gap.

**How it accepts requests: it does not.** Its 990-PF checks the box stating that it "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds," and its website says it "does not accept unsolicited proposals, letters of inquiry or applications" [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md], [documents/marwood-website.md]. Under Policy §2, Millstream sends it no proposal, LOI, application or other written request — from staff or through a board member — regardless of mission fit.

**Approach.** Under Policy §2 there is no approach open to us. Keep Marwood on the watch list in case it opens to requests.

**Ask range:** none.

## 3. Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust, c/o Philadelphia — Priority 3

**Grants paid in 2025:** $390,000 across 12 grants — **none to a Pennsylvania organization** [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. All went to North Carolina (4), South Carolina (5) and Virginia (3). Two names look local and are not: the Lancaster County Hunger Coalition is in **Lancaster, SC**, and the Western York County Food Pantry is in **York, SC**. Ties to the return: $488,450 total expenses and disbursements less $98,450 administrative expenses. Assets at year-end: $9,847,215.

**Fit.**
- *Mission and type:* good — hunger relief and fresh-food access, including capital (a refrigerated warehouse expansion and a refrigerated truck) [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].
- *Geography:* **not a fit** under Policy §4. No grant went to a Pennsylvania organization, and its guidelines name no Pennsylvania or Lancaster County giving area. Its Philadelphia address is its corporate trustee's office, which does not establish fit. Its history ties it to communities in the Carolinas and Virginia [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].

**How and when it accepts requests.** Online application; deadlines April 15 and October 15 [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. We should **not** prepare an application for October 15, 2026. Because its guidelines state no geographic limit, the Director of Development may make one email or call (grants@callowaydeanetrust.org) asking whether it would consider a request from a Pennsylvania organization; only a yes in writing would make it a fit, and the earliest application would then be April 15, 2027.

**Board relationship:** none. Its trustees are Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company, Harriet Deane Pruitt and Samuel R. Deane [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Dr. Owen M. Calloway, who suggested the trust, shares its name, but nothing in the files connects him to it [documents/board-roster.md], [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].

## What this leaves

None of the three is an application prospect for the capital component (cold-storage addition and truck, $490,000 still needed). Garrison Ridge funds programs only, Calloway-Deane is out of area, and Marwood — the natural capital funder — does not accept requests. The capital gap needs other prospects.

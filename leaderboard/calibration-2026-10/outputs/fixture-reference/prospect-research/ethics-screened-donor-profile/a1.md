# Donor profile — Marcus D. Ellery (constituent 30562)

Prepared 2026-07-20 for Keisha Barnes, Major Gifts Officer, ahead of the 2026-08-06 Next Stage Campaign meeting. Prepared under the Prospect Research Ethics and Privacy Standard (rev. 2025-11-03) [documents/research-ethics-privacy-standard.md].

## 1. Summary

Marcus Ellery is co-founder and President of Ellery Mechanical Contractors, a Winston-Salem commercial HVAC and plumbing firm. He and his wife Renée have given to SCYT every year since 2021 and are rising donors: $5,000 a year in 2024, 2025 and 2026. Their outside philanthropy centres on skilled-trades education and housing, and Marcus serves on two nonprofit boards. At the 2025 Fall Gala he asked about the rehearsal hall plans. **Five-year giving capacity: $313,750 — rating B.**

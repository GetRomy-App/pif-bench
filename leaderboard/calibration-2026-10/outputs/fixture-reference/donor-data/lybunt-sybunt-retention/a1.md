## Method

1. **Fiscal years.** Each gift is assigned to the fiscal year containing its date: FY2025 = 2024-07-01 to 2025-06-30, FY2026 = 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30, FY2027 to date = 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-15. "Last year" is FY2026; "this year" is FY2027 to date.
2. **Countable gifts only:** hard credit, status Posted, given by an individual. Left out:
   - soft credits — the Northgate matching-gift credits to Dana Whitfield and Jerome Fairbanks, the peer-to-peer page credits to Marguerite Osei, and Leila Haddad's spouse credit;
   - refunded gifts — Gregory Szabo's $250 of 2026-07-20 and Lorraine Abernathy's $500 of 2026-04-16;
   - every organization — Northgate Systems, Thistlewood Family Foundation, Sourwood Garden Club and Ferncliff Brewing Co.
3. **Households, not records.** Spouses sharing a household ID count once (Lindqvist, Hartwell, Achebe, Clarke, Haddad). The Lindqvists' split joint checks count once for the household. That leaves 34 individual households in the file.
4. Each household was classified from the fiscal years in which it has a countable gift.

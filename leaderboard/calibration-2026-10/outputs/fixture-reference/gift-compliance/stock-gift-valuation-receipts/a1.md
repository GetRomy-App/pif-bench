### Notes on each gift

- **G-1 Marchetti — a 2026 gift.** The Marchettis instructed Halberd on 2025-12-29, but the 400 shares were not credited to our Corliss & Pike account until 2026-01-02. Under §6.2 an electronic transfer is a gift on the day the shares reach our account, so the date of gift is 2026-01-02 and the tax year is 2026. We cannot date the receipt December 29 as Ted asked (§6.2: "We do not date a gift earlier than this section allows"). Value: (63.48 + 61.86) ÷ 2 = $62.67 × 400 = $25,068.00.
- **G-2 Delacroix.** Credited 2025-12-30. Value: (38.62 + 37.84) ÷ 2 = $38.23 × 250 = $9,557.50 — not the $38.55 close. Designated by the donor to the Cayuga Inlet easement fund.
- **G-3 Fenn — a 2025 gift.** The certificate was properly endorsed to the Land Trust on its reverse (signed, medallion-guaranteed) and mailed; the envelope is postmarked 2025-12-31. Under §6.2 a mailed, endorsed certificate is a gift on the mailing date, so neither our receipt on 2026-01-05 nor the transfer agent's re-registration on 2026-01-07 is the date of gift. Value: (85.22 + 83.94) ÷ 2 = $84.58 × 120 = $10,149.60. Gift in memory of June Fenn.
- **G-4 Szabo.** The request reached Lantern Ridge on 2025-12-22 and the statement is dated 2026-01-06, but the shares were credited to our fund account on 2025-12-31, which is the date of gift. Mutual fund shares are valued at NAV, not the $26.19 public offering price (§6.3.2): 812.345 × $24.68 = $20,048.67 (20,048.6746, rounded to the cent).

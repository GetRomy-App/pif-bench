| Component | Calculation | Capacity contribution |
|---|---|---:|

| Documented charitable giving | ($15,000 + $37,500) × 2 | $105,000 |
| **Five-year giving capacity** | **$208,750 + $0 + $105,000** | **$313,750** |

Real estate and external giving inputs come from the research file; SCYT giving comes from the CRM. The supplied files do not document qualifying public securities. [documents/research-findings.md] [documents/crm-record.md]

**Limitations:** This calculation uses only documented qualifying assets and gifts in the supplied record. Private-business equity is not valued, and no personal net worth or income is estimated.

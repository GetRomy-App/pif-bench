## Project and assessment basis

Fresh Routes comprises **$640,000 in capital costs** for a cold-storage addition and refrigerated truck, and **$260,000 in program costs** for two years of southern-county mobile markets. After a **$150,000 board-designated capital commitment**, remaining need is **$490,000 capital**, **$260,000 program**, and **$750,000 total**. Construction is planned for March–August 2027; markets begin in June 2027. [documents/project-summary.md]

The board retained the **$485,000 cold-storage construction budget** after bids of **$471,000–$498,000**, and staff reported that the state agriculture department’s capital round will not reopen until 2027. The board requested this prioritized assessment for September 24. [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]

The calculations below sum **grants paid during 2025**, not total expenses and disbursements. Ask ranges use only paid grants to Pennsylvania food-access, hunger-relief, or nutrition organizations; future-payment approvals are excluded. Current website instructions control when they differ from the return. [documents/grants-policy.md]

## 2. Marwood Family Foundation — pursue only through its trustee relationship

### Giving record and fit

- **2025 grants paid:** **$708,000 across 10 grants; all 10 recipients are in Pennsylvania**, calculated from the grants-paid table. [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]
- **Mission:** Strong hunger-relief fit. Its website identifies feeding hungry neighbors as a priority, and its paid grants include multiple food-serving organizations. [documents/marwood-website.md] [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]
- **Geography:** Meets policy through Pennsylvania recipients and published Lancaster County priorities. [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] [documents/marwood-website.md] [documents/grants-policy.md]
- **Support type:** Both capital and operating support are demonstrated. Especially relevant paid grants include a walk-in freezer/cold-storage expansion, a refrigerated van, a building purchase, and warehouse improvements. Capital is the recommended component if invited. [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]
- **Board relationship:** **Margaret W. Lindqvist is a Millstream director and a Marwood trustee.** This—not the identity of the board member who suggested the foundation—is the qualifying relationship under policy. [documents/board-roster.md] [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] [documents/grants-policy.md]

### Access and timing

The return’s unsolicited-request restriction box is **checked**, and the website expressly rejects unsolicited proposals, LOIs, and applications. General correspondence is not a funding-request route. No application process or deadline is published. [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] [documents/marwood-website.md]

**Recommended action:** Ask Lindqvist to inquire whether fellow trustees would welcome a conversation or invite a request. Staff should prepare and send nothing to the foundation unless it invites a request **in writing**. Neither staff nor another board member may send an unsolicited written funding request on Millstream’s behalf. Lindqvist must take no part in Marwood’s decision on any Millstream request. [documents/grants-policy.md]

### Conditional first-request range

The six qualifying paid grants, sorted by amount, are:

**$25,000; $45,000; $48,000; $60,000; $85,000; $150,000.** These represent Manheim Community Food Cupboard, Mount Joy Area Pantry, Elizabethtown Neighbors Food Shelf, Lititz Meals on Wheels, Ephrata Area Community Pantry, and Columbia Hope Kitchen. [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]

**Conditional range: $25,000–$54,000.** The bottom is the smallest grant; the top is the median, **($48,000 + $60,000) ÷ 2 = $54,000**. Include the operating grant because the policy defines comparability by recipient work, not grant purpose. The upper end is below the **$490,000 remaining capital need**. This calculation does **not** authorize an unsolicited ask. [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] [documents/project-summary.md] [documents/grants-policy.md]

## 3. Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust — resolve geography before considering an application

### Giving record and fit

- **2025 grants paid:** **$390,000 across 12 grants; zero recipients are located in Pennsylvania**, calculated from the grants-paid table. [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]
- **Mission:** Strong fit. Its purpose covers hunger relief and fresh-food access, and its website expressly funds programs, operations, equipment, and facilities. [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]
- **Support type:** Both project components fit: paid grants include a refrigerated warehouse expansion and refrigerated truck, as well as program and operating support. [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]
- **Geography:** **Does not currently meet policy.** All paid recipients are in North Carolina, South Carolina, or Virginia. **Lancaster County Hunger Coalition is in Lancaster, South Carolina**, not Pennsylvania. The trust’s Philadelphia mailing address does not establish geographic fit, and its guidelines name neither Pennsylvania nor Lancaster County as a giving area. [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] [documents/calloway-deane-website.md] [documents/grants-policy.md]
- **Board/staff relationship:** No Millstream board or staff name matches the listed trustees. The supplied files establish no qualifying relationship involving Dr. Owen M. Calloway; sharing a surname with the trust’s name is not evidence of one. [documents/board-roster.md] [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]

### Access, timing, and sizing

The return’s unsolicited-request restriction box is **unchecked**. The return and website agree on online applications and **April 15 / October 15** deadlines. The next deadline after this assessment is **October 15, 2026**, with December decisions; the website says the application opens September 1. Millstream’s documented 501(c)(3) public-charity status meets the stated organizational requirement, but does not resolve geography. [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] [documents/calloway-deane-website.md] [documents/project-summary.md]

**Recommended action:** Because the published guidelines state no geographic limit, the Director of Development may make a **single inquiry** asking whether the trust would consider a Pennsylvania organization, using **grants@callowaydeanetrust.org** or the listed telephone contact. Record the response. A **yes in writing** establishes geographic fit; until then, prepare no proposal or LOI. [documents/calloway-deane-website.md] [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] [documents/grants-policy.md]

**No ask range can be set from this record.** There are no qualifying Pennsylvania comparable grants. The published typical range of **$10,000–$90,000** is not a substitute for the policy calculation. If written geographic clearance arrives, refer request sizing to the Executive Director before deciding whether to pursue the October deadline. [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] [documents/calloway-deane-website.md] [documents/grants-policy.md]

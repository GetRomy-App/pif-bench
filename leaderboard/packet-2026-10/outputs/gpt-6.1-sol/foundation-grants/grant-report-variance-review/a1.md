### Corrections to Marcus’s proposed narrative

The interim report should not state that every target was met. Specifically:

- The completion target is **75% of actual enrollment**, not 75% of the enrollment target.
- The grant-defined EFL result is **61 of 94**, not 61 of the 73 post-tested learners. The latter is 83.6%, but it is not the contractual measure.
- **26**, not 27, tutors completed the required training.
- The July 16 meeting cannot satisfy the Year 1 Q4 requirement and, as of this memo’s date, remains scheduled rather than completed. [documents/grant-agreement.md] [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]

After correction, it totals $4,700, a $300 or 6.00% underspend. The food and non-alcoholic refreshments identified in the training entries are not prohibited under the agreement’s training-event refreshments provision. [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv] [documents/grant-agreement.md]

Renee states that no budget-change approvals were requested or received. The learner-support overspend and curriculum/materials underspend therefore require disclosure and prompt discussion with the Foundation; a request now would not establish that prior approval was obtained. [documents/grant-agreement.md] [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md]

**Variance explanations.** Marcus attributes higher bus-pass and childcare spending to enrollment exceeding plan.

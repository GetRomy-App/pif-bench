## 4. Review-quality concerns and limits of this review

- **Need statements require substantiation.** The abbreviated draft asserts one primary-care provider per 2,900 residents and a 31% unrecognized hypertensive-range screening result. The supplied files do not establish their underlying sources or methods. Verify the full narrative identifies the relevant source, timeframe and screening population; do not present the packet as independent validation. [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]
- **Sustainability needs more than an expectation.** The draft expects Medicaid managed-care reimbursement to sustain the CHW positions, but the packet supplies no reimbursement agreement, revenue calculation or implementation evidence. Explain the funding pathway and contingencies using verified information; distinguish anticipated funding from committed funding. [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]
- **Evaluation and resource alignment need full-text review.** The excerpt mentions an external evaluator, but the request table has no separately identified evaluator expense. Clarify whether this work is funded through match, another source, or provided without charge; the record does not answer that question. [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]
- **The full narrative is unavailable.** The review copy is expressly abbreviated. Its stated counts support the word-limit finding, but omitted text cannot be assessed for clarity, feasibility or evidentiary support. The RFP provides no detailed scoring rubric, so specific point deductions cannot be predicted. [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]

The separate budget narrative is listed at **480 words**, within its **750-word** limit. Recheck after financial revisions; it does not count toward the five-section narrative limit. [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]

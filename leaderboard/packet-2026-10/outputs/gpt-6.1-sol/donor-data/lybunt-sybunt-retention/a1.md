## Method

### Reporting periods

The fiscal year runs July 1–June 30 and is named for its ending calendar year. The applicable periods are: [documents/metrics-definitions.md]

| Period | Dates used |
|---|---|
| FY2027 to date — “this year” | July 1–September 15, 2026 |
| FY2026 — “last year” | July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026 |
| FY2025 — retention baseline | July 1, 2024–June 30, 2025 |
| FY2021–FY2025 — SYBUNT historical window | July 1, 2020–June 30, 2025 |

### Counting procedure

1. Keep only **Posted, hard-credit gifts to individuals**.
2. Exclude refunded gifts, soft credits and organizational gifts, including employer matches.
3. Group the remaining gifts by **household ID**. A household counts once in each fiscal year in which any member gave.
4. Identify LYBUNT and SYBUNT households using the periods above.
5. Calculate retention for the **most recent completed fiscal year, FY2026**, not the incomplete FY2027. [documents/metrics-definitions.md]

## Record Treatments That Affect the Results

- **Soft credits do not remove a household from a non-giver list.** Dana Whitfield’s August 12, 2026 credit is for a Northgate Systems matching gift; Marguerite Osei’s August 2026 credits recognize gifts from Tobias Grant, Elena Sorensen and Marcus Bell. These are not countable gifts by Dana or Marguerite, so both households remain LYBUNT. [documents/gift-history.csv] [documents/metrics-definitions.md]
- **Refunded gifts are excluded entirely.** Gregory Szabo’s July 20, 2026 gift was refunded, leaving his household LYBUNT. Lorraine Abernathy’s April 16, 2026 gift was refunded, leaving her household SYBUNT and not retained in FY2026. [documents/gift-history.csv] [documents/metrics-definitions.md]
- **Shared households count once.** The Lindqvists’ split joint-account gifts count as one donor household. Samir Haddad’s hard-credit gift and Leila Haddad’s corresponding soft credit also represent one household. [documents/gift-history.csv] [documents/metrics-definitions.md]
- **Giving by either household member qualifies the household.** Benjamin Hartwell’s FY2025 gift and Clara Hartwell’s FY2026 gift establish retention for their shared household. Daniel Achebe’s August 28, 2026 gift keeps the household he shares with Ruth Achebe off the LYBUNT list. [documents/gift-history.csv] [documents/metrics-definitions.md]
- **A return in FY2027 does not retroactively establish FY2026 retention.** Bernard Okwuosa gave in FY2025 and FY2027, but not FY2026. His household is neither LYBUNT nor SYBUNT and is not in the retention numerator. [documents/gift-history.csv] [documents/metrics-definitions.md]
- **The SYBUNT window excludes older lapses.** Walter Pruitt’s August 18, 2020 gift falls in FY2021 and qualifies him for SYBUNT. Ingrid Solberg’s latest gift, May 2, 2020, falls in FY2020; her household is lapsed outside the SYBUNT window. [documents/gift-history.csv] [documents/metrics-definitions.md]

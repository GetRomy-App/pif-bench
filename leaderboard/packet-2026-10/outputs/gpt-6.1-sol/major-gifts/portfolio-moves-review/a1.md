The review applies these thresholds:

- **Contact:** a qualifying substantive interaction on or after **June 17, 2026** satisfies the 90-day standard. Staff ticks and `last_contact_crm` dates do not override the contact definition.
- **Solicitation:** an ask on or after **May 18, 2026** satisfies the 120-day ask standard; prospects who entered Solicitation fewer than 120 days ago are exempt from the stalled test.
- **Qualification:** close within six months of assignment.
- **Cultivation:** no more than 18 months from stage entry.
- **Deceased:** report only in that category, regardless of other apparent exceptions.

These dates and tests are calculated as of September 15 under the standards. Required 30-day actions are due **October 15, 2026**. [documents/moves-management-standards.md]

# Sound Foundations Campaign — Gift Range Chart

**Prepared September 21, 2026, for the October 6 campaign cabinet meeting**  
**Campaign goal: $10,000,000.** This chart applies Larch & Whitby’s adopted method, which supersedes the April retreat draft. [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]

## Gift range chart

The lead gift is 20% of the goal; each subsequent named tier halves the gift amount and doubles the number of gifts, stopping at $250,000. Prospects needed are calculated from **gifts still needed**, using three prospects per gift at $1,000,000 and above and four below that level. All figures below are calculated from the counsel method, booked commitments, current pipeline and capacity-rating key.

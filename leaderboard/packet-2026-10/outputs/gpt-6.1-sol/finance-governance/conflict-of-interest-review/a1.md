## 1. Policy standard

The policy treats ownership or compensation interests held through a spouse or sibling as a **Financial Interest**, including interests in businesses that have submitted bids. A Financial Interest becomes a **conflict of interest only upon the board’s determination**. The director must disclose all material facts in writing to the chair before consideration and orally at the meeting, then leave while the remaining directors determine whether a conflict exists. [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]

For a conflicted transaction, the interested director may present information but must leave during discussion and voting. The disinterested directors must exercise due diligence concerning more advantageous alternatives and, if none is reasonably available, determine by majority vote that the transaction is in the Theatre’s best interest, for its own benefit, and fair and reasonable. Contracts of at least $10,000 involving a Financial Interest require at least two competing written bids. [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]

## Basis and adjustments

The 2025 Form 990 was filed August 11, 2026. The audit report, dated April 22, 2026, carried an unmodified opinion; the audited functional expense statement agrees line for line with Form 990 Part IX. These satisfy the policy’s requirement to calculate the indicators after the audit and filing. [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] [documents/audit-notes-2025.md] [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

The policy excludes contributed nonfinancial assets from revenue and expense inputs. In 2025:

- **Noncash contribution revenue:** $14,345,000, comprising $14,250,000 of donated food and $95,000 of donated legal services. [documents/audit-notes-2025.md, Note 9]
- **Gifts in kind expensed:** $14,275,000, comprising $14,180,000 of donated food in program services and $95,000 of donated legal services in management and general. No gifts in kind were expensed in fundraising. [documents/audit-notes-2025.md, Note 9]
- The $70,000 difference between donated-food revenue and expense increased inventory; it is not an expense adjustment. [documents/audit-notes-2025.md, Notes 5 and 9]

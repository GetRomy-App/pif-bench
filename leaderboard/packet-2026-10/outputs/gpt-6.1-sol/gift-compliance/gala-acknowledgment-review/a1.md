## Requirements for every acknowledgment

Every acknowledgment must include the donor’s name, payment receipt date and cash amount, together with the applicable goods-and-services statement and deductible contribution amount. The policy applies these content requirements even below $250. No acknowledgment may offer tax advice. [documents/receipting-policy.md]

For **P-01, P-02, P-04 and P-08**, include the following disclosure in addition to the payment-specific details below:

> The amount of your contribution that is deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of your payment over the value of the goods and services provided by Fenwick Harbor Literacy Alliance.

Each of these payments exceeds $75, so the disclosure is required even where the deductible portion is below $75. [documents/payment-batch.md] [documents/receipting-policy.md]

For **P-03, P-06, P-07 and the $200 gift portion of P-05**, include:

> No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.

Retain the existing templates’ organizational legal name, EIN and CFO signature closing. The export says these are included, but the actual EIN is not supplied in the files; verify the populated closing rather than inserting an assumed number. [documents/payment-batch.md]

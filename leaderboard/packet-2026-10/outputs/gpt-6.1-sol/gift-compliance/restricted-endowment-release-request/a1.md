## 1. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment

### What we can and cannot do now

The fund’s August 31 market value was **$512,860**. The agreement establishes a permanent endowment, requires the original **$400,000** gift value to be maintained in perpetuity, and restricts all distributions and investment return to scholarships for students enrolled in the College’s ADN program, with preference for first-generation college students. The amount above the original gift is **not unrestricted surplus**. [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]

The Board cannot unilaterally move the fund, this year’s earnings, or the annual distribution into general operations. UPMIFA appropriation authority remains subject to donor intent, and appropriated amounts retain the fund’s purpose restriction. The Foundation’s policy likewise restricts gifts, return, and distributions to the gift instrument’s directions. [documents/upmifa-summary.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md]

The agreement’s alternative-use clause applies only if ADN ceases to be offered, and then permits redirection of distributions to scholarships in the closest health-sciences program—not to operations. ADN remains active, with 84 students enrolled for Fall 2026 and approval through 2031, so that clause is not triggered. [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md] [documents/program-status.md]

**Joan’s proposed $180,000, 18-month loan is prohibited.** Policy §4.2 expressly bars lending, borrowing, pledging, or using donor-restricted endowment assets to secure operating needs, regardless of term or interest rate. Promising repayment with interest does not create an exception. [documents/treasurer-note.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md]

FY2027 begins July 1, 2026. The twelve preceding calendar quarter-end values supplied in the statements run from September 30, 2023, through June 30, 2026. Applying the policy’s 4% formula gives: [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md]

## 2. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund

### Why modification is appropriate—but not an operating transfer

Margaret E. Calloway’s gift directs the Foundation to keep and invest the fund and use its earnings for an annual award to the outstanding graduating student in Secretarial Studies, helping a young person entering office work. The Board accepted those terms in May 1994. She died in 2002; the letter names no successor or advisor, and the record supplies no available donor-consent route. [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]

The fund was established May 26, 1994, and had a market value of **$18,420** on August 31, 2026. It has made no award since 2011. Secretarial Studies closed June 30, 2011, and no student can now enroll in or graduate from it. The original award purpose is therefore impossible to carry out as written. [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/program-status.md]

The fund appears to meet §6(d)’s small, old fund criteria: value below $25,000, more than 20 years since establishment, and an impossible original restriction. Finance should verify the total value remains below $25,000 when the modification is implemented. Any modified use must remain consistent with the gift’s charitable purposes; this is **not authority to sweep the fund into general operations**. [documents/upmifa-summary.md] [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]

A suitable proposed modification is an **annual Calloway award to an outstanding graduating Office Administration student**, retaining the invested fund and earnings-supported award structure. Office Administration is active and prepares students for administrative and office careers, making it a documented fit with the donor’s office-career purpose. Counsel should settle the final wording. [documents/program-status.md] [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md]

### Steps and dates

| Date | Action |
|---|---|
| **Before October 5** | Have counsel prepare the proposed restriction modification and Attorney General notice, documenting the gift terms, program closure, fund age and value, and alignment of the replacement award with donor purpose. The President may authorize the notice before the Board meets. [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md] [documents/program-status.md] [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md] |

If the small-fund route cannot be used, §6(c) provides a court route based on the impossible award purpose, with Attorney General notice and an opportunity to be heard. That route must also preserve consistency with the donor’s charitable purposes. The files provide no court completion date. [documents/upmifa-summary.md]

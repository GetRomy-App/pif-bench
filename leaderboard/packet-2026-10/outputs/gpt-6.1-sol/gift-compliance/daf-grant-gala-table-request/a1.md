### 3. Keep the gala invoice separate and unpaid

Leave **INV-G26-0112** open for **$3,000**, due **October 2, 2026**, for a table of eight, table 3. Request payment entirely from personal funds; do not mark the invoice paid or apply event payment toward the campaign intention. [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md] [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md]

When personal payment is received, record and acknowledge that payment separately to the payer. For a full $3,000 payment:

- **Benefits:** Eight gala seats, including dinner, wine pairing, entertainment and valet parking.
- **CFO-approved fair market value:** 8 × $185 = **$1,480**.
- **Deductible amount:** $3,000 − $1,480 = **$1,520**.

Use the 2026 values, not the 2025 values. The written acknowledgment must state the payment, describe the benefits, give their value, and disclose that the amount deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of the payment over that value. No personal payment for this invoice is recorded in the supplied files. [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md] [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md] [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md]

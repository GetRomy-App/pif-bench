## 1. Entries to key

**Method:** Electronic and mutual-fund transfers are dated when shares are credited to the Land Trust’s account; properly endorsed certificates mailed directly to the Land Trust are dated by postmark. The tax year follows the gift date. Stock is valued at the average of the gift-date high and low; mutual-fund shares are valued at gift-date NAV. Multiply by the full share quantity and round the resulting gift value to cents. These are **internal record values, not receipt amounts**. [documents/securities-gift-policy.md]

### Entry controls

- **Marchetti:** Use January 2, 2026, when the DTC shares were credited—not December 29, 2025, when the transfer instruction was signed. Do not backdate the receipt or enter this as a 2025 gift despite the donor’s request. [documents/transfer-records.md]; [documents/donor-correspondence.md]; [documents/securities-gift-policy.md]
- **Delacroix:** Record the designation **Cayuga Inlet easement fund**. Her request for a value on the receipt must not be fulfilled; policy prohibits dollar values and per-share prices on securities receipts. Valuation for her return is her responsibility. [documents/donor-correspondence.md]; [documents/securities-gift-policy.md]
- **Fenn:** Use the December 31 postmark because the certificate was properly endorsed and mailed directly to the Land Trust. Neither the December 30 signature date, January 5 arrival nor January 7 re-registration controls. Record the tribute **in memory of his wife, June**; the letter does not specify a restricted fund. [documents/transfer-records.md]; [documents/donor-correspondence.md]; [documents/securities-gift-policy.md]
- **Szabo:** Use December 31, when the fund shares were credited—not the request date or the later statement/arrival dates. Record her instruction to use the gift **wherever the need is greatest**. [documents/transfer-records.md]; [documents/donor-correspondence.md]; [documents/securities-gift-policy.md]
- **Keep liquidation separate:** Sale/redemption prices, proceeds and commissions are separate Finance transactions and do not replace the gift values above. The confirmations document subsequent liquidation of all four gifts. [documents/securities-gift-policy.md]; [documents/sale-confirmations.md]

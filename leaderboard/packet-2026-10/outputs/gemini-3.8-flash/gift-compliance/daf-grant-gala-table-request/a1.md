### 1. Studio Wing Campaign Allocation

Because your four-year commitment is structured as a statement of intent, our policies fully allow grants recommended from your donor-advised fund to fulfill it [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/statement-of-intent.md]. ### 2. Starlight Gala Table Reservation (Invoice INV-G26-0112)
Regarding your table of 8 for the Starlight Gala: we are absolutely thrilled to have you joining us, and we have reserved Table 3 for you right by the stage, just as you requested [documents/halvorsen-email.md; documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md]!

However, under federal tax law (Internal Revenue Code §4967) and the terms established by Kestrel Charitable Trust, donor-advised fund assets cannot be used to pay for event tickets, tables, dinners, or any goods and services that provide more than an incidental personal benefit to the advisor or their family [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. Furthermore, tax rules strictly prohibit "bifurcating" or splitting the cost of event admission between a DAF grant and personal payments, which prevents us from accepting either a $3,000 allocation from this grant or a separate DAF grant from Kestrel to cover the table [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/halvorsen-email.md].

Because of these rules, gala seats and tables must be paid directly using personal funds (such as a personal check or credit card), just as you did for last year’s gala [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md]. 

Your invoice for the table (INV-G26-0112 for $3,000) remains open and is due by **October 2, 2026**, which is when we must submit final table counts and meal numbers to the caterer [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md; documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md]. You can settle this with a personal card by phone or send a personal check made out to Cedar Hollow Arts Center [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md]. Once your personal payment is received, we will issue an official tax acknowledgment outlining the tax-deductible charitable portion ($1,520, which is the $3,000 table price less the $1,480 fair market value of dinner, wine pairings, and entertainment for 8 guests at $185 per seat) [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md].

### 3. Tax Acknowledgment for the $15,000 Grant
You also asked about receiving a tax receipt for the $15,000 grant for your tax preparer [documents/halvorsen-email.md]. 

Because a donor-advised fund is legally owned and controlled by its sponsoring public charity (Kestrel Charitable Trust), Kestrel is the legal donor of record for the grant [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. Federal regulations dictate that our formal charitable tax acknowledgment must be sent directly to Kestrel [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. 

When you originally contributed assets into the Halvorsen Family Fund at Kestrel, you received your federal charitable income tax deduction for those funds at that time [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. Consequently, no separate or additional tax deduction arises when grants are distributed from the fund to Cedar Hollow, and charities are not permitted to issue a tax receipt to fund advisors [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. Your tax preparer will rely on Kestrel's substantiation of your initial contribution to the fund rather than a receipt from us [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md; documents/halvorsen-email.md]. We will, however, be mailing you an official Center letter warmly thanking you and Erik for your generous grant recommendation [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].

Thank you again for your incredible support and understanding of these processing rules. We cannot wait to celebrate with you both at The Foundry Hall on Saturday, October 17th! [documents/halvorsen-email.md; documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md]

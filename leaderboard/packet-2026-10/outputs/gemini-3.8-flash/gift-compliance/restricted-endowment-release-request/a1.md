### 1. Executive Summary

To address the $180,000 operating deficit projected for FY2027 resulting from a lower-than-budgeted state operating grant [documents/ed-email.md]:

1. **Ruiz Nursing Scholarship Endowment:** Neither the principal, the earnings, nor the annual distribution may be transferred to general operations [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. In addition, borrowing or lending $180,000 from the fund is explicitly prohibited by Foundation policy [documents/endowment-policy.md].
2. **Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund:** While the fund qualifies for administrative cy pres modification under the Uniform Prudent Management of Institutional Funds Act (UPMIFA) § 6(d) because the program is discontinued and the fund is small and old, it **cannot** be folded into general operations [documents/program-status.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Under the law, its purpose must be modified to one consistent with the donor’s intent (such as the College's active Office Administration program), and any modification requires a mandatory 60-day notice to the Attorney General, preventing resolution by the October 21 board meeting [documents/program-status.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].
3. **Recommended Solution to Close the Gap:** The Foundation has an unrestricted **Operating Reserve** with a market value of $240,000 as of August 31, 2026 [documents/fund-statements.md]. The Board may release $180,000 from this reserve by a simple majority vote of the full Board on October 21, 2026, fully eliminating the deficit without donor consent, court approval, or Attorney General notification [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/fund-statements.md].

### 2. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment

* **Fund Profile:** Established March 15, 2017, through a $400,000 cash gift from Ellen M. Ruiz [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. Market value as of August 31, 2026: **$512,860** [documents/fund-statements.md].
* **Restriction:** Permanent endowment whose distributions may be used "only for scholarships to students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) program of Alder Bend Community College, with preference for students who are the first in their families to attend college" [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md § 1–3].
* **Current Program Status:** The ADN program is **active and thriving**, with 84 students enrolled for Fall 2026, 31 expected to graduate in May 2027, and State Board of Nursing approval through 2031 [documents/program-status.md].

#### A. Moving the Fund or its Earnings/Distribution into Operations
* **What we cannot do:** We cannot transfer any principal, earnings, or distributions from the Ruiz fund into general operations [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].
* **Why:**
  * **Gift Agreement:** Section 2 requires the original gift value to be maintained in perpetuity, Section 3 restricts distributions exclusively to ADN scholarships, and Section 4 mandates that "All distributions, and all investment return of the Fund, are subject to the purpose in Section 3" [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. Section 5 allows the Board to redirect distributions only if the ADN program ceases to be offered, which is not the case [documents/program-status.md; documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
  * **Foundation Endowment Policy:** Policy § 3.2 specifies that "Each distribution may be spent only for the purpose of the fund it comes from," and Policy § 4.1 states that donor-restricted funds, their returns, and distributions must be used only as the gift instrument directs [documents/endowment-policy.md].
  * **UPMIFA Requirements:** Under UPMIFA § 4(a), any appropriated return carries the fund's purpose restriction [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Court modification under § 6(c) is unavailable because the purpose is neither unlawful, impracticable, impossible, nor wasteful, and counsel notes that an institutional budget shortfall does not make a purpose impracticable or impossible [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Modification under § 6(d) is unavailable because the fund exceeds $25,000 and is under 20 years old [documents/fund-statements.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Even if donor consent were sought under § 6(a), the Foundation's policy maintains the fund's designated purpose [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].
* **FY2027 Annual Spending Distribution:** 
  * Based on Policy § 3.1 (4% of the 12 quarter-end average market values preceding July 1, 2026: 2023-09-30 through 2026-06-30, totaling $5,930,200 with an average of $494,183.33) [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/fund-statements.md], the distribution is **$19,767.33**. This entire amount is legally restricted to ADN scholarships and cannot be used for general operations [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].

#### B. Proposed 18-Month Loan from the Ruiz Endowment (Joan's Proposal)
* **What we cannot do:** The Foundation cannot borrow $180,000 (or any amount) from the Ruiz fund to support operations [documents/endowment-policy.md].
* **Why:** Foundation Endowment Policy § 4.2 strictly and unambiguously prohibits this:  
  > *"Donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund, whatever the term or rate of the loan."* [documents/endowment-policy.md]  
  Regardless of repayment terms or interest rates, executing an internal loan from this fund would be a direct violation of Foundation policy [documents/endowment-policy.md].

### 3. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund

* **Fund Profile:** Established May 26, 1994, by resolution of the Board following a $5,000 gift from Margaret E. Calloway [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]. Market value as of August 31, 2026: **$18,420** [documents/fund-statements.md].
* **Restriction:** Earnings to provide an annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the College's Secretarial Studies certificate program [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].
* **Current Program and Donor Status:** The Secretarial Studies certificate was officially discontinued and closed by the Board of Trustees on June 30, 2011, and no student can enroll or graduate [documents/program-status.md]. No award has been made since 2011, and earnings have accumulated in the fund [documents/fund-statements.md]. The donor, Mrs. Calloway, died on February 9, 2002, leaving no successor or advisor [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].

#### A. Folding into General Operations
* **What we cannot do:** We cannot fold the Calloway fund into general operating funds [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
* **Why:** 
  * Because Mrs. Calloway is deceased, donor consent under UPMIFA § 6(a) is impossible [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].
  * Although the fund qualifies as a "small, old fund" under UPMIFA § 6(d) (total value of $18,420 is under $25,000; established 32 years ago in 1994, well over the 20-year threshold; and the purpose is impossible to achieve due to program closure) [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md; documents/fund-statements.md; documents/program-status.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md], **condition (3) of UPMIFA § 6(d) mandates that:**
    > *"the institution uses the property in a manner consistent with the charitable purposes expressed in the gift instrument."* [documents/upmifa-summary.md]
  * Transferring the fund to general operations does not meet the requirement of consistency with Mrs. Calloway’s intent to provide student awards for administrative office careers [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].

#### B. Permissible Route, Steps, and Timeline
* **Permissible Modification:** Modify the fund’s purpose under UPMIFA § 6(d) to provide an annual award to an outstanding graduating student in the College's **Office Administration (A.A.S.) program**, which was established in Fall 2012 as the successor program preparing students for administrative and office careers (currently active with 47 students enrolled for Fall 2026) [documents/program-status.md].
* **Steps and Timeline:**
  1. **Notification to the Attorney General (October 5, 2026):** As permitted by Policy § 4.3, President Oyelaran may authorize outside counsel to issue the required statutory notice to the Attorney General on Monday, October 5, 2026 [documents/ed-email.md; documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].
  2. **Mandatory 60-Day Waiting Period:** UPMIFA § 6(d) mandates a 60-day notification period prior to release or modification [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The 60-day window following October 5, 2026, expires on **Friday, December 4, 2026**.
  3. **Board Resolution (December 2026 or later):** Under Policy § 4.3, the restriction can be released or modified only by resolution of the Board [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Because the 60-day notice period will not have run by the October 21, 2026 board meeting, the Board cannot act on October 21 [documents/ed-email.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The Board can adopt the resolution modifying the purpose to Office Administration at its next meeting after December 4, 2026 [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md].

### 4. Recommended Solution: Board-Designated Operating Reserve

To eliminate the $180,000 operating deficit without legal barriers or delays, the Foundation should utilize its **Operating Reserve** [documents/ed-email.md; documents/fund-statements.md]:

* **Fund Profile:** Established June 18, 2019, under Board Resolution 2019-07 [documents/fund-statements.md]. Market value as of August 31, 2026: **$240,000** [documents/fund-statements.md].
* **Legal and Policy Status:** 
  * The fund is **unrestricted** and categorized as **Board-designated** [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/fund-statements.md].
  * UPMIFA § 6 does not apply to board-designated funds [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
  * Endowment Policy § 5.1 provides: *"A board-designated fund is unrestricted. The Board may change or remove its designation, and use the fund for any purpose of the Foundation, by majority vote of the full Board. No donor consent and no notice to the Attorney General is required."* [documents/endowment-policy.md]
* **Deficit Coverage:**
  * Transferring $180,000 from the Operating Reserve to general operations eliminates the $180,000 deficit in its entirety [documents/ed-email.md; documents/fund-statements.md].
  * Following this release, **$60,000** will remain in the Operating Reserve ($240,000 minus $180,000) [documents/fund-statements.md].
  * Prior to any action, the general operating fund projected a ($41,300) balance at June 30, 2027 [documents/fund-statements.md]; this allocation stabilizes the Foundation's operating position through FY2027 [documents/ed-email.md; documents/fund-statements.md].
* **Steps and Timeline:**
  * Prepare a resolution for the Board of Directors' regular meeting on **Wednesday, October 21, 2026** [documents/ed-email.md].
  * Approval requires a **majority vote of the full Board** [documents/endowment-policy.md § 5.1]. Funds are available immediately upon passage [documents/endowment-policy.md].

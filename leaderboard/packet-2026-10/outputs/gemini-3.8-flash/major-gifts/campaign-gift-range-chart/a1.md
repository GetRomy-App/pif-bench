## 1. Gift Range Chart

*Notes on chart construction:*
- **Tier structure:** Lead gift equals 20% of the $10,000,000 goal ($2,000,000, 1 gift); each lower tier halves the gift amount and doubles the number of gifts; tiers terminate below $250,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- **All other gifts:** Closes the chart with the balance of $2,000,000 ($10,000,000 goal less $8,000,000 named tier cumulative total) for "many" gifts [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- **Gifts in hand:** Signed pledge agreements count at the highest tier their amount meets or exceeds [documents/counsel-chart-method.md, documents/campaign-commitments.md].
- **Prospect ratio:** 3 prospects per gift needed for tiers of $1,000,000 or more; 4 prospects per gift needed for tiers below $1,000,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- **Prospect placement:** Placed at the highest named tier at or below the bottom of the prospect's five-year capacity rating band; donors with gifts in hand and prospects with inclination code N are excluded [documents/counsel-chart-method.md, documents/rating-key.md, documents/prospect-pipeline.csv].

### 1. Ask Amount and Sizing

* **Ask Amount:** **$80,000** (single figure, not a range) [documents/solicitation-policy.md]
* **Sizing Methodology:**
  1. *Five-Year Capacity Estimate:* $382,000 (Capacity Rating C: $250,000–$499,999) from the research profile dated 2026-06-14 [documents/research-profile.md].
  2. *Inclination Score and Factor:* Inclination score is 4 (strong) [documents/research-profile.md], which corresponds to an inclination factor of 0.25 [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
  3. *Uncapped Calculation:* $382,000 × 0.25 = $95,500 [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
  4. *Initial Rounding:* Rounded to the nearest $5,000 = $95,000 [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
  5. *Cap Assessment:* Under Section 1.4, the ask may not exceed four times the household's largest single gift to the Foundation in the five years preceding the plan date (2021-09-28 to 2026-09-28) [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
     * *Household giving history within five years* [documents/crm-record.md]:
       * 2021-11-18: $5,000 (personal check; hard credit)
       * 2022-05-12: $7,500 (personal check; hard credit)
       * 2024-06-03: $12,500 (personal check; hard credit)
       * 2025-10-14: $20,000 (Vandermolen Family Fund at the Puget Lowlands Community Foundation; donor-advised fund recommended by Celeste; soft credit)
     * *Excluded non-household gifts under Section 1.5* [documents/solicitation-policy.md]:
       * 2023-04-22: $25,000 Spring Gala presenting sponsorship paid by Vandermolen Marine Supply, Inc. (corporate sponsorship, excluded regardless of soft credit) [documents/crm-record.md], [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
       * 2026-04-18: $2,500 Spring Gala table purchase (ticket purchases excluded) [documents/crm-record.md], [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
     * *Largest single household gift:* $20,000 (2025-10-14 DAF grant) [documents/crm-record.md].
     * *Cap calculation:* 4 × $20,000 = $80,000 [documents/solicitation-policy.md].
  6. *Final Determination:* Because the initial rounded calculation of $95,000 exceeds the cap of $80,000, Section 1.4 dictates that the ask is capped at $80,000 (rounded down to the nearest $5,000) [documents/solicitation-policy.md].

### 1. Executive Summary

In response to your request ahead of the October 13 board meeting [documents/treasurer-email.md], here is the straight answer regarding our public support test for the 2025 Form 990:

* **Do we pass the 33⅓% public support test?**  
  **Yes.** The Council qualifies as a publicly supported organization under Section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) via the 33⅓% support test on Schedule A, Part II, Line 16a [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
* **What is our public support percentage?**  
  **34.33%** (Line 14) [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
* **How close is it?**  
  It is very close. We clear the 33.33% (33⅓%) threshold by **1.00 percentage point** (0.997 percentage points unrounded). In dollar terms, based on our five-year total support of $3,671,600, the 33⅓% threshold requires $1,223,866.67 in allowable public support. Our actual allowable public support is $1,260,596, leaving a margin of only **$36,729.33**.

Because the Council passes Line 16a, it does not need to rely on the 10% facts-and-circumstances test on Line 17a [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

#### Section C. Computation of Public Support Percentage

* **2% Threshold Amount:**  
  $$\text{Line 11(f)} \times 2\% = \$3,671,600 \times 0.02 = \mathbf{\$73,432.00}$$ [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
* **Line 14 — Public Support Percentage:**  
  $$\frac{\text{Line 6(f)}}{\text{Line 11(f)}} = \frac{\$1,260,596}{\$3,671,600} = 34.3337\% \longrightarrow \mathbf{34.33\%}$$ [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
* **Line 16a — 33⅓% Support Test:**  
  Because Line 14 is **34.33%** (which is $\ge 33\frac{1}{3}\%$, or $33.33\%$), the box on Line 16a is checked, and the Council qualifies as a publicly supported organization [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

### 4. Key Factors for the Board and Preparer

1. **Marguerite Ostrander’s Concentration:**  
   Marguerite’s cumulative gifts of $2,025,000 represent 55.15% of our five-year total support [documents/donors-10k-plus-2021-2025.csv, documents/revenue-by-source-2021-2025.csv]. Because her allowable support is capped at $73,432, $1,951,568 is excluded from public support [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
2. **Crucial Role of DEQ and Upper Alder Community Foundation:**  
   Because Oregon DEQ ($160,000) is a governmental unit and Upper Alder Community Foundation ($135,000) is a Section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) public charity awarding competitive grants, neither is subject to the 2% ceiling [documents/donors-10k-plus-2021-2025.csv, documents/finance-manager-notes.md, documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md]. Together, they contribute $295,000 in un-capped public support, which provides the cushion that keeps the Council above 33⅓%.
3. **Exclusion of Capital Gain:**  
   Excluding the $182,000 realized gain on the stock sale from Line 10 (per IRS instructions) is critical [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md, documents/revenue-by-source-2021-2025.csv]. If that gain had been included in total support (denominator), our percentage would have dropped to 32.71%, failing the one-third test.
4. **Treatment of Kettle County Contract:**  
   Classifying the Kettle County contract ($256,000 total) as program service revenue on Line 12 rather than Line 1 or Line 10 keeps it outside total support [documents/finance-manager-notes.md, documents/revenue-by-source-2021-2025.csv, documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

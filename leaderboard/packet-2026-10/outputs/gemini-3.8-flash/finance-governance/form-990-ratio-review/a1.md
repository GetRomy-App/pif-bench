### 1. Purpose and Overview

In accordance with Section 4.1 of the Ashby Creek Regional Food Bank Financial Health Indicators Policy [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md], this memorandum presents the annual calculation of the Food Bank’s three financial health indicators for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. 

The calculations are prepared following the issuance of the independent auditor’s report by Marrow Bennett CPAs on April 22, 2026 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md] and the electronic filing of Form 990 on August 11, 2026 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. All calculations adhere strictly to the definitions, sources, gift-in-kind exclusions, and rounding conventions established in the board’s policy [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Sections 2 and 3].

### Summary of Results

| Indicator | FY 2025 Result | Board Target | Target Met? |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **1. Program Expense Ratio** | **78.3%** | 80.0% or higher | **No** |
| **2. Cost to Raise a Dollar** | **$0.22** | $0.20 or less | **No** |
| **3. Months of Operating Reserve** | **3.1 months** | 3.0 months or more | **Yes** |

### 2. Indicator Calculations and Source Documentation

#### 2.1 Program Expense Ratio

* **Policy Definition:** `(Program service expenses − gifts in kind expensed in program services) ÷ (Total functional expenses − all gifts in kind expensed)` [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.1]
* **Target:** 80.0% or higher [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.1]

##### Inputs and Sources:
1. **Program service expenses:** $19,066,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part IX, line 25, column (B) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
2. **Gifts in kind expensed in program services:** $14,180,000  
   *Source:* Donated food distributed during 2025, reported in Form 990, Part IX, line 24a, column (B) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] and audited financial statement Note 9 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
3. **Net program service expenses (Numerator):**  
   $$\$19,066,000 - \$14,180,000 = \$4,886,000$$
4. **Total functional expenses:** $20,515,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part IX, line 25, column (A) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
5. **All gifts in kind expensed:** $14,275,000  
   *Source:* Audit Note 9 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md] identifies total recognized nonfinancial contributions of $14,345,000 ($14,250,000 donated food plus $95,000 donated legal services). Of the donated food, $14,180,000 was expensed as distributions (Part IX, line 24a) while the remaining $70,000 was added to inventory. The $95,000 in donated legal services was fully expensed in management and general legal fees (Part IX, line 11b, column (C)). Total gifts in kind expensed in 2025:  
   $$\$14,180,000 + \$95,000 = \$14,275,000$$
6. **Net total functional expenses (Denominator):**  
   $$\$20,515,000 - \$14,275,000 = \$6,240,000$$

##### Calculation:
$$\frac{\$4,886,000}{\$6,240,000} = 0.78301... \rightarrow \mathbf{78.3\%}$$

* **Target Comparison:** 78.3% vs. target of 80.0% or higher.
* **Finding:** **Target Not Met.**

#### 2.2 Cost to Raise a Dollar

* **Policy Definition:** `Fundraising expenses ÷ Contributions` [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.2]
  * *Fundraising expenses:* Form 990 Part IX, line 25, column (D), less any gifts in kind expensed in fundraising [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.2].
  * *Contributions:* Form 990 Part VIII, line 1h, less line 1g (noncash contributions), less every bequest and decedent estate distribution [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.2].
* **Target:** $0.20 or less [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.2]

##### Inputs and Sources:
1. **Total fundraising expenses:** $866,300  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part IX, line 25, column (D) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
2. **Gifts in kind expensed in fundraising:** $0  
   *Source:* Audit Note 9 confirms that contributed nonfinancial assets were expensed exclusively in program services ($14,180,000) and management and general ($95,000); no nonfinancial assets were expensed in fundraising [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
3. **Net fundraising expenses (Numerator):**  
   $$\$866,300 - \$0 = \$866,300$$
4. **Total contributions and grants:** $18,930,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part VIII, line 1h [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
5. **Noncash contributions:** $14,345,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part VIII, line 1g [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] and Audit Note 9 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
6. **Bequests and estate distributions:** $600,000  
   *Source:* Audit Note 10 discloses a $600,000 distribution received in August 2025 from the Estate of Harriet M. Okafor (included in Form 990, Part VIII, line 1f), with no other estate distributions received in 2025 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
7. **Adjusted cash contributions (Denominator):**  
   $$\$18,930,000 - \$14,345,000 - \$600,000 = \$3,985,000$$

##### Calculation:
$$\frac{\$866,300}{\$3,985,000} = \$0.21739... \rightarrow \mathbf{\$0.22}$$

* **Target Comparison:** $0.22 vs. target of $0.20 or less.
* **Finding:** **Target Not Met.**
* **Policy Threshold Notes:** 
  * While the cost to raise a dollar exceeded the $0.20 target, it did not exceed the $0.25 threshold that mandates an external review of fundraising costs [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.2].
  * *Separate Reporting of Bequests:* As required by Section 3.2 of the policy, the CFO separately reports that the Food Bank received $600,000 from the Estate of Harriet M. Okafor in August 2025, which carried no donor restrictions and remains undesignated by the board [documents/audit-notes-2025.md, Note 10; documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.2].

#### 2.3 Months of Operating Reserve

* **Policy Definition:** `Operating reserve ÷ (Annual operating expenses ÷ 12)` [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.3]
  * *Operating reserve:* Net assets without donor restrictions (Part X, line 27) less land, buildings, and equipment, net (Part X, line 10c) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.3].
  * *Annual operating expenses:* Total functional expenses (Part IX, line 25, column (A)) less depreciation (Part IX, line 22, column (A)) less all gifts in kind expensed [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.3].
* **Target:** 3.0 months or more [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.3]

##### Inputs and Sources:
1. **Net assets without donor restrictions (end of year):** $6,612,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part X, line 27, column (B) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
2. **Land, buildings, and equipment, net (end of year):** $5,108,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part X, line 10c, column (B) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] and Audit Note 6 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
3. **Operating reserve (Numerator):**  
   $$\$6,612,000 - \$5,108,000 = \$1,504,000$$
4. **Total functional expenses:** $20,515,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part IX, line 25, column (A) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
5. **Depreciation expense:** $446,000  
   *Source:* Form 990, Part IX, line 22, column (A) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] and Audit Note 6 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
6. **All gifts in kind expensed:** $14,275,000  
   *Source:* Sum of $14,180,000 donated food distributed and $95,000 donated legal services [documents/audit-notes-2025.md, Note 9].
7. **Annual operating expenses:**  
   $$\$20,515,000 - \$446,000 - \$14,275,000 = \$5,794,000$$
8. **Average monthly operating expenses (Denominator):**  
   $$\frac{\$5,794,000}{12} = \$482,833.33...$$

##### Calculation:
$$\frac{\$1,504,000}{\$482,833.33...} = 3.1149... \rightarrow \mathbf{3.1\text{ months}}$$

* **Target Comparison:** 3.1 months vs. target of 3.0 months or more.
* **Finding:** **Target Met.**
* **Policy Threshold Notes:** The operating reserve is above the target and well above the 1.5-month threshold requiring emergency board notification by the treasurer [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 3.3].

### 3. Policy Requirements and Next Steps

1. **Corrective Action Plans (Policy Section 4.2):**  
   Because the **Program Expense Ratio (78.3%)** and the **Cost to Raise a Dollar ($0.22)** did not meet their respective board-established targets (80.0% and $0.20), management is required to formulate and present corrective action plans for both indicators to the Finance Committee within 90 days of this report [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 4.2].
2. **External Reporting Limitation (Policy Section 4.3):**  
   The committee and staff are reminded that these indicators may only be published externally (including in annual reports, on the website, or within grant applications) using the exact policy-defined calculations set forth above [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md, Section 4.3].

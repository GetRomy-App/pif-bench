### 1. Executive Summary

In preparation for the Governance Committee’s meeting on September 3, 2026 [documents/controller-email.md], this memorandum provides a formal review of the vendor contracts awarded for the May 2, 2026 Spring Gala [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. This review evaluates the issues raised by Controller Wesley Tran regarding two gala vendors [documents/controller-email.md] against the Crescent Harbor Children’s Theatre Conflict of Interest Policy [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md] and IRS Form 990 Schedule L reporting guidelines [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

The findings of this review are:
1. **Audio-Visual Contract (BrightStage AV Services LLC):** Involved a disclosed Financial Interest through Treasurer Priya Ramaswamy's brother, Arun Ramaswamy [documents/annual-statements-2026.md], [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. The Conflict of Interest Policy was fully satisfied in all respects [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md], [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. Furthermore, because total FY2026 payments of $18,250 do not exceed the IRS reporting threshold of $39,400 (1% of the Theatre's $3,940,000 total revenue), BrightStage does not require reporting on Schedule L, Part IV of the 2025 Form 990 [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
2. **Catering Contract (Saffron & Sage Events LLC):** Involved an undisclosed Financial Interest and conflict of interest through Director Thomas Aldana, whose spouse, Celeste M. Aldana, is the 100% sole owner and manager of the entity [documents/annual-statements-2026.md], [documents/state-business-registry.md]. Multiple serious policy violations occurred: Director Aldana failed to disclose this interest on his annual statement [documents/annual-statements-2026.md], failed to disclose it prior to or during the February 12, 2026 board meeting [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md], actively advocated for the contract, moved to approve it, and voted in favor of the $68,400 award to his spouse’s company in a narrow 6–5 vote [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
3. **Form 990 Schedule L Reporting:** Total FY2026 payments to Saffron & Sage Events LLC were $71,860, exceeding the $39,400 transaction threshold [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]. This transaction must be disclosed on Schedule L, Part IV of the 2025 Form 990 [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
4. **Mandated Next Steps:** Under Article IV of the Conflict of Interest Policy, Director Aldana must immediately be recused from Governance Committee proceedings [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. The committee must issue written notice giving him 15 days to explain [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md], report findings to the board for disciplinary action [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md], require a corrected annual statement within 30 days [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md], and initiate a board re-review of the transaction by disinterested directors, including consulting legal counsel regarding possible excess benefit transactions under Internal Revenue Code (IRC) section 4958 [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

### 2. Gala Vendor Conflict Analysis

The Theatre issued requests for proposals on December 1, 2025, for gala catering (six caterers invited) and audio-visual production (four companies invited), with bids due January 9, 2026 [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].

#### A. Audio-Visual Production: BrightStage AV Services LLC
* **Vendor Ownership:** BrightStage AV Services LLC is an active domestic LLC whose managing member, Arun Ramaswamy, owns 80% (Lena Ortiz owns 20%) [documents/state-business-registry.md].
* **Relationship to Theatre:** Arun Ramaswamy is the brother of Director and Treasurer Priya Ramaswamy [documents/annual-statements-2026.md], [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md].
* **Policy Application:** Under Article II, Section 3 of the Conflict of Interest Policy, brothers are defined as Family Members [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. Under Article II, Section 2(c), a person has a Financial Interest if they have, directly or indirectly through a Family Member, a potential compensation arrangement or ownership/investment interest in an entity negotiating or submitting a bid [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. Director Ramaswamy is an "Interested Person" under Article II, Section 1 [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
* **Board Determination:** At the February 12, 2026 board meeting, the remaining disinterested directors formally determined that Director Ramaswamy's interest constituted a conflict of interest under the policy [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].

#### B. Catering: Saffron & Sage Events LLC
* **Vendor Ownership:** Saffron & Sage Events LLC is an active domestic LLC whose sole member and manager (100%) is Celeste M. Aldana, with its principal office at 88 Orchard Terrace, Crescent Harbor [documents/state-business-registry.md]. The vendor W-9 confirms Celeste M. Aldana is the single member and signs as Managing Member [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].
* **Relationship to Theatre:** Thomas Aldana is a current Director of the Theatre, residing at 88 Orchard Terrace, Crescent Harbor [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md]. In Question 3 of his January 20, 2026 conflict-of-interest statement, Director Aldana identified his spouse as Celeste Aldana [documents/annual-statements-2026.md].
* **Policy Application:** Under Article II, Section 3, a spouse is a Family Member [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. Under Article II, Section 2, Director Aldana had a direct and indirect Financial Interest in Saffron & Sage Events LLC through his spouse's 100% ownership and compensation arrangement [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md], [documents/state-business-registry.md]. Saffron & Sage submitted a bid on January 8, 2026 [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md]. Therefore, Director Aldana was an Interested Person under Article II, Section 1 [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
* **Board Determination:** The board was prevented from making a conflict-of-interest determination at the February 12, 2026 meeting because Director Aldana concealed the Financial Interest [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. Under Article II, Section 2 and Article IV, Section 1, an actual conflict existed that required formal disclosure and board determination [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

### 4. Form 990, Schedule L (Part IV) Reporting Analysis

The Theatre reports on a fiscal year ending June 30; transactions for the fiscal year July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026 are reportable on the 2025 Form 990, due November 16, 2026 [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

#### A. Threshold Rules for Business Transactions with Interested Persons
Under IRS Schedule L, Part IV instructions [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]:
1. **Interested Persons:** Include current directors, family members (including spouses and brothers), and entities more than 35% owned by them [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
   * BrightStage AV Services LLC is an interested person entity because Arun Ramaswamy (brother of Director Priya Ramaswamy) owns 80% (exceeding 35%) [documents/state-business-registry.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
   * Saffron & Sage Events LLC is an interested person entity because Celeste M. Aldana (spouse of Director Thomas Aldana) owns 100% (exceeding 35%) [documents/state-business-registry.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
2. **Monetary Thresholds:** A transaction is reportable if:
   * *(Threshold a)* All payments during the tax year between the organization and the interested person exceeded $100,000 [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]; OR
   * *(Threshold b)* All payments during the tax year from a single transaction exceeded the **greater of $10,000 or 1% of the organization’s total revenue** (Form 990, Part VIII, line 12) [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
3. **Calculation of 1% Total Revenue Threshold:**
   * Total revenue on the 2025 draft Form 990 (trial balance line 12) is **$3,940,000** [documents/controller-email.md].
   * 1% of $3,940,000 = **$39,400** [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
   * The greater of $10,000 or $39,400 is **$39,400** [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

#### B. Vendor Testing Against Thresholds
* **BrightStage AV Services LLC:**
  * FY2026 payments totaled **$18,250** ($9,125 deposit on 2026-02-24 and $9,125 balance on 2026-05-08) [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md].
  * $18,250 does not exceed the $100,000 threshold or the $39,400 threshold [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
  * **Result:** BrightStage is **not reportable** on Schedule L, Part IV [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
* **Saffron & Sage Events LLC:**
  * FY2026 payments totaled **$71,860** ($20,520 deposit on 2026-02-20 and $51,340 balance on 2026-05-12, covering the $68,400 contract, $2,160 for 12 additional guests, and a $1,300 bar upgrade) [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md].
  * Payments exceed the $39,400 threshold [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].
  * **Result:** Saffron & Sage is **reportable** on Schedule L, Part IV [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

The Controller must provide the following transaction details for Schedule L, Part IV [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]:

### 5. Mandatory Actions Required Under Policy (Article IV)

Because Director Aldana failed to disclose an actual or possible conflict of interest, Article IV of the Conflict of Interest Policy governs the committee's and the board's required actions [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]:

1. **Immediate Recusal from the Governance Committee (Article IV, Section 4):**
   * Director Aldana is currently a member of the Governance Committee [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md].
   * The policy requires: *"The director whose conduct is under review takes no part in the Governance Committee's or the board's deliberations or votes under this Article, including as a member of the Governance Committee"* [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
   * Committee Chair Pettigrew and member Naomi Sato must handle this matter without Director Aldana’s participation or presence [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md], [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

2. **Notice and Opportunity to Explain (Article IV, Section 1):**
   * The Governance Committee must immediately inform Director Aldana in writing of the basis for its belief that he failed to disclose a conflict of interest [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
   * The notice must give Director Aldana an opportunity to explain the failure to disclose, in writing or at a meeting of the committee, within **15 days of the notice** [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

3. **Investigation and Findings Report to the Board (Article IV, Section 2):**
   * Following the 15-day response period and any further investigation, the Governance Committee must report its findings to the full Board of Directors [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

4. **Board Corrective and Disciplinary Action (Article IV, Section 2):**
   * Acting by a majority vote of disinterested directors, the board must determine whether Director Aldana failed to disclose a conflict of interest [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
   * Upon such a determination, the board must impose appropriate disciplinary action, which under the policy may include:
     * A written reprimand [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md];
     * Removal from committee positions (e.g., Governance Committee) [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md], [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md];
     * A request for resignation [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]; or
     * Removal under Bylaws Article IV, Section 7 [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

5. **Formal Re-Review of the Catering Contract (Article IV, Section 3):**
   * The policy mandates that any transaction approved without the required disclosure and recusal must be re-reviewed by the disinterested directors under Article III, Sections 3(c) and 3(d) [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
   * The disinterested directors must determine whether the Theatre could have obtained a more advantageous transaction with reasonable efforts (such as the Gala Committee's recommended proposal from Harborview Provisions at $52,900, which was $15,500 less than the contract award) [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md], [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md], [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].

6. **Legal Consultation Regarding Excess Benefit Transactions (Article IV, Section 3):**
   * If the re-review determines that the Theatre paid more than fair market value, the board must consult legal counsel regarding:
     * Potential liability for an excess benefit transaction under IRC section 4958 [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]; and
     * Remedies for recovering the excess payment from the vendor/interested person [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

7. **Corrected Annual Statement (Article IV, Section 5):**
   * Director Aldana must file a corrected annual conflict-of-interest statement within **30 days** of the board's determination [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

8. **Filing Notification to Form 990 Preparer (Article VII):**
   * The Controller and Governance Committee must ensure that the interested-person transaction with Saffron & Sage Events LLC ($71,860) is supplied to Pryce & Elwood CPAs for inclusion in Schedule L, Part IV of the 2025 Form 990 prior to the November 16, 2026 filing deadline [documents/controller-email.md], [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md], [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

## Ask Amount Determination (Harvest Gala 2026)

Section 5 dictates that for unrated prospects, every personal ask (including renewals, appeals, gala paddle-raise asks, and event-sponsorship asks) is calculated as **1.5 × the donor's last gift, rounded up to the next multiple of $250** [documents/research-standards.md].

### "Last Gift" Analysis
Under Section 5, "last gift" is defined strictly as the donor's most recent outright gift (cash, check, card, or stock) hard-credited to the donor [documents/research-standards.md]. Purchases, event tickets, matching gifts, and soft credits (including soft credits from a business the donor owns) are specifically excluded [documents/research-standards.md]:
* **2026-07-02 ($2,000):** Soft credit for Summer Concert Series sponsorship via Hartwell Bookkeeping & Tax, LLC — *Excluded per Section 5 (soft credit / business entity gift)* [documents/crm-record.md, documents/research-standards.md].
* **2026-05-14 ($150):** Spring Recital reception tickets — *Excluded per Section 5 (event purchase)* [documents/crm-record.md, documents/research-standards.md].
* **2026-04-03 ($750):** Spring Appeal check — *Hard-credit outright gift* [documents/crm-record.md].

The qualifying "last gift" is **$750** [documents/crm-record.md].

### Calculation
$$\$750 \times 1.5 = \$1,125$$
Rounding $\$1,125$ up to the next multiple of $\$250$ yields **$1,250** [documents/research-standards.md].

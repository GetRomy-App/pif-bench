## 1. Summary

Marcus D. Ellery is the co-founder and President of Ellery Mechanical Contractors, LLC, a Winston-Salem-based commercial HVAC and plumbing contractor with 140 employees and approximately $38 million in 2024 revenue [documents/research-findings.md]. He also serves as the sole member and manager of EMC Properties, LLC, a commercial real estate holding company [documents/research-findings.md]. 

Mr. Ellery and his spouse, Dr. Renée L. Ellery, have been connected to Salem Creek Youth Theatre (SCYT) since 2019 [documents/crm-record.md]. They have contributed annually to SCYT since 2021, increasing their annual fund support from $1,000 to $5,000, supplemented by a $2,500 corporate season sponsorship from Ellery Mechanical Contractors [documents/crm-record.md]. The household has an active programmatic connection to SCYT, with a minor child enrolled in the Summer Intensive [documents/crm-record.md, documents/research-findings.md]. In November 2025, Mr. Ellery attended SCYT's Fall Gala and specifically inquired about plans for the rehearsal hall [documents/crm-record.md].

Beyond SCYT, Mr. and Dr. Ellery are active civic donors and leaders in the Piedmont Triad region, holding board trusteeships with the Yadkin Valley Community College Foundation and Twin City Housing Partners [documents/research-findings.md]. 

Based on SCYT's capacity methodology, Mr. Ellery's five-year giving capacity is calculated at **$312,750**, placing him in **Rating B ($250,000 – $499,999)** [documents/research-ethics-privacy-standard.md].

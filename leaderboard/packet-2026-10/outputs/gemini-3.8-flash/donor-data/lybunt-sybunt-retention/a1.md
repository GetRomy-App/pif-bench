## 2. Methodology & Policy Governance

All metrics are governed by the Development Operations standards [documents/metrics-definitions.md]:

### Fiscal Year Calendar (§1)
* Lantern Hill Arts Center operates on a fiscal year running **July 1 through June 30**, named for the calendar year in which it closes [documents/metrics-definitions.md]:
  * **FY2021:** July 1, 2020 – June 30, 2021
  * **FY2022:** July 1, 2021 – June 30, 2022
  * **FY2023:** July 1, 2022 – June 30, 2023
  * **FY2024:** July 1, 2023 – June 30, 2024
  * **FY2025:** July 1, 2024 – June 30, 2025
  * **FY2026 ("Last Year"):** July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026 (most recent complete fiscal year)
  * **FY2027 ("This Year to Date"):** July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027 (current reporting period through September 15, 2026)

### Unit of Measure & Exclusions (§2 & §3)
* **Household Level:** Donors are tracked and counted by `household_id`, not individual constituent records. Constituents sharing a household ID count as a single donor unit [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* **Countable Gift Definition:** A hard-credit gift to an individual constituent with a status of `Posted` [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* **Organizational Exclusion:** All gifts from companies, foundations, and clubs (e.g., Northgate Systems, Thistlewood Family Foundation, Sourwood Garden Club, Ferncliff Brewing Co.) are excluded from counts and rates regardless of amount [documents/gift-history.csv, documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* **Soft Credit Exclusion:** Soft credits (such as matching gift employee credits, peer-to-peer birthday fundraiser credits, or spousal soft credits) do not represent the constituent's own direct gift and are excluded from countable history [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* **Refunded Gifts Exclusion:** Gifts with status `Refunded` do not count in any fiscal year, regardless of when the refund was processed [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* **Joint Accounts:** Gifts from joint accounts count once for the household, whether entered under one spouse with a soft credit to the other or split between spousal records [documents/metrics-definitions.md].

## 4. SYBUNT Report

### Definition (§4)
A **SYBUNT** ("some year but unfortunately not this year") is defined as a household with at least one countable gift in the five fiscal years **FY2021 through FY2025** (July 1, 2020 – June 30, 2025), **none in FY2026**, and **none in FY2027 to date** [documents/metrics-definitions.md]. 
* A household whose last countable gift is dated prior to FY2021 (before July 1, 2020) is defined as **lapsed** and is excluded from SYBUNT [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* Households with gifts in FY2026 are evaluated under LYBUNT; no household can be both a LYBUNT and a SYBUNT [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* Households that have already given in FY2027 are current donors and are excluded from SYBUNT [documents/metrics-definitions.md].

## 5. Donor Retention Rate Report

### Definition & Policy (§4)
* The **donor retention rate** is calculated for the most recent complete fiscal year (**FY2026**) [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Donor Retention Rate} = \frac{\text{Number of households with a countable gift in both FY2025 and FY2026}}{\text{Number of households with a countable gift in FY2025}} \times 100$$
* **Current Fiscal Year Exclusion:** FY2027 is not used for retention calculations until it closes on June 30, 2027 [documents/metrics-definitions.md].

### Retention Rate Calculation
$$\text{Retention Rate} = \frac{15}{22} = 68.1818\dots\% \longrightarrow \mathbf{68.2\%}$$

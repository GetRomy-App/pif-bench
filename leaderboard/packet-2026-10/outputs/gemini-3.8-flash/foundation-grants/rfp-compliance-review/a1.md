### Executive Summary

A compliance review of Draft v3 of the Sierra Ladrones Community Foundation (SLCF) proposal against the Foundation’s Request for Proposals (RFP) identified six critical compliance defects [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Under the Foundation's RFP terms, five of these issues would cause the application to be returned without review or automatically locked out:

1. **Submission Time Zone Error:** The planned submission time of 4:30 p.m. Phoenix time on October 1 is 5:30 p.m. Mountain Daylight Time (MDT)—30 minutes after the portal closes [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md, documents/summit-itinerary.md].
2. **Request Amount Exceeds RFP Maximum:** The current request of $83,847 exceeds the hard RFP cap of $75,000 [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
3. **Indirect Cost Rate Exceeds Cap:** The draft applies a 15% indirect cost rate ($10,937), violating the RFP ceiling of 10% of total direct costs [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
4. **Ineligible Match and Match Deficit:** Of the $51,000 match claimed, $17,000 is non-compliant ($12,000 in-kind supplies and $5,000 uncommitted Rotary funds) [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/draft-proposal-v3.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Only $34,000 in cash match is documented by signed letters in Attachment E [documents/attachments-folder.md]. Because the RFP requires a 50% cash match ($1 for every $2 requested), the current documented match caps the maximum allowable request at $68,000 [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
5. **Missing Narrative Section and Word Count Overage:** The draft completely omits required Section 5 ("Sustainability Plan"), and Sections 1–4 already total 2,655 words—155 words over the 2,500-word maximum for the entire narrative [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
6. **Attachment Misplacement:** In-kind documentation is included in Attachment E, which is restricted to cash match commitment letters [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].

Below is an analysis of each defect, the mandatory fix, and the corrected figures required for submission.

### Issue 1: Fatal Submission Deadline and Time Zone Discrepancy

* **RFP Requirement:** Applications must be submitted via the online portal by **5:00 p.m. Mountain Daylight Time (MDT, UTC−6) on Thursday, October 1, 2026** [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. The portal closes automatically at the deadline and rejects late submissions; technical support closes at 4:00 p.m. MDT [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Draft Status:** Lucia Montoya plans to upload the application from the Southwest Community Health Worker Summit hotel in Phoenix on Thursday, October 1, at 4:30 p.m. [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
* **Compliance Defect:** Arizona observes Mountain Standard Time (MST, UTC−7) year-round and does not observe daylight saving time [documents/summit-itinerary.md]. Consequently, Phoenix is one hour behind New Mexico (MDT):
  * 4:30 p.m. MST (Phoenix) = **5:30 p.m. MDT (New Mexico)**.
  * The SLCF portal closes at 5:00 p.m. MDT (4:00 p.m. MST) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * Technical support closes at 4:00 p.m. MDT (3:00 p.m. MST) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * Lucia's planned upload would occur 30 minutes after the portal has closed, resulting in complete exclusion [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Furthermore, Lucia is scheduled to present in a breakout session from 1:30 p.m. to 4:15 p.m. MST on October 1 [documents/summit-itinerary.md].
* **Mandatory Fix:** 
  * The application must be submitted no later than **3:00 p.m. MST (4:00 p.m. MDT)** on Thursday, October 1, to preserve access to technical support [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * Submission should take place during Lucia’s summit lunch break on October 1 (12:00 p.m.–1:30 p.m. MST / 1:00 p.m.–2:30 p.m. MDT) [documents/summit-itinerary.md], or the final package should be submitted from Albuquerque by development staff on Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

### Issue 2: Request Amount Exceeds the RFP Maximum

* **RFP Requirement:** "Requests may not exceed **$75,000**. Requests above $75,000 will not be considered" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Draft Status:** Draft v3 requests **$83,847** [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Lucia noted in her cover email that an SLCF program officer verbally indicated "flexibility for really strong proposals" during an August info session [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
* **Compliance Defect:** The written RFP governs the competition; it explicitly states that any request exceeding $75,000 will be disqualified without consideration [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Mandatory Fix:** The total grant request must be reduced to an amount not exceeding $75,000 (and capped further by documented cash match constraints, as detailed below) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].

### Issue 3: Indirect Cost Rate Exceeds Foundation Ceiling

* **RFP Requirement:** Indirect costs "may not exceed **10% of the total direct costs** in the request" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Draft Status:** Draft v3 requests $10,937 in indirect costs, calculated at 15% of direct costs ($72,910 direct × 15% = $10,937) based on Puente’s federal de minimis rate [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md, documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
* **Compliance Defect:** SLCF limits indirect costs to 10% of total direct costs; federal negotiated or de minimis rates do not supersede the foundation's specific RFP cap [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Charging 15% violates RFP Section 4 [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Mandatory Fix:** Re-budget indirect costs at a maximum of 10% of revised direct costs [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].

### Issue 4: Match Ineligibility and Resulting Request Cap

* **RFP Requirement:**
  1. Cash match must be at least **$1 for every $2 requested** (a minimum 50% match) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  2. Match must be cash committed to the project for calendar year 2027 [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  3. "In-kind contributions may be described in the budget narrative but do not count toward the match" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  4. Each match source must be documented by a signed commitment letter uploaded as Attachment E by the application deadline; match without a signed letter at deadline will not be counted [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Draft Status:** The draft lists $51,000 in total match (claimed as 61% of $83,847) across four sources [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]:
  1. *Valencia County Health Council:* $20,000 cash [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
  2. *Puente Health Partners Program Reserve:* $14,000 cash [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
  3. *Rio Grande Medical Supply:* $12,000 in-kind equipment donation [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
  4. *Socorro Rotary Club:* $5,000 cash ("Letter to follow") [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
* **Compliance Defect:**
  * **Rio Grande Medical Supply ($12,000):** This is an in-kind donation of 20 blood pressure monitors and 10 glucometers [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Under RFP Section 4, in-kind contributions cannot count toward the match [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * **Socorro Rotary Club ($5,000):** The Rotary Club board does not vote on grant allocations until October 14, 2026, two weeks after the October 1 submission deadline [documents/attachments-folder.md]. Ed Trujillo's email is an informal statement of intent, not an official signed commitment letter [documents/attachments-folder.md]. Because no signed letter can be provided by October 1, this $5,000 cannot be counted [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * **Allowable Cash Match:** Only two sources possess valid signed commitment letters in hand:
    * Valencia County Health Council: $20,000 (signed 2026-09-08, Attachment E-1) [documents/attachments-folder.md].
    * Puente Board Program Reserve: $14,000 (signed 2026-09-15, Attachment E-2) [documents/attachments-folder.md].
    * **Total Allowable Documented Cash Match: $34,000** [documents/attachments-folder.md].
* **Impact on Request Amount:**
  * At a 50% match ratio, $34,000 in verified cash match can support a maximum request of:
    $$\frac{\$34,000}{0.50} = \mathbf{\$68,000}$$
  * If Puente maintains the maximum allowable request of $75,000 under RFP Section 3, it would require a minimum cash match of $37,500 ($75,000 × 50%) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. With only $34,000 documented, there is a **$3,500 match shortfall** for a $75,000 request [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Mandatory Fix:** Choose between two pathways:
  * **Option A (Default with existing documentation):** Cap the grant request at **$68,000**, fully backed by the $34,000 documented cash match (exactly 50.0%) [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * **Option B (To request the full $75,000 ceiling):** Have the Board Treasurer execute an amended commitment letter designating **$17,500** (an additional $3,500) from Puente's program reserve prior to submission, bringing total verified cash match to **$37,500** ($20,000 + $17,500) [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * Remove the $12,000 Rio Grande Medical Supply in-kind contribution from the Match table and Attachment E, describing it solely in the Budget Narrative [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  * Remove Socorro Rotary Club from the Match table and Attachment E entirely [documents/attachments-folder.md, documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].

### Corrected Figures and Re-Budgeting Scenarios

$$\text{Total Request} = \text{Direct Costs} + (0.10 \times \text{Direct Costs}) = 1.10 \times \text{Direct Costs}$$
$$\text{Maximum Direct Costs} = \frac{\text{Total Request}}{1.10}$$

Because the files do not specify which individual direct cost lines should be trimmed to fit the revised direct cost limits, the development team must decide how to adjust specific lines (e.g., personnel FTE, travel mileage, screening supplies, or training) [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].

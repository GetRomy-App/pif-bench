### Executive Summary

Year 1 of the Bergquist-Lund Foundation *Pathways to Work* grant closed on June 30, 2026 [documents/grant-agreement.md]. In preparation for drafting the interim report due to the Foundation by August 14, 2026 [documents/grant-agreement.md], this memorandum provides a compliance and performance review reconciling the grant agreement requirements [documents/grant-agreement.md], the program data submitted by Marcus Bell [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md], and the general ledger records provided by Renee Thao [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md, documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].

Key findings include:
1. **Deliverable Status:** Contrary to program director Marcus Bell’s assessment that all targets were met [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md], only three of the five deliverables (D1, D3, D4) were met under the contractual definitions of the agreement [documents/grant-agreement.md]. Deliverables D2 (completion rate) and D5 (quarterly employer meetings) were **not met** [documents/grant-agreement.md, documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md].
2. **Budget Variances & Prior Approval:** One budget line—**Curriculum & materials**—experienced a variance exceeding the agreement's 10% threshold (underspent by 13.50% / -$1,080.00) [documents/grant-agreement.md, documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]. Because no prior written approval was requested or received [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md], this requires formal disclosure and narrative explanation in the interim report [documents/grant-agreement.md].
3. **Unallowable Costs:** The ledger contains a **$640.00** charge for beer and wine service at a tutor training wrap-up event [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]. Under Section 6.3 of the grant agreement, alcoholic beverages are strictly unallowable [documents/grant-agreement.md]. This cost cannot be reported to the Foundation and must be removed from Fund 310 and reclassified to unrestricted funds [documents/grant-agreement.md].
4. **General Ledger Reconciliation:** The ledger export entries total **$118,306.00** [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv], whereas Renee Thao's cover note states total Year 1 charges were **$119,306.00** [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md]. This $1,000.00 discrepancy must be resolved internally prior to submission.
5. **Net Expenditures and Carryforward:** Adjusting the ledger total ($118,306.00) for the $640.00 unallowable alcohol charge results in **$117,666.00** in allowable Year 1 expenses [documents/grant-agreement.md, documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]. Against the $120,000.00 Year 1 payment received [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md, documents/grant-agreement.md], **$2,334.00** remains unspent and carries forward into Year 2 [documents/grant-agreement.md].

### 3. Financial Compliance: Unallowable Costs & Ledger Discrepancies

#### A. Unallowable Alcohol Expenditure ($640.00)
- **Transaction Detail:** Reference `AP-2242`, dated 2026-05-29, paid to Harborview Taproom for "Spring tutor training wrap-up session — beer and wine service" in the amount of **$640.00**, charged to *Tutor training* [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].
- **Agreement Prohibition:** Section 6.3 explicitly mandates that "Grant funds may not be used for: alcoholic beverages..." [documents/grant-agreement.md]. (Food charges for the same event from Hearthside Catering, reference `AP-2241` for $1,020.00, are allowable under Section 6.2 [documents/grant-agreement.md, documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]).
- **Required Action:** Section 6.4 dictates that an unallowable cost "must be removed from the grant's accounts and paid from the Grantee's own unrestricted funds" and cannot be reported as a grant expense [documents/grant-agreement.md]. Renee Thao must process a reclassification journal entry removing $640.00 from Fund 310 to unrestricted operating funds prior to report submission.

#### B. Reconciliation of Total Ledger Charges ($1,000.00 Discrepancy)
- In her cover memorandum, Renee Thao states: *"Total charged to Fund 310 for Year 1: $119,306.00"* [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md].
- Summing every item in the exported general ledger file (`fund-310-ledger-year1.csv`) yields exactly **$118,306.00** [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]:
  - Personnel: $68,220.00 (gross) - $1,240.00 (JE-0412) = $66,980.00 [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]
  - Fringe benefits: $13,644.00 (gross) - $248.00 (JE-0412F) = $13,396.00 [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]
  - Non-personnel lines: $12,560.00 (Supports) + $6,920.00 (Curriculum) + $5,340.00 (Tutor training) + $3,310.00 (Travel) + $9,800.00 (Evaluation) = $37,930.00 [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]
  - Total: $66,980.00 + $13,396.00 + $37,930.00 = $118,306.00 [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].
- There is an exact **$1,000.00 discrepancy** between Renee’s memo and the underlying ledger export. Renee must confirm whether this was a clerical typo in her memo or if an unposted entry is missing from the export.

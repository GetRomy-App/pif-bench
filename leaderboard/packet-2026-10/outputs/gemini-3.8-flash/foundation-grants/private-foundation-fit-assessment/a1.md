# Foundation Fit Assessment: Fresh Routes Project

**To:** Development Committee, Millstream Community Food Bank  
**From:** Elena Ruiz-Hartman, Director of Development  
**Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Assessment and Prioritization of Three Foundations Suggested at the September 10, 2026 Board Meeting  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Prioritized Ranking

At the September 10, 2026 meeting of the Board of Directors, three private foundations were suggested for the Fresh Routes project: the Garrison Ridge Foundation, the Marwood Family Foundation, and the Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]. Fresh Routes carries a total budget of $900,000, with $150,000 committed from the board-designated facilities reserve, leaving a remaining need of $490,000 in capital support (cold-storage addition and refrigerated box truck) and $260,000 in program support (southern-county mobile markets) [documents/project-summary.md].

Pursuant to Section 7 of the Foundation Grants Policy, staff evaluated each foundation against its most recent Form 990-PF and published website, assessing mission fit, geographic fit (Section 4), type of support fit (Section 5), application procedures and deadlines, board/staff relationships (Section 3), and request sizing (Section 6) [documents/grants-policy.md].

The foundations are ranked as follows:

1. **First Priority: Garrison Ridge Foundation (Open Application / Direct Approach)**  
   Fits mission and South-Central Pennsylvania/Lancaster County geography; fits **program support** (mobile markets); accepts unsolicited requests via an open portal; next deadline is March 1, 2027; recommended ask range is **$15,000 to $30,000** for program support [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md], [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
2. **Second Priority: Marwood Family Foundation (Reachable Only via Board Relationship)**  
   Excellent mission, geographic, and capital/program fit, but bars unsolicited requests (Form 990-PF preselection box checked; website bars unsolicited proposals); under Policy Section 3, reachable **solely** through Millstream Director Margaret W. Lindqvist, who serves as a Marwood trustee, to inquire whether Marwood would invite a request; if invited, the policy-derived ask range is **$25,000 to $54,000** [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md], [documents/board-roster.md].
3. **Third Priority: Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust (Not a Fit / Geographic Fit Not Established)**  
   Strong mission and type-of-support fit, but failed geographic fit under Policy Section 4 (zero 2025 grants to Pennsylvania organizations; giving history centered in NC, SC, and VA; corporate trustee's Philadelphia address does not establish fit) [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. No proposal may be prepared; development staff may make a single inquiry regarding PA eligibility [documents/grants-policy.md].

## 2. Individual Foundation Assessments

### Priority 1: Garrison Ridge Foundation

*Suggested by Raymond Ibarra [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]*

#### Giving History (Tax Year 2025)
- **Total Grants Paid:** $480,000 across 13 grants [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
- **Grants to Pennsylvania Organizations:** 12 of the 13 grants ($460,000 of $480,000) went to organizations located in Pennsylvania (Adams, Cumberland, Dauphin, Lancaster, Lebanon, Perry, and York counties); only 1 grant ($20,000) went outside PA (Westminster, MD) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].

#### Alignment Analysis
- **Mission Fit:** High. The foundation's stated priorities include "Basic needs — food access and nutrition, housing stability, and help for families in crisis" [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Fresh Routes directly addresses food access and nutrition [documents/project-summary.md].
- **Geographic Fit:** High fit under Policy Section 4. Published guidelines explicitly list Lancaster County among its seven south-central Pennsylvania giving areas [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md], and 12 of 13 grants paid in 2025 were to PA organizations [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
- **Type of Support Fit:** **Program support only.** Policy Section 5 mandates asking a foundation only for a type of support that appears on its most recent Form 990-PF grants-paid list unless published guidelines expressly invite that type [documents/grants-policy.md]. All 13 grants paid in 2025 were for program or operating support (e.g., mobile pantry operations, summer meals, SNAP outreach, case management) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. Furthermore, published guidelines state: "Our grants support programs and the organizations that run them" and do not invite capital requests [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Therefore, Millstream **cannot** request capital funding from Garrison Ridge, but may request program support for the southern-county mobile markets ($260,000 component need) [documents/project-summary.md].

#### How and When It Accepts Requests
- **Differences Between Form 990-PF and Website (Policy Section 1):** Form 990-PF (filed May 2026 for tax year 2025) states that letters of inquiry (LOIs) of no more than two pages are accepted on a rolling basis by mail to Teresa M. Albright [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. However, the website states that since January 2026, LOIs are submitted via an online portal and reviewed in two set cycles per year (Spring and Fall) [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Following the website as required by Policy Section 1 [documents/grants-policy.md], Millstream must adhere to the two-cycle portal process.
- **Application Process & Next Deadline:** Two-step process: online LOI (maximum two pages), followed by an invited full proposal approximately seven weeks later [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. LOIs for the Fall 2026 cycle closed September 1, 2026 [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. The next deadline falling after this assessment is the **Spring 2027 cycle: LOI due March 1, 2027 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern** (invited full proposals due mid-April 2027; decisions announced in June 2027) [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md].

#### Board or Staff Relationships
- None. None of Millstream's board directors or staff members serve as an officer, director, or staff member of the Garrison Ridge Foundation [documents/board-roster.md], [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].

#### Sizing the First Request (Policy Section 6)
Comparable grants paid during 2025 to Pennsylvania organizations focused on food access, hunger relief, or nutrition [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]:
1. Upper Dauphin Food Pantry (Millersburg, PA) — SNAP outreach and enrollment: $15,000
2. Hanover Area Food Share (Hanover, PA) — Summer meals for children: $20,000
3. Carlisle Community Meals (Carlisle, PA) — Senior congregate meals: $25,000
4. Gettysburg Area Nutrition Program (Gettysburg, PA) — Nutrition education classes: **$30,000**
5. Southern Lancaster County Food Network (Quarryville, PA) — Mobile market for fresh produce: $35,000
6. York Fresh Start Pantry (York, PA) — General operating support: $40,000
7. Lebanon Valley Food Hub (Lebanon, PA) — Mobile pantry operations: $60,000

*(Note: Harrisburg Youth Mentoring Collaborative, Mechanicsburg Community Clinic, Perry County Literacy Council, Lancaster Reentry Partners, and Dauphin County Diaper Bank do not focus on food access/hunger/nutrition; Mason-Dixon Food Share is located in Maryland [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].)*

- **Total Comparable Grants:** 7 (an odd number) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
- **Bottom of Range (Smallest):** $15,000 [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md], [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **Top of Range (Median — 4th grant):** $30,000 [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md], [documents/grants-policy.md]. This does not exceed the $260,000 remaining need for program support [documents/project-summary.md].
- **Ask Range:** **$15,000 to $30,000** [documents/grants-policy.md].

#### Recommended Approach
Submit an online LOI of up to two pages via Garrison Ridge's portal for the March 1, 2027 deadline, requesting **$15,000 to $30,000 in program support** for the Fresh Routes southern-county mobile markets (commencing June 2027) [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md], [documents/project-summary.md].

### Priority 2: Marwood Family Foundation

*Suggested by Joan E. Pfautz [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]*

#### Giving History (Tax Year 2025)
- **Total Grants Paid:** $708,000 across 10 grants [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- **Grants to Pennsylvania Organizations:** 10 of 10 grants ($708,000) went to organizations in Pennsylvania (all 10 located within Lancaster County) [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].

#### Alignment Analysis
- **Mission Fit:** Exceptional. The foundation funds projects to strengthen Lancaster County by "feeding neighbors who are hungry, keeping families housed, and helping children learn," specifically helping organizations "build, repair and equip the places where they serve" [documents/marwood-website.md].
- **Geographic Fit:** High fit under Policy Section 4. Guidelines name Lancaster County [documents/marwood-website.md], and 100% of 2025 grants were paid to Lancaster County, PA organizations [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- **Type of Support Fit:** Fits both capital and program support under Policy Section 5. The 2025 grants list includes substantial capital awards (walk-in freezer/cold storage $85,000; commercial kitchen renovation $60,000; building purchase $150,000; refrigerated van $48,000; warehouse shelving $45,000) as well as program/operating support ($25,000) [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. Its published guidelines specifically emphasize building, repairing, and equipping facilities [documents/marwood-website.md]. This aligns directly with Fresh Routes' capital components ($490,000 remaining need for cold storage and refrigerated truck) [documents/project-summary.md].

#### Policy Restrictions on Unsolicited Requests (Policy Section 2)
- Form 990-PF checks the box stating that the foundation "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds" [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- Its website explicitly confirms: "The Marwood Family Foundation does not accept unsolicited proposals, letters of inquiry or applications... Grant requests sent to this address will not be considered" [documents/marwood-website.md].
- **Rule:** Under Policy Section 2, Marwood may receive **no unsolicited proposal, letter of inquiry, application, or other written request for funds** from staff or board members. Strong mission fit does not override this rule [documents/grants-policy.md].

#### Board or Staff Relationships (Policy Section 3)
- **Margaret W. Lindqvist**, a Director on Millstream's Board of Directors [documents/board-roster.md], serves as a Trustee (1.0 hour/week, uncompensated) of the Marwood Family Foundation [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- Under Policy Section 3.2, because Marwood bars unsolicited requests, **this relationship is the ONLY permitted approach** [documents/grants-policy.md].
- Ms. Lindqvist may approach fellow Marwood trustees to ask whether the foundation would welcome a conversation with Millstream or invite a formal request [documents/grants-policy.md]. Staff must prepare and submit nothing unless Marwood extends a written invitation [documents/grants-policy.md].
- Under Policy Section 3.3, Ms. Lindqvist must take no part in Marwood's decision on any Millstream request [documents/grants-policy.md].

#### Sizing the Request (Policy Section 6, in the event an invitation is issued)
Comparable grants paid during 2025 to Pennsylvania food access, hunger relief, or nutrition organizations [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]:
1. Manheim Community Food Cupboard — General operating support: $25,000
2. Mount Joy Area Pantry — Loading dock and warehouse shelving: $45,000
3. Elizabethtown Neighbors Food Shelf — Refrigerated van: $48,000
4. Lititz Meals on Wheels — Commercial kitchen renovation: $60,000
5. Ephrata Area Community Pantry — Walk-in freezer and cold-storage expansion: $85,000
6. Columbia Hope Kitchen — Building purchase for soup kitchen/pantry: $150,000

*(Note: Lancaster Tenant Assistance League, Strasburg Reading Circle, New Holland Free Clinic, and Quarryville Youth Center focus on housing, reading, dental healthcare, and after-school youth programming [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].)*

- **Total Comparable Grants:** 6 (an even number) [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- **Bottom of Range (Smallest):** $25,000 [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md], [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **Top of Range (Median — average of 3rd and 4th grants):** ($48,000 + $60,000) / 2 = **$54,000** [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md], [documents/grants-policy.md]. This does not exceed remaining capital need ($490,000) or program need ($260,000) [documents/project-summary.md].
- **Ask Range:** **$25,000 to $54,000** [documents/grants-policy.md].

#### Recommended Approach
Staff prepare no materials. Ms. Lindqvist should be briefed and requested to sound out Marwood trustees regarding their interest in Fresh Routes' cold storage and mobile vehicle needs. If Marwood issues a written invitation, staff will submit a proposal for **$25,000 to $54,000 for capital support** (specifically cold-storage equipment or the refrigerated truck) [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/project-summary.md].

### Priority 3: Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust

*Suggested by Dr. Owen M. Calloway [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]*

#### Giving History (Tax Year 2025)
- **Total Grants Paid:** $390,000 across 12 grants [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
- **Grants to Pennsylvania Organizations:** **0 of 12 grants.** All 12 grants paid in 2025 went to organizations in North Carolina (5 grants), South Carolina (4 grants), and Virginia (3 grants) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. The grant to "Lancaster County Hunger Coalition" ($20,000) was awarded to Lancaster, **South Carolina**, not Lancaster, Pennsylvania [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].

#### Alignment Analysis
- **Mission Fit:** High. Dedicated to hunger relief, fresh food access, pantries, mobile markets, and food rescue [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].
- **Geographic Fit:** **NOT A FIT under Policy Section 4.** Policy Section 4 dictates that geographic fit is established only if the 990-PF grants list includes at least one grant to a Pennsylvania organization, or if published guidelines name Lancaster County or Pennsylvania [documents/grants-policy.md]. Calloway-Deane paid zero PA grants in 2025 [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], and its published history reflects a grocery chain that served small towns across the Carolinas and Virginia [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Although corporate trustee Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company has an office in Philadelphia, PA, Policy Section 4 explicitly states: *"A foundation's own mailing address does not establish fit"* [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
- **Type of Support Fit:** Fits capital (warehouse expansion, truck purchase, equipment) and program support [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].

#### How and When It Accepts Requests
- Unsolicited box on Form 990-PF is not checked [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Website confirms online applications open March 1 and September 1, with deadlines on April 15 and October 15 [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].
- The next deadline is **October 15, 2026** [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. However, because geographic fit is not established, Millstream cannot submit an application [documents/grants-policy.md].

#### Board or Staff Relationships
- None. Dr. Owen M. Calloway is a Millstream board director [documents/board-roster.md], but has no role with the Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust (the trustees are Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company, Harriet Deane Pruitt, and Samuel R. Deane) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Dr. Calloway suggested the trust solely because he read about its funding priorities [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].

#### Sizing the First Request (Policy Section 6)
- Because Calloway-Deane made zero grants to Pennsylvania organizations in 2025, there are **0 comparable grants** [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], [documents/grants-policy.md].
- Under Policy Section 6: *"If the foundation made no comparable grants, set no ask range and refer the question to the Executive Director"* [documents/grants-policy.md].

#### Recommended Approach
Millstream **must not apply** for the October 15, 2026 deadline [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Under Policy Section 4, because the guidelines state no explicit geographic limitation on the website (stating only 501(c)(3) public charity status) [documents/calloway-deane-website.md], the Director of Development may make a single phone ((215) 555-0143) or email inquiry (grants@callowaydeanetrust.org) to Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company to ask whether the Trust would consider an application from a Pennsylvania organization [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Unless written confirmation of eligibility is received, no proposal or LOI will be prepared [documents/grants-policy.md]. If written confirmation is received, sizing will be referred to Executive Director Thomas Greider [documents/grants-policy.md], [documents/board-roster.md].

## 1. Summary

The policy requires this memo to give, for each indicator, the inputs and their sources, the result, the target, and whether the target was met [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. All three indicators are calculated exactly as the policy defines them (§2.5). Under §2.2, gifts in kind are removed from every revenue and expense figure [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

| Indicator | FY2025 result | Target | Met? |
|---|---:|---:|:---:|
| Program expense ratio | **78.3%** | 80.0% or higher | **No** |
| Cost to raise a dollar | **$0.22** | $0.20 or less | **No** |
| Months of operating reserve | **3.1 months** | 3.0 months or more | **Yes** |

Two secondary thresholds apply, and neither is triggered [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]:

- **External fundraising review.** This is required only if the cost to raise a dollar exceeds $0.25. The result is $0.22.
- **Treasurer's ten-day notice to the full board.** This is required only if the reserve falls below 1.5 months. The result is 3.1 months.

Two targets were missed. Under §4.2, management must therefore present corrective plans for the program expense ratio and the cost to raise a dollar within 90 days [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. The policy does not say what event starts the 90 days. The committee may want to fix that date at the September 15 meeting.

**Sources.** The 2025 Form 990 was filed electronically on 2026-08-11 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. The audit received an unmodified opinion from Marrow Bennett CPAs, dated April 22, 2026. The audited statement of functional expenses agrees line for line with Form 990 Part IX [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].

## 3. Indicator calculations

### 3.1 Program expense ratio

**Formula:** (Program service expenses − gifts in kind expensed in program services) ÷ (Total functional expenses − all gifts in kind expensed) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

|---|---:|---|
| Program service expenses (Part IX, line 25, col. B) | $19,066,000 | [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] |
| Less: donated food expensed in program services | (14,180,000) | [documents/audit-notes-2025.md] Note 9; Part IX line 24a |
| **Numerator** | **$4,886,000** | |
| Total functional expenses (Part IX, line 25, col. A) | $20,515,000 | [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] |

**Result:** $4,886,000 ÷ $6,240,000 = **78.3%**

**Target:** 80.0% or higher. **Not met.**

## 4. Staff comments

Section 2.5 of the policy permits these comments. They do not change the reported results above.

**1. The reserve target was met only because of the Okafor bequest.**

- The $600,000 bequest is unrestricted and undesignated, so it counts toward the reserve under §3.3 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md; documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].
- Without it, the reserve would be $904,000, or about 1.9 months. That is below the 3.0-month target, though still above the 1.5-month notification threshold.
- Because Note 10 confirms the bequest is non-recurring [documents/audit-notes-2025.md], the committee may want to weigh this when it considers the 2026 outlook.

**2. The reserve includes donated-food inventory.**

- Net assets without donor restrictions include inventories of $498,000, of which $410,000 is donated food [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
- That inventory is not cash available for operations. The policy counts it as written, so it is included in the reserve.

**3. Liquidity per the audit.**

- The auditor reports $1,581,000 of financial assets available for general expenditure within one year.
- The Food Bank also has an unused $500,000 line of credit [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].

**4. The cost to raise a dollar was close to target.**

- The result is $0.22 against a $0.20 target.
- Meeting $0.20 at 2025 contribution levels would have required fundraising expenses of about $797,000 or less, about $69,000 below actual. Alternatively, it would have required countable contributions of about $4,332,000 or more.
- For context, the "Harvest Table" dinner's $47,000 of direct expenses is netted against event revenue in Part VIII, line 8b, rather than reported in Part IX [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. It therefore does not enter this calculation.

**5. Program expense ratio sensitivity.**

- Reaching 80.0% would have required non-gift-in-kind program spending of about $4,992,000 or more at the same total, about $106,000 above actual.
- The files do not show the prior year's indicators. This memo therefore cannot say whether either missed target reflects a trend.

**6. External publication must use the policy basis.**

- The unadjusted Form 990 program share is $19,066,000 ÷ $20,515,000, or about 92.9% [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
- Under §4.3, only the policy-basis figure of **78.3%** may be published in the annual report, on the website or in grant applications [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

1. **Receive the indicator results.** The program expense ratio was 78.3% (not met), the cost to raise a dollar was $0.22 (not met), and the operating reserve was 3.1 months (met).
2. **Set the deadline for corrective plans.** Management must present plans for the program expense ratio and the cost to raise a dollar within 90 days (§4.2). The committee should confirm the start date for that period, which the policy does not specify.
3. **Note that no further actions are triggered.** No external fundraising review is required (§3.2), and no treasurer notification to the full board is required (§3.3).
4. **Receive the separate report on the $600,000 Okafor bequest** (§3.2).

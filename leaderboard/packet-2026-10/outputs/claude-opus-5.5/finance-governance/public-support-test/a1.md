## 1. The answer

**Yes, we pass, but by a thin margin.** On the 2025 Schedule A, Part II computation for calendar years 2021–2025, the Council's public support percentage is **34.33%**. The one-third threshold is 33⅓% [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

- **Public support (line 6):** $1,260,596
- **Total support (line 11):** $3,671,600
- **Public support needed to reach 33⅓%:** $1,223,867
- **Margin:** about **$36,729 of public support**, or roughly **1.0 percentage point**.

Because line 14 is at least 33⅓%, the line 16a box would be checked. Under the preparer's instructions, that means the Council qualifies as a publicly supported organization for the year [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

The result depends on several classification calls. Section 5 explains why a different treatment of two funders would move us below the line.

## 5. How close is it?

**The cushion.** Public support exceeds the one-third threshold by about $36,729, or roughly 1.0 percentage point. Three judgment calls drive the result. Each has documentary support in the files, and if any were treated differently the answer would change:

1. **Upper Alder Community Foundation is unlimited.**
   - If its grants were instead subject to the 2% limit (for example, if any were found to be earmarked or donor-advised), $61,568 would be added to line 5.
   - Public support would fall to $1,199,028, or **32.66%**, which **fails** the test.
   - The finance manager's notes state they were not earmarked and not from a donor-advised fund [documents/finance-manager-notes.md]. Keeping that documentation ready for Ferrante & Lowe is worthwhile.

2. **DEQ funding is treated as grants, not service contracts.**
   - If DEQ's $160,000 were reclassified to line 12, total support would drop to $3,511,600 and the 2% limit to $70,232.
   - Public support would be $1,090,996, or **31.07%**, which **fails** the test.
   - The notes support grant treatment: we design and run the projects, and DEQ receives no services [documents/finance-manager-notes.md].

3. **The 2024 securities gain is excluded from line 10.**
   - The instructions require excluding it [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
   - Including it would have pushed the percentage down to about 32.7% (1,260,596 ÷ 3,853,600).

**Why large gifts do not help.** Additional dollars from donors already over the limit (Ostrander, Dellwood, Hollins-Pryor) add to total support but not to public support, so they lower the percentage. The percentage rises with:

- contributions from donors who stay under the 2% limit;
- grants from governmental units;
- unearmarked grants from publicly supported charities [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

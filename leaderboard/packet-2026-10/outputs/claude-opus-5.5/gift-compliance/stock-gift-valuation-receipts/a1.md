## Rules applied

All dating and valuation follows Gift Acceptance Policy Section 6 [documents/securities-gift-policy.md].

- **Electronic (DTC) or fund-family transfers.** The date of gift is the date the shares were **credited to the Land Trust's account**. It is not the date the donor instructed the broker or fund, and it is not the date a confirmation letter arrived (§6.2).
- **Properly endorsed certificates mailed to us.** The date of gift is the **postmark date** (§6.2).
- **Tax year.** This is the calendar year in which the date of gift falls. We never date a gift earlier than §6.2 allows, even if the donor asks (§6.2).
- **Stock value.** This is the mean of the day's high and low on the date of gift, not the closing price (§6.3.1).
- **Mutual fund value.** This is the NAV on the date of gift, not the public offering price (§6.3.2).
- **Gift value.** This is shares × per-share value, rounded to the cent. Sale price, proceeds and commissions are never used as the gift value (§6.3.3–6.3.4).

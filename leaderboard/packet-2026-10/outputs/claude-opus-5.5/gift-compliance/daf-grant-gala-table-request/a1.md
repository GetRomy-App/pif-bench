### 1. Why we cannot do what Peggy asked

Peggy asked us to use $3,000 of the grant for the Starlight Gala table and the remaining $12,000 for the Studio Wing. As an alternative, she offered to have Kestrel send a second, smaller grant for the table [documents/halvorsen-email.md]. We cannot do either, for the following reasons:

- **Our policy prohibits it.**
  - DAF funds may not pay for any part of a ticket, table, sponsorship, membership or other benefit, and we do not split a benefit's price between a DAF grant and the advisor [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md, §4.3.1].
  - We do not accept a DAF grant designated for an event purchase, and we never redirect any part of a DAF grant to one [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md, §4.3.3].
  - Under the policy, IRC §4967 imposes penalty taxes when a DAF distribution gives an advisor more than an incidental benefit. We treat gala seats and dinners as more than incidental [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md, §4.3].
- **Kestrel's grant terms prohibit it.**
  - The grant may not be used, in whole or in part, to provide any benefit to the fund's advisors, "including event tickets or tables" [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md, Condition 2].
  - The grant's stated purpose is the Studio Wing Campaign [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md], and we honor the purpose in the sponsor's grant letter [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md, §4.1].
- **A second grant for the table is also off the table** under §4.3.3. If one arrives designated for the gala, hold it and contact me before depositing it.

We can accept this grant on Kestrel's terms by booking it entirely to the Studio Wing. The check therefore does not need to be returned under Condition 4 [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].

- the payment amount: $3,000.00;
- a description of the goods and services: a table of 8 at the Starlight Gala on 2026-10-17, including dinner, wine pairing, entertainment and valet parking [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md];

- the resulting deductible amount: $3,000.00 − $1,480.00 = **$1,520.00**.

This is a standard table of 8, not a patron table, so it includes no program listing. Program listings carry no value in any case [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md].

## Part 2 — Reply to Peggy Halvorsen

**To:** Margaret (Peggy) Halvorsen
**From:** Jordan Ellery, Director of Development, Cedar Hollow Arts Center
**Subject:** Re: Kestrel grant + our gala table

Dear Peggy,

Thank you, and please thank Erik too! The $15,000 grant from The Halvorsen Family Fund arrived safely on September 8. It is a wonderful boost for the Studio Wing at exactly the moment the building is taking shape.

**The grant and the Studio Wing.** We are directing the full $15,000 to the Studio Wing Campaign, which is the purpose Kestrel named in its grant letter. It counts in full toward the statement of intent you and Erik signed for the campaign. With your gifts in January and December 2025, you have now given $35,000 of the $40,000 you set out to give through 2028. We are so grateful.

**The gala table.** I wish I could make this simpler for you. Unfortunately, we are not able to use any part of a donor-advised fund grant toward gala tickets or tables. Kestrel's grant terms prohibit it, and federal rules on donor-advised funds bar fund grants from paying for benefits like dinners and event seats. For the same reason, we couldn't accept a second Kestrel grant for the table, so please don't trouble yourself asking them for one.

The table invoice for $3,000 (INV-G26-0112) is due October 2. It can be paid by personal check or card. Once it is paid, we will send you a receipt for it showing the value of the evening and the portion that is tax-deductible.

**The receipt for the $15,000.** I know your tax preparer likes to have everything well before year-end, so I want to explain this one clearly. Because the grant comes from a donor-advised fund, Kestrel Charitable Trust is the legal donor, so our formal acknowledgment goes to Kestrel. Any tax deduction for you and Erik came when you contributed to your fund, so there is no separate receipt for the grant itself. You will receive a thank-you letter from us recognizing The Halvorsen Family Fund's $15,000 grant. Kestrel's own records of your fund contributions should be what your preparer needs.

Erik and I share your excitement about the wing, and I'm looking forward to seeing you both on October 17. You're at table 3, and our events team will confirm all the table details with you before the night.

With warm thanks,

Jordan Ellery
Director of Development
Cedar Hollow Arts Center

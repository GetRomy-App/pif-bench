## 2. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment

### Facts

- **Type:** donor-restricted permanent endowment, established March 15, 2017, with a $400,000 gift from Ellen M. Ruiz [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- **Value:** $512,860 at August 31, 2026 [documents/fund-statements.md].
- **Perpetuity:** the original gift value "shall be maintained in perpetuity" (§2) [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- **Purpose:** distributions are to be used "only for scholarships to students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) program," with preference for first-generation students (§3) [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- **Scope of the restriction:** "All distributions, and all investment return of the Fund, are subject to the purpose in Section 3" (§4) [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- **Program status:** the ADN program is **active**. It has 84 students enrolled for Fall 2026 and 31 expected to graduate in May 2027. It is approved by the State Board of Nursing through 2031 [documents/program-status.md].

### What we cannot do

**1. Move the fund into general operations.**
- Our policy says restricted funds "are used only as the gift instrument directs" (§4.1) [documents/endowment-policy.md].
- The available routes under UPMIFA §6 each fail:
  - **§6(c), court cy pres.** This requires the purpose to be "unlawful, impracticable, impossible to achieve, or wasteful" [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The ADN program is enrolling students and approved through 2031, so none of those conditions applies [documents/program-status.md]. Counsel's practice note says: "A budget shortfall at the institution does not, by itself, make a fund's purpose impracticable or impossible" [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
  - **§6(d), small and old funds.** This is not available. The fund is worth $512,860, well above the $25,000 limit. It was established in 2017, so fewer than 20 years have elapsed [documents/upmifa-summary.md; documents/fund-statements.md].
  - **Gift agreement §5.** This lets the board redirect distributions only if the ADN program ceases. Even then, the money can go only to the closest health-sciences scholarship, not to operations [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. The program has not ceased [documents/program-status.md].

**2. Use this year's earnings or distribution for operations.**
- Investment return and distributions carry the fund's purpose. This is set out in gift agreement §4 [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md], policy §§3.2 and 4.1 [documents/endowment-policy.md], and UPMIFA §4(a) as summarized by counsel [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
- The FY2027 distribution under policy §3.1 is 4% of the average of the twelve quarter-end values from September 30, 2023 through June 30, 2026 [documents/endowment-policy.md; documents/fund-statements.md]:
  - Sum of the twelve values: $5,930,200
  - Average: $494,183.33
  - **Distribution: $19,767.33**
- That $19,767.33 is for ADN scholarships only.
- The files do not show whether the operating budget currently pays for any ADN scholarships from unrestricted money. If it does, applying the Ruiz distribution to those awards could relieve the budget by a corresponding amount. That should be checked against the FY2027 budget.

**3. Lend operations $180,000 (Joan's proposal).**
- Policy §4.2: "Donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund, whatever the term or rate of the loan" [documents/endowment-policy.md].
- Repayment with interest does not cure the problem. The policy applies "whatever the term or rate."
- The gift agreement also subjects all of the fund's assets and return to the ADN purpose [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- Amending §4.2 would not change the gift agreement. Any view that a loan could be squared with the gift agreement is a question for counsel, and nothing in the files supports it.

### What remains possible in principle: donor consent, UPMIFA §6(a)

- With Mrs. Ruiz's consent "in a record," the Foundation may release or modify a restriction. However, the release "may not allow a fund to be used for a purpose other than a charitable purpose of the institution" [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
- The files do not tell us:
  - whether Mrs. Ruiz is living or reachable;
  - what her views would be;
  - whether general operations would qualify as "a charitable purpose of the institution" under this state's law; or
  - how a release would interact with the perpetuity requirement in §2.
- There is no statutory waiting period for this route, so the timing would depend entirely on the donor.
- This would mean asking a memorial donor to give up both the nursing purpose and the perpetual endowment. **I do not recommend it as a way to close a one-year gap.** If the board wants to explore it, counsel should advise first.
- No Attorney General notice is needed for Ruiz, because we would not be pursuing a court route.

## 3. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund

### Facts

- **Origin:** a $5,000 gift from Margaret E. Calloway, accepted by the board on May 26, 1994 [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].
- **Purpose:** the earnings are to provide "an annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the College's Secretarial Studies certificate program, so that a young person entering office work may begin with confidence and a little help" [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].
- **Value:** $18,420 at August 31, 2026 [documents/fund-statements.md].
- **Activity:** no award has been made since 2011, and the earnings have accumulated in the fund [documents/fund-statements.md].
- **Program status:** the certificate was closed on June 30, 2011, and "no student can now enroll in or graduate from it" [documents/program-status.md].
- **Successor program:** the Office Administration A.A.S. was created in Fall 2012 as "the College's program preparing students for administrative and office careers." It has 47 students enrolled for Fall 2026 [documents/program-status.md].
- **Donor:** Mrs. Calloway died on February 9, 2002, and her letter names no successor or advisor [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]. Donor consent under §6(a) therefore appears unavailable. Whether anyone else could consent on her behalf is not addressed in the files.

### Route: UPMIFA §6(d), small and old funds

Each condition is met on the files [documents/upmifa-summary.md]:

| Condition | Status |
|---|---|
| Restriction is impossible to achieve | Met. No student can graduate from the certificate [documents/program-status.md]. |
| Total value under $25,000 | Met. $18,420 at August 31, 2026 [documents/fund-statements.md]. Counsel should confirm the value on the notice date. |
| More than 20 years since establishment | Met. Established May 26, 1994, which is about 32 years ago [documents/fund-statements.md]. |
| Property used consistent with the gift's charitable purposes | **This is the limit.** See below. |

### What we cannot do

- We cannot fold the fund into general operations.
- §6(d)(3) requires the institution to use the property "in a manner consistent with the charitable purposes expressed in the gift instrument" [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Those purposes are an award to an outstanding graduating student entering office work [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].
- General operating support does not fit that description on the face of the documents.
- In any case, the fund's $18,420 would cover only about a tenth of the $180,000 gap.

### What we can do

- Modify the restriction so that the fund provides an annual award to an outstanding graduating student in the **Office Administration A.A.S.** program.
- This is the College's current program preparing students for office careers [documents/program-status.md], and it closely tracks Mrs. Calloway's stated aim.
- Awards could resume with the accumulated balance.

### Steps and dates

1. **By Friday, October 2:**
   - Renata decides on the proposed modification (the Office Administration award).
   - Renata authorizes counsel to send the notice. Policy §4.3 allows the President to do this before the board meets [documents/endowment-policy.md].
   - Finance confirms the fund's current value for the notice.
2. **Monday, October 5, 2026:** counsel sends the §6(d) notice to the Attorney General.
3. **October 21, 2026 (board meeting):**
   - The board receives a report on the notice and the proposed modification.
   - The 60-day period will not have run yet, so the release cannot take effect at this meeting.
   - Counsel should advise whether the board may adopt a resolution on October 21 that takes effect on December 4, or whether the resolution must be adopted on or after December 4. Policy §4.3 requires a board resolution [documents/endowment-policy.md].
4. **Friday, December 4, 2026:** this is 60 days after the October 5 notice and the **earliest date the release or modification can take effect** [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
   - If a resolution cannot be adopted in advance, the board will need to act at a meeting on or after this date.
   - The files do not give the date of the next regular board meeting after October 21.
5. **After December 4:** update the fund's restriction record and name, and notify the College's Office Administration program.

## 4. Other ways to close the gap

### Operating Reserve (board-designated)

- **Balance:** $240,000 [documents/fund-statements.md].
- **Nature:** it is unrestricted. The board "may change or remove its designation, and use the fund for any purpose of the Foundation, by majority vote of the full Board. No donor consent and no notice to the Attorney General is required" (§5.1) [documents/endowment-policy.md]. UPMIFA §6 does not apply to board-designated funds [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
- **Coverage:** releasing $180,000 would cover the deficit and leave $60,000 in the reserve.
- **Steps:**
  1. Draft a resolution for October 21 releasing up to $180,000 of the designation for FY2027 operations.
  2. The board adopts it by majority of the **full** board. The files do not state the board's size, so the required vote count needs to be confirmed against the bylaws.
- **Timing:** this can be completed on October 21.
- **Optional:** the board may wish to adopt a plan to rebuild the reserve once state funding recovers.

### Reconciling the deficit figures

- The memo request describes a $180,000 FY2027 operating deficit [documents/ed-email.md].
- The fund statements project the general operating fund at **($41,300)** at June 30, 2027, before any action [documents/fund-statements.md].
- The files do not explain how these two figures relate. Finance should reconcile them before the board meeting so the board releases the right amount.

### Other sources

The files contain nothing on other unrestricted gifts, pledges, expense reductions, or other revenue. I cannot assess further options from this record.

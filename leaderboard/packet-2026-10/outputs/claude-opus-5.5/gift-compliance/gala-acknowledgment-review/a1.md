Leaving the program listing unvalued is correct (§4.2) [documents/receipting-policy.md].

**Deductible amount: $7,950.00**

| Benefit | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Gala seats | 10 × $165 | $1,650.00 |
| VIP reception | 10 × $40 | $400.00 |
| **Total benefit** | | **$2,050.00** |

$10,000.00 payment − $2,050.00 benefit = **$7,950.00 deductible**

**Template:** GALA-QPQ. The §6115 disclosure is required.

**The acknowledgment must state:**
- the donors' names;
- the date received (2026-07-30);
- the cash amount of $10,000.00;
- a description of the benefits: a reserved table of 10 gala seats (dinner, open bar, live band) and the VIP pre-gala reception for 10 guests;
- the estimated value of $2,050.00;
- the §6115 disclosure sentence;
- "Your deductible contribution is $7,950.00."

**The acknowledgment must not:**
- list the sponsor-page or signage recognition as a benefit, or assign it a value (§4.2);
- use the $375 ticket price as the benefit value.

**Harriet Lindgren's seat.** Ms. Lindgren sat at this table as the sponsors' guest. Her seat is part of the Vasquez-Hart benefit and is already counted in the 10 seats above (§4.4) [documents/receipting-policy.md].

**CRM correction:**
- Benefit value: change $3,750.00 to **$2,050.00**.
- Receipt amount: this becomes **$7,950.00**.
- Benefits field: update it to describe 10 seats plus the VIP reception for 10.
- Processor note: update it to match.

### P-05: Denise Okafor ❌ Separate the raffle purchase from the gift

**Payment:** $300.00 in one card swipe at the gala on 2026-09-12. Dana's note says "a $200 gift to Books for Every Backpack plus 5 raffle tickets for $100. Booked as one gift." [documents/payment-batch.md]. The $100 matches the raffle price of 5 tickets for $100 [documents/gala-fact-sheet.md].

**What is wrong.** As drafted, the GALA-GIFT template would print "gift of $300.00" and a deductible contribution of $300.00. This breaks three rules [documents/receipting-policy.md]:
- §3.3: raffle purchases are not deductible contributions.
- §6: a combined payment must be treated part by part.
- §7, "may not" item 2: no acknowledgment may count a raffle purchase in a contribution or state a deductible amount for it.

**Deductible amount: $200.00.** The $100 raffle purchase is not deductible, and no deductible amount may be stated for it.

**Template:** GALA-GIFT for the $200 gift. The raffle tickets were a separate purchase and were not given in exchange for the gift.

**The acknowledgment must state:**
- her name;
- the date received (2026-09-12);
- the gift amount of $200.00 to Books for Every Backpack;
- "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution";
- "Your deductible contribution is $200.00."

**The acknowledgment must not:**
- show $300 as the gift or as the deductible amount;
- state any deductible amount for the raffle tickets.

**If the raffle is mentioned.** If the letter refers to the raffle tickets or to the $300 card charge at all, it must say that raffle ticket purchases are not charitable contributions and are not deductible (§6). The GALA-GIFT template does not contain that sentence. The simplest course is to leave the raffle off this acknowledgment. If staff want to mention it, the sentence must be added as an approved template edit.

**CRM correction:**
- Split the $300.00 swipe into two records:
  - a **$200.00 gift**: GALA-GIFT, benefit $0.00, receipt amount $200.00;
  - a **$100.00 raffle purchase**: booked as a raffle sale, not a contribution, and given no acknowledgment showing a deductible amount.
- Update the processor note to match.

### Notes on each deliverable, including corrections to Marcus's talking points

- **D1.** 131 learners against a target of 120 [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. Met.

- **D2.** The target is 75% "of enrolled learners" [documents/grant-agreement.md]. Our enrolled learners number 131, so we needed at least 99 completers (75% × 131 = 98.25). We had 94, which is 71.8% [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md].
  - Marcus measured 94 completers against a "goal of 90 (75% of 120)." That uses the enrollment *target* (120) rather than actual enrollment (131), which the agreement's definition does not support.
  - This deliverable is **not met**.

- **D3.** The agreement's denominator is "learners completing 40 or more hours," which is 94 [documents/grant-agreement.md].
  - 61 of those 94 gained an EFL, which is **64.9%**. That clears the 60% target.
  - Marcus's 83.6% (61 of 73) uses only the post-tested learners as the denominator. It leaves out the 21 completers who were not post-tested [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. The report should state 64.9%.
  - For planning: the Year 2 target rises to 65% [documents/grant-agreement.md]. Our Year 1 result would just miss that. The 21 completers who left before the post-test window are the main drag on this measure.

- **D4.** The deliverable counts tutors "who complete at least 12 hours of pre-service training" [documents/grant-agreement.md]. That count is 26. The 27th tutor had completed only 8 hours as of June 30 [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md].
  - The deliverable is met, but the report should say **26**, not "27 tutors trained."

- **D5.** Meetings were held on:
  - 2025-09-18 (Q1)
  - 2025-12-11 (Q2)
  - 2026-03-19 (Q3)

  The Q4 meeting planned for 2026-06-11 was postponed to **2026-07-16** [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. Year 1 ended June 30, 2026, and Year 2 begins July 1, 2026 [documents/grant-agreement.md]. A July 16 meeting therefore falls in **Year 2, Q1**. It cannot count toward Year 1 Q4.
  - Marcus's statement that it "still counts for Q4" is not consistent with the agreement.
  - The July 16 meeting should be counted once, toward Year 2.

### 3.4 The approval problem

Section 5 of the agreement says the following [documents/grant-agreement.md]:
- Any variance of more than 10%, **above or below**, requires the Foundation's **prior** written approval.
- Requests go by email to the program officer.
- Approvals are given only in writing.

Renee reports: "We have not requested or received any budget-change approvals from the Foundation this year" [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md].

This creates two issues:

- **Learner supports (+13.0%).** Marcus describes this overspend as "a good-news story for the report" [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. Under the agreement, it is a variance that required approval we did not get. The enrollment explanation may well be a reasonable justification, but it does not replace the approval requirement.
- **Curriculum & materials (−13.5%).** An *under*spend of more than 10% also triggers the approval requirement. Marcus's notes do not mention this line.

The agreement does not say what happens when a variance over 10% occurs without prior approval, or whether retroactive approval is possible. I am not going to guess at the consequence.

**Recommendation:** Contact Gwen Halloran, Senior Program Officer [documents/grant-agreement.md], before submitting the interim report. Disclose both variances, explain them, and ask in writing how the Foundation wants them handled.

The interim report must also include [documents/grant-agreement.md]:
- an explanation of every variance over 10%; and
- copies of any written approvals under Section 5.

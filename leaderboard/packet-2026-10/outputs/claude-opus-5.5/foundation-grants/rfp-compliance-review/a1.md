## Detailed findings

### 1. Request exceeds the $75,000 cap

- **Finding:** The cover sheet and budget table request **$83,847** [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
- **The rule:** "Requests may not exceed $75,000. Requests above $75,000 will not be considered." The request is the sum of direct and indirect costs [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- **Lucia's reasoning:** Her cover note relies on the program officer's remark at the August info session that the Foundation has "some flexibility for really strong proposals" [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
  - The written RFP leaves no room on this point.
  - A verbal comment should not be relied on against it.
  - If anyone wants to test it, the place to ask is grants@sierraladronescf.org, in writing and before submission [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Even so, I recommend submitting at or under $75,000.
- **Fix:** Reduce the total request to **$75,000 or less** (see Corrected figures).

### 2. Indirect rate exceeds the 10% cap

- **Finding:** Indirect is charged at **15%** of total direct costs, described as "our federal de minimis rate, which we apply to all our grants" [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md; documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
- **The rule:** "Indirect costs may not exceed 10% of the total direct costs in the request" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Our standard rate does not override the funder's cap.
- **Fix:**
  - Recalculate indirect at no more than 10% of total direct costs.
  - Round **down** so the rate never exceeds 10%.
  - Remove the "federal de minimis … all our grants" language from the budget narrative and state the 10% rate instead.

### 3. Arithmetic error in total direct costs

- **Finding:** The budget table lists these direct line items [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]:

  | Line item | Amount |
  |---|---|
  | Personnel | $48,000 |
  | Fringe | $10,560 |
  | Mileage | $5,400 |
  | Screening supplies | $3,850 |
  | Training | $2,600 |
  | Interpretation | $1,500 |
  | **Sum** | **$71,910** |

  The table shows **$72,910**, which overstates direct costs by $1,000. The draft's indirect figure of $10,937 is 15% of the incorrect $72,910.
- **Line items that are internally correct:**
  - 2 × 0.75 FTE × $32,000 = $48,000.
  - 22% × $48,000 = $10,560.
  - 12,000 miles × $0.45 = $5,400.
  - 60 hours × $25 = $1,500.
- **Effect of the error:** Even with the corrected direct total and a 10% indirect rate, the request would be $71,910 + $7,191 = **$79,101**. That is still over the cap, so direct costs must also be reduced.
- **Fix:** Correct the total direct costs, then reduce line items (see Corrected figures).

### 5. Missing required section: Sustainability Plan

- **Finding:** The narrative has Sections 1–4 only [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
- **The rule:** The RFP requires Section 5, "Sustainability Plan — how the work will continue after the grant period, including the sources of future funding," in order and under that heading. Omitting a required section means the application is "returned without review" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- **Current draft:** The only sustainability content is one sentence at the end of the Evaluation Plan, about sustaining the CHW positions through Medicaid managed-care reimbursement [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
- **Fix:**
  - Add a **"5. Sustainability Plan"** heading after the Evaluation Plan.
  - Move the Medicaid reimbursement sentence into it.
  - Name the sources of future funding.
  - The files identify only Medicaid managed-care reimbursement. Any other planned sources (for example, the existing Valencia County contract) would need to come from Lucia or Jim. I have not assumed them.

### 6. Narrative exceeds the 2,500-word limit

- **Finding:** Word's section counts are 410 + 585 + 1,140 + 520 = **2,655 words** [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
  - Lucia's figure of 2,135 [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md] equals Sections 1–3 only. It appears to leave out the 520-word Evaluation Plan.
  - The 480-word budget narrative is correctly excluded, since it goes in a separate portal field with a 750-word limit [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- **The rule:** Sections 1–5 together may not exceed 2,500 words. Over-length applications are returned without review [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- **Fix:**
  - Cut **at least 155 words**, plus however many words the new Sustainability Plan uses.
  - Example: a 200-word Sustainability Plan means Sections 1–4 must come down to 2,300 words or less, a cut of 355 words.
  - Moving the sustainability sentence out of the Evaluation Plan helps slightly.
  - The Project Description, at 1,140 words, is the largest place to trim.
  - Recount all five sections in Word before upload.

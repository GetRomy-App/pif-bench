**The unpaid balance is $70,000, not the $80,000 the CRM shows.** The CRM shows $120,000 paid and an $80,000 balance. That figure reflects the last reconciliation on May 31, 2026, which predates the June 12 check for $10,000 [documents/crm-record.md].

Under DR-7 §5, the balance is the amount pledged less payments applied, so it is **$70,000** ($200,000 − $130,000). That breaks down as:

- $30,000 remaining on the 2026 installment; and
- the $40,000 installment for 2027.

Finance should confirm this figure.

**Other gifts do not reduce the balance.** The Annual Fund gifts, Gala sponsorships and Foundation grants were not applied to the pledge [documents/donor-death-policy.md §5].

**Handling the pledge from here:**

## 4. Planned gift (bequest intention)

**What Gerry documented:**

- **Letter of intent.** A letter signed May 10, 2021 states a bequest of **$100,000** to the Harborview Arts Center Endowment, unrestricted [documents/contact-reports.md; documents/crm-record.md].
- **Status.** It is recorded as an expectancy [documents/crm-record.md].
- **Gerry's wishes.** He asked that it "count toward nothing until it arrives" and that he and Peg be listed by name in the Legacy Circle [documents/contact-reports.md].

**Under DR-7 §6** [documents/donor-death-policy.md]:

- A bequest intention is revocable.
- It remains an expectancy and is not booked as revenue, as a receivable or as campaign credit until the estate's distribution is received.
- Only the Director of Planned Giving contacts the Personal Representative or the estate's attorney to learn whether the will includes a gift to the Center.

**What we do not know.** The files do not show whether the will, as admitted to probate, still contains this bequest.

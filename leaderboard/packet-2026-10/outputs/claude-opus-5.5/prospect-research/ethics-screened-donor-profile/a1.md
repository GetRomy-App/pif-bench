## 1. Summary

- **Who he is:** Marcus D. Ellery is co-founder and President of Ellery Mechanical Contractors, LLC, a commercial HVAC and plumbing firm in Winston-Salem. [documents/research-findings.md — Piedmont Business Review, 2025-11-14]
- **His history with SCYT:** He has been a constituent since 2019. [documents/crm-record.md]
  - He and his wife, Renée, have increased their Annual Fund giving every year, from $1,000 in 2021 to $5,000 in each of 2024, 2025 and 2026. [documents/crm-record.md]
  - A minor child is enrolled in SCYT's Summer Intensive. [documents/crm-record.md]
  - At the 2025 Fall Gala, he asked about the rehearsal hall plans. [documents/crm-record.md]
- **Other philanthropy:** He gives and serves as a board member in the local community:
  - Trustee of the Yadkin Valley Community College Foundation, where the couple established a $25,000 skilled-trades scholarship.
  - Board member of Twin City Housing Partners. [documents/research-findings.md]
- **Five-year giving capacity: $313,750. Rating: B.** (See Section 7.)

## 7. Capacity calculation and rating

**Method:** 5% of real estate + 5% of public securities + 2 × documented giving.

### Real estate

| Property | Basis for counting | 2026 tax value |
|---|---|---|
| 418 Arbor Ridge Ln | Joint with spouse | $1,140,000 |
| 214 Laurel Gap Rd | Joint with spouse | $685,000 |
| 1550 Northpoint Industrial Dr | EMC Properties LLC, sole member | $2,350,000 |
| **Total real estate** | | **$4,175,000** |
| **5% of real estate** | | **$208,750** |

605 Brookstown Industrial Ct is excluded. It is owned by Ellery Mechanical Contractors LLC, which has two members, and no permitted record shows a majority share.

### Public securities
- No SEC filings naming Marcus D. Ellery appear in the research file.
- Equity in the privately held Ellery Mechanical Contractors is not counted.
- **5% of public securities: $0.**

### Documented giving
The window is the five most recent full calendar years, 2021–2025.

|---|---|---|
| SCYT Annual Fund | 2021 | $1,000 |
| SCYT Annual Fund | 2022 | $1,500 |

| SCYT Annual Fund | 2024 | $5,000 |

| Twin City Housing Partners ($10,000–$24,999 range; bottom of range used) | 2023 | $10,000 |
| Forsyth Arts Alliance ($2,500–$4,999 range; bottom of range used) | 2022 | $2,500 |
| **Total documented giving** | | **$52,500** |

The following gifts are not counted:
- **SCYT 2026-04-28, $5,000:** 2026 is not yet a full calendar year.
- **Piedmont Regional Hospital Foundation 2020, $20,000:** falls before the window.
- **Ellery Mechanical's SCYT season sponsorship ($2,500) and Twin City Food Bank sponsorship ($15,000):** these are business giving.

| Component | Amount |

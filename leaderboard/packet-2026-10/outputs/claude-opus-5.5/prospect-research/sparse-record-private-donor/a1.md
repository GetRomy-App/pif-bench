## 6. Gala ask

**Rule for unrated prospects:** Every personal ask is 1.5 × the last gift, rounded up to the next multiple of $250. "Last gift" means the most recent outright, hard-credited gift. Tickets, other purchases and soft credits are excluded, including soft credits for gifts from a business the donor owns [documents/research-standards.md].

**Step 1: Exclude the most recent entries that do not count** [documents/crm-record.md]:

- 2026-07-02, $2,000: soft credit for the Hartwell Bookkeeping & Tax, LLC sponsorship. Excluded.
- 2026-05-14, $150: Spring Recital reception tickets. Excluded.

**Step 2: Identify the last gift.** It is the 2026-04-03 Spring Appeal check for **$750** [documents/crm-record.md].

**Step 3: Calculate the ask.**

- 1.5 × $750 = $1,125.
- Rounded up to the next multiple of $250, this gives **$1,250**.

**Gala ask: $1,250.** This applies to the paddle raise and to any sponsorship ask. The standards do not permit a $25,000 Presenting Sponsor ask, or any ask above $1,250, until a discovery visit has occurred and she has been rated [documents/research-standards.md].

### How the tiers were built

- **Lead gift.** The lead gift is 20% of $10,000,000, which is $2,000,000. Each lower tier halves the gift amount and doubles the number of gifts [documents/counsel-chart-method.md §1.1–1.2].
- **Where the named tiers stop.** They stop at $250,000. The next tier would be $125,000, which is below the $250,000 floor, so it is not charted [documents/counsel-chart-method.md §1.3].
- **All other gifts.** This line is the goal minus the $8,000,000 cumulative total of the named tiers, which leaves $2,000,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md §1.4].
- **Prospect ratios.** The ratio is 3:1 at tiers of $1,000,000 and above, and 4:1 below that. Each ratio is applied to the gifts still needed at that tier [documents/counsel-chart-method.md §3].

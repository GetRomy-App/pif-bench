## 1. Rules applied

**Fiscal years.** The fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30. It is named for the calendar year in which it ends. A gift belongs to the fiscal year that contains its gift date [documents/metrics-definitions.md]. The windows used here are:

| Fiscal year | Dates |
|---|---|
| FY2021 | 2020-07-01 to 2021-06-30 |
| FY2022 | 2021-07-01 to 2022-06-30 |
| FY2023 | 2022-07-01 to 2023-06-30 |
| FY2024 | 2023-07-01 to 2024-06-30 |
| FY2025 | 2024-07-01 to 2025-06-30 |
| FY2026 ("last year") | 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30 |
| FY2027 to date ("this year") | 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-15 |

**Who counts.** Households are counted, not individual records. Members who share a household ID count as one donor. Organizations are excluded whatever they gave [documents/metrics-definitions.md].

**What counts.** Only hard-credit, Posted gifts from individuals count. The following are excluded [documents/metrics-definitions.md]:
- soft credits, including peer-to-peer page credits, spouse credits and employee credits on matching gifts;
- refunded gifts;
- employer matching gifts.

Joint-account gifts count once for the household [documents/metrics-definitions.md].

**Definitions** [documents/metrics-definitions.md]:
- **LYBUNT:** a household with at least one countable gift in FY2026 and none in FY2027 to date.
- **SYBUNT:** a household with at least one countable gift in FY2021–FY2025, none in FY2026, and none in FY2027 to date. A household whose last countable gift is before FY2021 is lapsed, not SYBUNT.
- **Retention:** households with countable gifts in both FY2025 and FY2026, divided by households with a countable gift in FY2025, to one decimal place.

## 7. Limitations

- **Data completeness through September 15.** The most recent gift in the file is dated 2026-09-10 [documents/gift-history.csv]. The files do not say whether every gift received through the report date has been entered. Gifts posted late would move households off the LYBUNT or SYBUNT lists.
- **Lists may change before the appeal.** The counts are as of September 15, 2026. Any household that gives before September 24, or before the fall appeal mailing, would drop off its list. The lists should be refreshed immediately before the mail file is pulled.
- **No mailing details.** The files do not include contact information, communication preferences or do-not-solicit flags. This report does not determine whether any listed household should receive the appeal.

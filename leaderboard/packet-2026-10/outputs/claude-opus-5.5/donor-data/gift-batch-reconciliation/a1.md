## 2. Discrepancies and adjusting entries

### Discrepancy 1 — Keying error: G-41809, Marisol Etxeberria (donor 37702)

- **Found:** The gift is posted in B-2607-02 at **$15,000.00** (check 4410, General Fund) [documents/crm-july-batches.csv]. The bank's deposit item for check 4410 in RDC-0710-1 is **$1,500.00** [documents/bank-deposit-detail.md]. The posting is overstated by $13,500.00.
- **Adjusting entry:** Edit G-41809 to **$1,500.00** to match the bank deposit item, per the standards' keying-error rule [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.1]. The effect on the July total is −$13,500.00.
- **Acknowledgment:** An acknowledgment was sent on 2026-07-13 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv]. Presumably it states the $15,000 posted amount. Void it and send a corrected acknowledgment for $1,500.00 [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.1].
  - This should be done promptly, because the donor may currently hold a receipt for ten times her actual gift.

### Discrepancy 2 — June-postmarked gift posted to July: G-41803, Gerald & Mae Lindgren (donor 38877)

- **Found:** The gift is in B-2607-01 for **$1,000.00** (check 9014, General Fund). It shows gift date 2026-07-02, received 2026-07-02, and **postmark 2026-06-30** [documents/crm-july-batches.csv].
  - The standards say a mailed gift postmarked on or before June 30 belongs to the fiscal year ending that June 30. Such gifts go to B-2606-YE and "never to a July batch" [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §2].
  - The gift date should be the postmark date, 2026-06-30, not the received date. The check date of 2026-06-27 does not decide the gift date [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §2].
  - The check was deposited in July in RDC-0706-1 [documents/bank-deposit-detail.md], so the bank receipt stays in July.
- **Adjusting entry:**
  - Change the G-41803 gift date to **2026-06-30**.
  - Move the gift from B-2607-01 to **B-2606-YE** (FY2026).
  - The effect on the July total is −$1,000.00.
  - B-2606-YE is open for posting only until **August 14, 2026** [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §2].
- **Acknowledgment:** An acknowledgment was sent on 2026-07-08 with the July gift date [documents/crm-july-batches.csv]. Treat the date as a keying error: void it and send a corrected acknowledgment dated June 30, 2026 [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.1].

### Discrepancy 3 — Returned item (NSF): G-41810, Hollis Grant (donor 41933)

- **Found:** The gift is posted in B-2607-02 for **$500.00** (check 2231, Backpack Program) [documents/crm-july-batches.csv]. The bank returned check 2231 from RDC-0710-1 as **NSF on 2026-07-16**, debiting −$500.00 plus a −$15.00 returned-item fee [documents/bank-deposit-detail.md]. No reversal appears in the CRM export.
- **Adjusting entry:**
  - Keep G-41810 as posted; do not delete it.
  - Post a **reversing adjustment of −$500.00** to Hollis Grant, Backpack Program, **dated 2026-07-16**, in a July 2026 batch [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.3]. The effect on the July total is −$500.00.
  - The $15.00 returned-item fee is a bank charge. Code it to account **6130 (Bank fees)**, not as a gift adjustment [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.3].
  - The standards call for the reversal to go in "the batch for the month of the return" but do not name a specific July batch. Please designate the batch when posting.
- **Acknowledgment / notification:**
  - Void the acknowledgment sent 2026-07-13 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv].
  - Notify the relationship manager [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.3]. The files do not identify Mr. Grant's relationship manager.

## 3. Items reviewed with no discrepancy

- **Check gifts:** All 21 CRM check numbers and the remaining 20 amounts match bank items in the five deposits [documents/crm-july-batches.csv; documents/bank-deposit-detail.md]. The one amount that does not match is G-41809, covered in Discrepancy 1.
- **Check gift dates:** For gifts received July 1–15, the gift date equals the recorded postmark, except G-41803. Gifts received from July 16 onward use the date received [documents/crm-july-batches.csv; documents/gift-processing-standards.md §2].
- **Card gifts:** All 15 PayBridge transactions match a CRM posting by transaction ID, donor, created date and gross amount [documents/paybridge-july-2026.csv; documents/crm-july-batches.csv].
- **PayBridge fees:** Each fee equals 2.2% + $0.30 of gross [documents/paybridge-july-2026.csv; documents/gift-processing-standards.md §3].
- **PayBridge payouts:** Each payout equals the sum of its transactions' net amounts and matches the bank ACH credit to the cent [documents/paybridge-july-2026.csv; documents/bank-deposit-detail.md].
- **Vendor refund:** RDC-0724-1 item 5, $86.40, is memoed "Valley Office Supply – refund of duplicate payment, inv. 33817" [documents/bank-deposit-detail.md]. It is non-gift money and correctly does not appear in the CRM. Finance should code it in the general ledger [documents/gift-processing-standards.md §4.4].
- **Payout timing:** Lorraine Beck's gift (G-41837, $500.00, created 2026-07-31) is correctly a July gift. Its payout, po_260803KV ($488.70), reached the bank on 2026-08-03 [documents/paybridge-july-2026.csv; documents/bank-deposit-detail.md].

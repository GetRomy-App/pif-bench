# Memorandum

**To:** Dr. Renata Oyelaran, President; Joan Whitacre, Treasurer  
**From:** Malcolm Ferreira-Lund, Director of Finance and Gift Administration  
**Date:** September 28, 2026  
**Subject:** Options for addressing the FY2027 operating deficit  

As requested, I have reviewed our files regarding the Ruiz and Calloway funds and their potential use in closing the Foundation’s projected $180,000 FY2027 operating deficit. I have also identified a viable alternative to close the gap before the October 21 board meeting. 

### 1. The Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment
**What we can and cannot do:** We cannot move the Ruiz fund into general operations, nor can we use its earnings or annual distributions to cover operations, nor can we borrow from it as suggested. 

**Reasons and constraints:** 
* **Moving the fund or using its earnings/distributions:** The Ruiz fund is a donor-restricted permanent endowment valued at $512,860 as of August 31, 2026 ([documents/fund-statements.md]). The gift agreement explicitly limits the use of distributions and investment returns to scholarships for students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) program ([documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]). Our Endowment and Restricted Funds Policy requires us to use donor-restricted funds and their investment returns exactly as the gift instrument directs (§4.1), and limits the spending of distributions solely to the fund's purpose (§3.2) ([documents/endowment-policy.md]). Because the ADN program is active and currently has 84 enrolled students ([documents/program-status.md]), the restriction remains possible and practicable to fulfill. Furthermore, our legal counsel has advised that an operating deficit does not, by itself, make a fund's purpose impossible or impracticable under UPMIFA ([documents/upmifa-summary.md]). 
* **Borrowing from the fund:** Joan asked if we could lend operations $180,000 from the Ruiz endowment for 18 months, with interest ([documents/treasurer-note.md]). We cannot do this. Section 4.2 of our Endowment Policy strictly dictates: "Donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund, whatever the term or rate of the loan" ([documents/endowment-policy.md]).

### 2. The Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund
**What we can and cannot do:** We cannot fold the Calloway fund directly into general operations. However, because the Secretarial Studies certificate was closed in 2011 and is discontinued ([documents/program-status.md]), the original restriction has become impossible to achieve. We can legally modify the restriction to repurpose the fund, provided the new purpose is consistent with the donor's original charitable intent ([documents/upmifa-summary.md]). 

**Route, steps, and timeline:**
* **Route:** Mrs. Calloway passed away in 2002 and left no successor to consent to a change ([documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]). However, because the fund has a balance of $18,420 (less than $25,000) and was established in May 1994 (more than 20 years ago) ([documents/fund-statements.md]), it qualifies for administrative modification under UPMIFA § 6(d) for "Small, old funds" ([documents/upmifa-summary.md]). 
* **Steps:** The statute requires us to notify the Attorney General 60 days before releasing or modifying the restriction. Because Mrs. Calloway originally intended her gift to assist "a young person entering office work" ([documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]), we must redirect the fund to a similar purpose—such as the active Office Administration (A.A.S.) program, which prepares students for administrative and office careers ([documents/program-status.md]). Modifying the restriction into a general operating fund would not be consistent with her expressed charitable intent. 
* **Dates:** You may authorize our outside counsel to send the required notice to the Attorney General before the board meets ([documents/endowment-policy.md] §4.3). If counsel sends the notice on Monday, October 5, 2026 ([documents/ed-email.md]), the 60-day notice period will expire on Friday, December 4, 2026. After that date, the Board of Directors may officially approve the modification. 

### 3. Alternative option to close the deficit
To address the $180,000 deficit immediately, the Board can utilize the Foundation's Operating Reserve. 

* **Available Funds:** The Operating Reserve currently has a balance of $240,000 ([documents/fund-statements.md]). 
* **Steps and timeline:** This reserve is a board-designated (unrestricted) fund. Under Section 5.1 of our Endowment Policy, the Board may change or remove its designation and use the money for any Foundation purpose by a majority vote of the full Board ([documents/endowment-policy.md]). Because this fund is not restricted by a donor gift instrument, UPMIFA § 6 does not apply; therefore, no donor consent or Attorney General notice is required ([documents/upmifa-summary.md], [documents/endowment-policy.md]). The board can simply hold a majority vote to release $180,000 from the reserve at the October 21 meeting to close the gap.

Dear Peggy,

Thank you so much for the generous $15,000 grant from the Halvorsen Family Fund at Kestrel [documents/halvorsen-email.md] [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. Regarding your Starlight Gala table: Federal law prohibits Donor-Advised Funds from paying for any part of an event table or ticket, and we cannot split the price between a DAF grant and a personal payment [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md]. Kestrel's grant conditions also explicitly forbid using grant funds for event tables [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. Because of these legal rules, we unfortunately cannot use $3,000 of your current grant for the Gala, nor could we accept a second, separate DAF grant for the table [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md] [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. 

To keep your wonderful table of 8 by the stage, we ask that you please pay the $3,000 invoice (INV-G26-0112) via a personal check or credit card [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md] [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md]. That payment will be due on October 2 so we can give the caterer our final numbers [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md]. 

I know you also requested a tax receipt for the full $15,000 for your tax preparer [documents/halvorsen-email.md]. Because Kestrel Charitable Trust has legal control of the fund's assets, they are the legal donor of record for the grant, and we are required to send the official tax acknowledgment directly to them [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md] [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. You will receive a warm thank-you letter from us shortly, but it cannot be used as a tax receipt because you already received your tax deduction when you contributed the funds to Kestrel [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md] [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md]. 

However, when you pay the $3,000 Gala invoice personally, we will gladly provide a tax receipt for that purchase! The fair market value for a table of 8 is $1,480 ($185 per guest), making $1,520 of your personal payment tax-deductible [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md] [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md]. 

We are so grateful for your continued dedication to the Studio Wing, and Erik and you are going to love the artists' showcase at the Gala [documents/halvorsen-email.md] [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md]. We can't wait to celebrate with you on October 17th!

Warmly,

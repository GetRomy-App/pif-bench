# Compliance Review Memo: Sierra Ladrones Community Foundation Proposal (v3)

**To:** Daniel Archuleta, Director of Development  
**Review of:** Draft v3, Sierra Ladrones Community Foundation 2027 Community Health Access Grants 

The following compliance issues in the current draft and submission plan will result in the application being returned without review or disqualified. Fixes and corrected figures are provided below.

### 1. Fatal Submission Time Error (Time Zone Mismatch)
* **Issue:** Lucia plans to submit the application from her hotel in Phoenix, Arizona at 4:30 p.m. on Thursday, October 1 [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md]. However, Phoenix is on Mountain Standard Time (MST) and does not observe daylight saving time [documents/summit-itinerary.md]. The foundation's deadline is strictly 5:00 p.m. Mountain Daylight Time (MDT) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Because MST is one hour behind MDT, Lucia's planned submission time of 4:30 p.m. in Phoenix is 5:30 p.m. MDT, which is past the deadline. The portal automatically closes at 5:00 p.m. MDT and will not accept late submissions [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Fix:** Lucia must submit the application before 4:00 p.m. Phoenix time (MST), which equals 5:00 p.m. MDT. Because she presents in a breakout session from 1:30 to 4:15 p.m. Phoenix time on October 1 [documents/summit-itinerary.md], she must submit the proposal *before* her afternoon sessions begin. 

### 2. Disqualified Match Funds
* **Issue:** The proposal lists a match of $51,000 [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md], but two of the four match sources are ineligible under the RFP:
  1. **Rio Grande Medical Supply ($12,000):** This is an in-kind donation [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. The RFP explicitly states that "in-kind contributions may be described in the budget narrative but do not count toward the match" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  2. **Socorro Rotary Club ($5,000):** The Rotary board does not vote until October 14, and the letter will not be sent until then [documents/attachments-folder.md; documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md]. The RFP states that any match "not documented by a letter at the deadline will not be counted" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Corrected Figures:** The only valid cash match funds are the Valencia County Health Council ($20,000) and our board-designated reserve ($14,000) [documents/attachments-folder.md; documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. **The true valid match total is $34,000.**
* **Fix:** Remove Rio Grande Medical Supply and the Socorro Rotary Club from the Match table in the proposal (Rio Grande's donation may remain in the budget narrative as an in-kind description).

### 3. Request Exceeds Hard Cap and Match Constraints
* **Issue:** The draft requests $83,847 [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Lucia notes a program officer mentioned "flexibility" [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md], but the RFP strictly states: "Requests above $75,000 will not be considered" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Furthermore, applicants must document a cash match of at least $1 for every $2 requested (50% of the request) [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. Because our valid cash match is only $34,000 (see Issue #2), requesting the maximum $75,000 is no longer possible, as that would require a $37,500 match.
* **Corrected Figures:** Based on our valid $34,000 match, **the maximum allowable request is $68,000** ($34,000 × 2). 
* **Fix:** Reduce the total request to no more than $68,000. All associated fields (Application Cover Sheet, Budget Narrative, and Budget Table) must be updated to reflect this new total. 

### 4. Incorrect Indirect Cost Rate
* **Issue:** The draft budget applies an indirect cost rate of 15% ($10,937 on $72,910 in direct costs) because this is our federal de minimis rate [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md; documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. The RFP prohibits this, dictating that indirect costs "may not exceed 10% of the total direct costs in the request" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
* **Corrected Figures:** To maximize the $68,000 allowable request using the mandated 10% indirect rate, **total direct costs cannot exceed $61,818.18, and indirect costs cannot exceed $6,181.82.** 
* **Fix:** Revise the budget narrative and budget table to utilize a maximum 10% indirect cost rate and scale down direct costs (Personnel, Travel, Supplies, etc.) to fit the $61,818.18 limit. 

### 5. Missing Mandatory Narrative Heading
* **Issue:** The RFP requires the narrative to contain exactly five sections, in a specific order, under specific headings. If a required section is omitted, the application "will be returned without review" [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. The draft proposal only contains four headings. The required 5th section, "Sustainability Plan", is missing, and the sustainability information is instead tacked onto the end of "4. Evaluation Plan" [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
* **Fix:** Pull the final sentence regarding Medicaid managed-care reimbursement out of Section 4 and place it under a new, distinct heading titled **"5. Sustainability Plan"**.

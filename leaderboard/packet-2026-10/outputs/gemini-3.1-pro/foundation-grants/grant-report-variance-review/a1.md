Based on the grant agreement targets (`[documents/grant-agreement.md]`) and the Year 1 outcomes data (`[documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]`), here is the status of our deliverables. Please note that Marcus’s talking points contain miscalculations for Deliverables 2, 3, and 5 due to misinterpreting the agreement's definitions.

    *   **Status: Met.**

    *   **Status: Not Met.**
*   **D3: Learners completing 40+ hours who gain 1+ EFL on a standardized post-test**

    *   **Actual:** 61 learners (`[documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]`).
    *   **Status: Met.**
*   **D4: Volunteer tutors recruited who complete at least 12 hours of pre-service training**
    *   **Target:** 25 (`[documents/grant-agreement.md]`).
    *   **Actual:** 26 (`[documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]`).
    *   **Status: Met.**
*   **D5: Employer Advisory Council meetings**
    *   **Target:** At least one meeting in each quarter of the year (`[documents/grant-agreement.md]`).
    *   **Actual:** Meetings were held in Q1, Q2, and Q3, but the Q4 meeting was postponed to July 16, 2026 (`[documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]`). Because Year 1 ended June 30, 2026 (`[documents/grant-agreement.md]`), a July 16 meeting falls outside the Year 1 grant period. (Marcus incorrectly notes this "still counts for Q4", `[documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]`).

# Foundation Fit Assessment — Fresh Routes

Prepared for the Development Committee (Meeting: 2026-09-24)

## Priority 1: Garrison Ridge Foundation
*Category: Accepts requests and fits on geography and type of support for at least one project component [documents/grants-policy.md].*

**Grant Data & Fit**
*   **Total Grants Paid:** The foundation paid 13 grants during the most recent year totaling $480,000 [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Pennsylvania Grants:** 12 of the 13 paid grants went to Pennsylvania organizations [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Mission Fit:** Yes. The foundation funds "Basic needs — food access and nutrition" [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md].
*   **Geographic Fit:** Yes. Lancaster County is explicitly listed in their giving areas [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md], and they paid 12 grants to PA organizations [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Type of Support Fit:** Program support only. The published guidelines do not expressly invite capital support, stating they "support programs and the organizations that run them" [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. The 990-PF grants-paid list contains no explicit capital requests (e.g., vehicles, equipment, buildings) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Relationships:** No Millstream board or staff members serve as officers, directors, or trustees of this foundation [documents/board-roster.md, documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. 

**Application Process & Deadlines**
*   **How to Apply:** Organizations submit a letter of inquiry (LOI) of no more than two pages via an online portal [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. *Note on discrepancy:* The 990-PF states LOIs are accepted on a rolling basis [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md], but the website indicates that as of January 2026, they use two specific cycles [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Per policy [documents/grants-policy.md], we follow the website. 
*   **Next Deadline:** The next deadline after our assessment date (2026-09-15) is March 1, 2027 (the Spring cycle LOI deadline) [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. 

**Recommended Approach & Ask Range**
Submit an online LOI for program support for the Fresh Routes southern-county mobile markets [documents/project-summary.md] for an ask range of **$15,000 to $30,000** [documents/grants-policy.md]. This range is based on seven comparable grants paid to PA organizations for food access/nutrition [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. The lowest comparable grant was $15,000, and the median of the seven was $30,000 [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. The $30,000 top does not exceed the remaining program need of $260,000 [documents/project-summary.md].

## Priority 2: Marwood Family Foundation
*Category: Reachable only through a board or staff relationship [documents/grants-policy.md].*

**Grant Data & Fit**
*   **Total Grants Paid:** The foundation paid 10 grants during the most recent year totaling $708,000 [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Pennsylvania Grants:** All 10 paid grants went to Pennsylvania organizations [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Mission Fit:** Yes. The foundation supports "feeding neighbors who are hungry" [documents/marwood-website.md].
*   **Geographic Fit:** Yes. Lancaster County is named in their guidelines [documents/marwood-website.md], and all 10 grants went to PA [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Type of Support Fit:** Both Capital and Program support. The website expressly notes grants help organizations "build, repair and equip" [documents/marwood-website.md], and the 990-PF lists several capital grants (e.g., freezer expansion, refrigerated van) alongside general operating support [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Relationships:** Millstream Board Director Margaret W. Lindqvist [documents/board-roster.md] serves as a Trustee of the Marwood Family Foundation [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. 

**Application Process & Deadlines**
*   **How to Apply:** The foundation checks the 990-PF box stating it "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds" [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. The website confirms they do not accept unsolicited proposals, LOIs, or applications [documents/marwood-website.md]. 
*   **Next Deadline:** The files do not indicate submission deadlines [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md, documents/marwood-website.md].

**Recommended Approach & Ask Range**
Because the foundation does not accept unsolicited requests, the board relationship is the only approach [documents/grants-policy.md]. Staff must prepare and send nothing unless a request is invited [documents/grants-policy.md]. Director Lindqvist may ask the Marwood trustees whether the foundation would welcome a conversation or invite a request from Millstream, but she must take no part in the foundation's decision [documents/grants-policy.md]. 

If invited to request funds, the ask range is **$25,000 to $54,000** [documents/grants-policy.md]. This is based on six comparable grants to PA organizations for hunger relief, ranging from $25,000 to $150,000 [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. The bottom of the range is the smallest comparable grant ($25,000), and the top is the median of the six grants ($54,000) [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md, documents/grants-policy.md]. The $54,000 top does not exceed the remaining capital or program needs for Fresh Routes [documents/project-summary.md].

## Priority 3: Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust
*Category: Not a fit [documents/grants-policy.md].*

**Grant Data & Fit**
*   **Total Grants Paid:** The trust paid 12 grants during the most recent year totaling $390,000 [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Pennsylvania Grants:** 0. None of the grants went to Pennsylvania organizations (the Lancaster County Hunger Coalition listed is in Lancaster, SC) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Mission Fit:** Yes. The trust makes grants to "relieve hunger and widen access to fresh, healthy food" [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].
*   **Geographic Fit:** No. The trust paid no grants to Pennsylvania organizations [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], and the published guidelines do not name Pennsylvania or Lancaster County among its giving areas [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. 
*   **Type of Support Fit:** Both Capital and Program support. The trust funds "programs, operations, equipment and facilities" [documents/calloway-deane-website.md], and the 990-PF shows grants for both (e.g., refrigerated truck purchase, operating support) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
*   **Relationships:** No Millstream board or staff members serve as foundation officers, directors, or trustees [documents/board-roster.md, documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Board member Dr. Owen M. Calloway suggested the trust [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md], but is not listed as having an official role with it [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].

**Application Process & Deadlines**
*   **How to Apply:** Online application via the trust's website [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. *Note on discrepancy:* The 990-PF states applications should be addressed to the Grants Administrator by mail in Philadelphia and submitted as an online application [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], but the website lists only the online application [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Per policy [documents/grants-policy.md], we follow the website. 
*   **Next Deadline:** The next deadline is October 15, 2026 [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. 

**Recommended Approach & Ask Range**
Because the trust is not a geographic fit, we will prepare no proposal or LOI [documents/grants-policy.md]. However, because the published guidelines state no explicit geographic limit [documents/calloway-deane-website.md], Director of Development Elena Ruiz-Hartman [documents/board-roster.md] may make a single inquiry by email or phone to ask whether the trust would consider a request from a Pennsylvania organization, and must record the answer [documents/grants-policy.md]. 

Because there are no comparable PA grants [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], we set no ask range [documents/grants-policy.md]. If the trust replies in writing that it is a geographic fit, the question of ask size must be referred to Executive Director Thomas Greider [documents/grants-policy.md, documents/board-roster.md].

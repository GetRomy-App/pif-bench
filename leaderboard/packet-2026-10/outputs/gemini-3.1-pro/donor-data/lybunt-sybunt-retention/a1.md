## Methodology and Definitions
Per the organization’s metrics definitions [documents/metrics-definitions.md], the following rules were applied to the gift history [documents/gift-history.csv]:
*   **Donor Entity:** Gifts are grouped by household (`household_id`). Organizations are excluded.
*   **Countable Gifts:** Only individual gifts with "Hard" credit and a "Posted" status are counted. Soft credits and "Refunded" gifts are excluded. Joint account gifts are counted once per household.
*   **Fiscal Years:** The fiscal year runs from July 1 to June 30.
    *   **FY2025:** July 1, 2024 – June 30, 2025
    *   **FY2026:** July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026
    *   **FY2027 (This Year):** July 1, 2026 – present

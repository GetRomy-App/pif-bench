**Ask Amount: $80,000**

In accordance with the Major Gift Solicitation Policy `[documents/solicitation-policy.md]`, the ask is calculated as follows:
*   **Base:** The prospect's five-year capacity estimate is $382,000 `[documents/research-profile.md]`.
*   **Multiplier:** The prospect's inclination score is 4 (strong) `[documents/research-profile.md]`, meaning the capacity is multiplied by a factor of 0.25 `[documents/solicitation-policy.md]`. ($382,000 x 0.25 = $95,500).
*   **Rounding:** Rounded to the nearest $5,000, the preliminary ask is $95,000 `[documents/solicitation-policy.md]`.
*   **Cap:** The ask cannot exceed four times the household's largest single eligible gift in the last five years `[documents/solicitation-policy.md]`. 
    *   The largest eligible household gift (excluding corporate sponsorships and event tickets) was a $20,000 grant from their donor-advised fund on 2025-10-14 `[documents/crm-record.md]`. 
    *   The cap is therefore $80,000 ($20,000 x 4) `[documents/solicitation-policy.md]`. 
    *   Because the preliminary ask ($95,000) exceeds the cap, the final ask is capped at $80,000 `[documents/solicitation-policy.md]`. *(Note: While Teresa Lindahl's contact report suggests a $150,000 ask `[documents/contact-reports.md]`, policy restricts the ask to $80,000).*

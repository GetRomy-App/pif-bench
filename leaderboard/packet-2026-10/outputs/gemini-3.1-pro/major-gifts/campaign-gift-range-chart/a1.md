# Sound Foundations Campaign — Gift Range Chart

The following chart and analysis are built according to the methodology defined by campaign counsel Larch & Whitby [documents/counsel-chart-method.md] to reach the board-approved goal of $10,000,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. 

### Chart Methodology & Figures
*   **Tiers:** The lead gift is set at 20% of the goal ($2,000,000), with each subsequent tier halving the gift amount and doubling the number of gifts until dropping below $250,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. The "All other gifts" line captures the $2,000,000 remainder to reach the $10,000,000 cumulative total [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. 
*   **Gifts in Hand:** The Hollis Family Foundation's signed pledge of $1,000,000 counts as one gift in hand at the $1,000,000 tier [documents/campaign-commitments.md; documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. Ruth and Anders Lindqvist's signed pledge of $300,000 counts as one gift in hand at the $250,000 tier, which is the highest named tier it meets or exceeds [documents/campaign-commitments.md; documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. (Committed gifts from Judith Albers and the Trustees total $236,500 but fall below $250,000, counting toward "All other gifts" [documents/campaign-commitments.md; documents/counsel-chart-method.md].)
*   **Prospects Needed:** Calculated as gifts still needed multiplied by 3 for tiers of $1,000,000 or more, and multiplied by 4 for tiers below $1,000,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].

# Review of Spring Gala Vendor Conflicts of Interest

This memo reviews the Theatre's transactions with two Spring Gala vendors against the Conflict of Interest Policy and Form 990, Schedule L reporting requirements. 

## 1. BrightStage AV Services LLC

**Conflict of Interest Analysis & Policy Compliance:** 
Director Priya Ramaswamy's brother, Arun Ramaswamy, owns 80% of BrightStage AV Services LLC [documents/state-business-registry.md, documents/annual-statements-2026.md]. Because a brother is defined as a "Family Member" under Article II, Section 3 of the Conflict of Interest Policy, Director Ramaswamy had a Financial Interest in this transaction [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. 

The policy required Director Ramaswamy to disclose this interest in writing prior to the meeting, disclose it orally at the meeting, and recuse herself from the vote [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. She fully complied: she disclosed the interest on her 2026 Annual Conflict-of-Interest Statement [documents/annual-statements-2026.md], notified the Board Chair via email on February 2 [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md], and disclosed it orally at the February 12 board meeting [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. She then left the room, took no part in the vote, and the disinterested directors voted 10–0 to award the contract after reviewing three competitive bids [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. 

**Schedule L Reporting:**
BrightStage meets the definition of an "interested person" for Form 990, Schedule L, Part IV because it is more than 35% owned by the brother of a director [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]. However, it does **not** need to be reported on Schedule L because the payments do not meet the reporting threshold. To be reportable, payments from a single transaction must exceed the greater of $10,000 or 1% of the organization's total revenue [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]. The Theatre's total draft revenue for the 2025 Form 990 is $3,940,000, making the 1% threshold $39,400 [documents/controller-email.md]. The Theatre paid BrightStage a total of $18,250 in the fiscal year, falling well below the $39,400 threshold [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md].

## 2. Saffron & Sage Events LLC

**Conflict of Interest Analysis & Policy Compliance:** 
Director Thomas Aldana's spouse, Celeste M. Aldana, is the sole member (100% owner) of Saffron & Sage Events LLC, sharing a primary address with Director Aldana [documents/annual-statements-2026.md, documents/state-business-registry.md, documents/board-roster-2025-26.md]. A spouse is a "Family Member" under Article II, Section 3, meaning Director Aldana had a clear Financial Interest in the catering contract [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

The policy was **not followed**. Director Aldana failed to disclose this interest. He answered "No" to Question 2 on his annual statement [documents/annual-statements-2026.md], and made no disclosure when the Board Chair specifically asked for them at the February 12 meeting [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. Instead of recusing himself as required by Article III, Section 3, Director Aldana actively advocated for Saffron & Sage, arguing their food was "a cut above," and made the motion to award them the $68,400 contract, which passed 6–5 with his vote [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. 

**Schedule L Reporting:**
Saffron & Sage Events LLC meets the definition of an "interested person" because it is more than 35% owned by a director's spouse [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]. This transaction **must be reported** on Schedule L. The Theatre paid Saffron & Sage a total of $71,860 during the fiscal year for the gala contract [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md]. This exceeds the reporting threshold of $39,400 (1% of the Theatre's total revenue) [documents/controller-email.md, documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

## Next Steps Required by the Policy

Because the policy was violated regarding the Saffron & Sage transaction, Article IV requires the following immediate actions [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]:

1. **Notice and Opportunity to Explain:** The Governance Committee must inform Director Aldana in writing of its reasonable cause to believe he failed to disclose the conflict. He must be given an opportunity to explain the failure to disclose (either in writing or at a committee meeting) within 15 days of the notice.
2. **Committee Recusal:** Director Aldana currently serves on the Governance Committee [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md]. Under Article IV, Section 4, he must take no part in the Committee's or the Board's deliberations or votes concerning his conduct.
3. **Board Reporting and Action:** After hearing his response and conducting any necessary investigation, the Governance Committee must report its findings to the Board. If a majority of disinterested directors determine he failed to disclose the conflict, they must take disciplinary or corrective action (which may include a reprimand, removal from positions, a request for resignation, or removal from the board). 
4. **Re-review of the Transaction:** Because the transaction was approved without disclosure or recusal, the disinterested directors must re-review the contract under Article III, Sections 3(c) and 3(d). If this re-review determines the Theatre paid more than fair market value—noting Saffron & Sage's base bid was $15,500 higher than the lowest bid [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]—the Board must consult legal counsel regarding whether the payment constitutes an excess benefit transaction under IRS Code Section 4958 and regarding the recovery of the excess funds.
5. **Corrected Statement:** Director Aldana must file a corrected annual conflict-of-interest statement within 30 days of the Board's determination.

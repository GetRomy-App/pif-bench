**SUBJECT:** Fiscal 2025 Financial Health Indicators

Per the Financial Health Indicators Policy [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md], this memo calculates the three financial health indicators for Ashby Creek Regional Food Bank for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. All calculations are based on the 2025 audited financial statement notes [documents/audit-notes-2025.md] and the 2025 Form 990 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].

### 1. Program Expense Ratio
**Formula:** (Program service expenses − gifts in kind expensed in program services) ÷ (Total functional expenses − all gifts in kind expensed) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

**Inputs & Sources:**
*   **Program service expenses:** $19,066,000 (Form 990 Part IX, line 25, column (B)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **Gifts in kind expensed in program services:** $14,180,000 for donated food distributed (Audit Note 9 indicates this was expensed in program services via Part IX, line 24a) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
*   **Total functional expenses:** $20,515,000 (Form 990 Part IX, line 25, column (A)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **All gifts in kind expensed:** $14,275,000. This consists of $14,180,000 in donated food distributed and $95,000 in donated legal services (Audit Note 9 indicates legal services were expensed in Management and general via Part IX, line 11b) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].

**Calculation:**
*   Adjusted Program Service Expenses: $19,066,000 − $14,180,000 = $4,886,000
*   Adjusted Total Functional Expenses: $20,515,000 − $14,275,000 = $6,240,000
*   Ratio: $4,886,000 ÷ $6,240,000 = 78.3% (rounded to one decimal place) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

**Result vs. Target:**
*   **Target:** 80.0% or higher [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]
*   **Actual:** 78.3%
*   **Status:** **Target Not Met**

### 2. Cost to Raise a Dollar
**Formula:** Fundraising expenses ÷ Contributions [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

**Inputs & Sources:**
*   **Fundraising expenses:** $866,300 (Form 990 Part IX, line 25, column (D)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. There are no gifts in kind expensed in fundraising (Audit Note 9 lists only program services and management/general allocations) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
*   **Total Contributions:** $18,930,000 (Form 990 Part VIII, line 1h) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **Less noncash contributions:** $14,345,000 (Form 990 Part VIII, line 1g) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **Less bequests:** $600,000 (Audit Note 10 reports a $600,000 distribution from the Estate of Harriet M. Okafor included in contributions; no other bequests were received) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].

**Calculation:**
*   Adjusted Contributions: $18,930,000 − $14,345,000 − $600,000 = $3,985,000
*   Ratio: $866,300 ÷ $3,985,000 = $0.22 (rounded to nearest cent) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

**Result vs. Target:**
*   **Target:** $0.20 or less [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]
*   **Actual:** $0.22
*   **Status:** **Target Not Met** *(Note: The cost did not exceed $0.25, so an external review of fundraising costs is not automatically triggered [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md])*

### 3. Months of Operating Reserve
**Formula:** Operating reserve ÷ (Annual operating expenses ÷ 12) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

**Inputs & Sources:**
*   **Net assets without donor restrictions:** $6,612,000 (Form 990 Part X, line 27, column (B)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **Land, buildings and equipment, net:** $5,108,000 (Form 990 Part X, line 10c, column (B)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **Total functional expenses:** $20,515,000 (Form 990 Part IX, line 25, column (A)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **Depreciation:** $446,000 (Form 990 Part IX, line 22, column (A)) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
*   **All gifts in kind expensed:** $14,275,000 (Audit Note 9) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].

**Calculation:**
*   Operating Reserve: $6,612,000 − $5,108,000 = $1,504,000
*   Adjusted Annual Operating Expenses: $20,515,000 − $446,000 − $14,275,000 = $5,794,000
*   Monthly Operating Expenses: $5,794,000 ÷ 12 = $482,833.33
*   Months of Reserve: $1,504,000 ÷ $482,833.33 = 3.1 months (rounded to one decimal place) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]

**Result vs. Target:**
*   **Target:** 3.0 months or more [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]
*   **Actual:** 3.1 months
*   **Status:** **Target Met**

## 1. Garrison Ridge Foundation — Tier 1

Sources: [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md] (Form 990-PF, tax year 2025, filed 2026-05-11) and [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md] (captured 2026-09-11).

**990-PF vs. website (Policy §1).** The 990-PF reports that letters of inquiry are accepted "on a rolling basis," but the website says that **since January 2026 LOIs are reviewed in two cycles a year** (spring cycle LOI due March 1, fall cycle LOI due September 1). The website is more current; we follow the website and note the difference.

**How and when it accepts requests.** Two-page LOI through the online portal; full proposals only by invitation [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. The fall 2026 LOI deadline (2026-09-01) has already passed; the **next LOI deadline after today (2026-09-15) is March 1, 2027**, with invited proposals due mid-April and decisions in June 2027.

**Grants paid in 2025 (990-PF).** Total paid: $60,000 + $25,000 + $40,000 + $20,000 + $15,000 + $35,000 + $30,000 + $75,000 + $50,000 + $25,000 + $45,000 + $40,000 + $20,000 = **$480,000 across 13 grants**. **12 of 13** grants are to Pennsylvania organizations (the exception is Mason-Dixon Food Share, Westminster, MD). The grants approved for future payment (Lebanon Valley Food Hub, $60,000; Harrisburg Youth Mentoring Collaborative, $75,000) do not count toward comparable-grant analysis under Policy §6.

**Mission fit — strong.** The website's "What we fund" lists "Basic needs — food access and nutrition" first [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. 2025 grants include several direct models for the Fresh Routes *program* component: a **mobile-pantry operations** grant (Lebanon Valley Food Hub, $60,000), a **mobile market for fresh produce** (Southern Lancaster County Food Network, Quarryville, $35,000), summer meals for children (Hanover Area Food Share, $20,000), SNAP outreach (Upper Dauphin Food Pantry, $15,000) and nutrition education (Gettysburg Area Nutrition Program, $30,000) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].

**Geographic fit (Policy §4) — yes.** Twelve of 13 grants paid are to Pennsylvania organizations, and the website explicitly names **Adams, Cumberland, Dauphin, Lancaster, Lebanon, Perry and York counties** as the Foundation's giving area — including Millstream's Lancaster County [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md, documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].

**Type of support (Policy §5).**
- **Program — yes.** Multiple 2025 grants support program and operating costs of food-related organizations (mobile pantry, mobile market, summer meals, SNAP outreach, nutrition education, general operating support).
- **Capital — no.** No grant on the 990-PF grants-paid list is for land, buildings, construction, renovation, equipment or a vehicle, and the website's "What we fund" section does not expressly invite capital support.
- Conclusion: **Type fit for the program component of Fresh Routes only.**

**Board/staff relationship (Policy §3) — none.** The Foundation's officers and directors (Okafor, Fenstermacher, Rudisill, Desai-Moreno, Albright) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md] do not overlap with the Millstream board or staff [documents/board-roster.md].

**Ask range (Policy §6) — program component only.** Comparable grants: 2025 grants paid to a Pennsylvania organization whose work is food access, hunger relief or nutrition (purpose of grant does not matter):

| Recipient | City, PA | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Upper Dauphin Food Pantry | Millersburg | $15,000 |
| Hanover Area Food Share | Hanover | $20,000 |
| Carlisle Community Meals | Carlisle | $25,000 |
| Gettysburg Area Nutrition Program | Gettysburg | $30,000 |
| Southern Lancaster County Food Network | Quarryville | $35,000 |
| York Fresh Start Pantry | York | $40,000 |
| Lebanon Valley Food Hub | Lebanon | $60,000 |

Seven comparable grants. **Bottom: $15,000.** Median (odd count, 4th of 7 sorted ascending): **$30,000**. Remaining program-component need is $260,000 [documents/project-summary.md]; the median sits well below that cap. **Ask range: $15,000 – $30,000.** No ask range is set for the capital component, because the Foundation is not a Type fit for capital.

**Recommended approach.** Prepare and submit a two-page LOI through Garrison Ridge's online portal for the **Spring 2027 cycle (LOI due March 1, 2027)**, requesting support for Fresh Routes' **program component** (mobile-market coordinator and driver/market assistant; fuel, maintenance and insurance; outreach and SNAP-enrollment materials — line items totaling $260,000) at **$15,000 – $30,000** [documents/project-summary.md]. If invited, prepare the full proposal for the mid-April 2027 deadline; expect a decision in June 2027.

## 2. Marwood Family Foundation — Tier 2

Sources: [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] (Form 990-PF, tax year 2025, filed 2026-05-08) and [documents/marwood-website.md] (captured 2026-09-11).

**990-PF vs. website (Policy §1) — aligned.** The 990-PF **checks** the box stating the Foundation "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds." The website states: "The Marwood Family Foundation does not accept unsolicited proposals, letters of inquiry or applications. Our trustees identify the organizations the Foundation supports." Policy §2 applies: Millstream sends no unsolicited proposal, LOI, application or other written request.

**How and when it accepts requests.** It does not, by either medium. No deadline [documents/marwood-website.md, documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].

**Grants paid in 2025 (990-PF).** $85,000 + $60,000 + $150,000 + $25,000 + $48,000 + $100,000 + $35,000 + $120,000 + $40,000 + $45,000 = **$708,000 across 10 grants**. **10 of 10** are to Pennsylvania organizations (the $96,400 reported in operating/administrative expenses reconciles with the $804,400 total expenses and disbursements from the 990-PF) [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].

**Mission fit — strong.** "Feeding neighbors who are hungry" is the first line on "What we support" [documents/marwood-website.md]. The 2025 grants list reads like a catalogue of Fresh Routes-style activity:

- Walk-in freezer and cold-storage expansion — Ephrata Area Community Pantry, $85,000
- Purchase of a refrigerated van — Elizabethtown Neighbors Food Shelf, $48,000
- Purchase of a building for a soup kitchen and pantry — Columbia Hope Kitchen, $150,000
- Commercial kitchen renovation — Lititz Meals on Wheels, $60,000
- Loading dock and warehouse shelving — Mount Joy Area Pantry, $45,000
- General operating support — Manheim Community Food Cupboard, $25,000
[documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md, documents/project-summary.md]

**Geographic fit (Policy §4) — yes.** All 10 of 10 grants are to Pennsylvania organizations, and the website names "**Lancaster County**" as the Foundation's giving area [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md, documents/marwood-website.md].

**Type of support (Policy §5) — both.**
- **Capital — yes** (freezer/cold-storage, refrigerated van, building, kitchen renovation, dock/shelving).
- **Program — yes** (general operating support).

**Board/staff relationship (Policy §3) — yes.** **Margaret W. Lindqvist** — Director on the Millstream board, Lititz, PA [documents/board-roster.md] — is listed on the Marwood Form 990-PF as a **Trustee, 1.0 hours per week, $0 compensation** [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. The relationship is named here at the policy's instruction. Per Policy §3.2, this relationship is the **only** approach available to Millstream. Per Policy §3.3, Ms. Lindqvist takes no part in the foundation's decision on any Millstream request. (Ms. Lindqvist was absent from the 2026-09-10 board meeting [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]; her board seat and Marwood trusteeship are unchanged.)

**Ask range (Policy §6).** Comparable grants: 2025 grants paid to a Pennsylvania organization whose work is food access, hunger relief or nutrition:

| Recipient | City, PA | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Manheim Community Food Cupboard | Manheim | $25,000 |
| Mount Joy Area Pantry | Mount Joy | $45,000 |
| Elizabethtown Neighbors Food Shelf | Elizabethtown | $48,000 |
| Lititz Meals on Wheels | Lititz | $60,000 |
| Ephrata Area Community Pantry | Ephrata | $85,000 |
| Columbia Hope Kitchen | Columbia | $150,000 |

Six comparable grants. **Bottom: $25,000.** Median (even count; average of the two middle after sorting — $48,000 and $60,000): **$54,000**. Remaining project need (total) is $750,000 [documents/project-summary.md]; the median sits well below that cap, so the cap is not binding. **Ask range: $25,000 – $54,000**, presented only if the foundation invites a written request.

**Recommended approach.** Per Policy §2 and §3.2, **staff prepare and send nothing to Marwood.** Per Policy §3.2, Ms. Lindqvist may ask the other Marwood trustees — by whatever informal channel she ordinarily uses — whether the Foundation would welcome a conversation with Millstream or would invite a written request. The Development Committee at the 2026-09-24 meeting should authorize that step. If Marwood invites a request in writing, prepare a single proposal (capital or program component, at our choice) at $25,000 – $54,000. If no invitation, make no further approach. Throughout, Ms. Lindqvist does not participate in Marwood's decision-making on this request (Policy §3.3).

## 3. Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust — Tier 3

Sources: [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] (Form 990-PF, tax year 2025, filed 2026-05-14) and [documents/calloway-deane-website.md] (captured 2026-09-11).

**990-PF vs. website (Policy §1) — aligned.** The 990-PF does **not** check the "no unsolicited requests" box; the website confirms an online application with two deadlines a year [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md, documents/calloway-deane-website.md].

**How and when it accepts requests.** Online application at the trust's website; deadlines **April 15** (decisions June) and **October 15** (decisions December). Grants typically $10,000 – $90,000; applicants must be 501(c)(3) public charities; no grants to individuals [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. The next deadline after today (2026-09-15) is **October 15, 2026** (decisions in December 2026).

**Grants paid in 2025 (990-PF).** $90,000 + $20,000 + $35,000 + $25,000 + $15,000 + $30,000 + $40,000 + $65,000 + $20,000 + $25,000 + $10,000 + $15,000 = **$390,000 across 12 grants**. **0 of 12** are to Pennsylvania organizations — all are in North Carolina, South Carolina or Virginia. (The $98,450 reported in operating/administrative expenses reconciles with the $488,450 total expenses and disbursements on the 990-PF.) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]

**Mission fit — strong.** The trust's stated purpose is "nonprofit organizations that relieve hunger and widen access to fresh, healthy food — food banks and pantries, meal programs, gleaning and food-rescue groups, community gardens and mobile markets" [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. 2025 grants include specifically relevant precedents: a refrigerated warehouse expansion ($90,000 to Piedmont Fresh Food Alliance, Greensboro, NC) and purchase of a refrigerated truck ($65,000 to Tidewater Food Rescue, Norfolk, VA) — direct parallels to the Fresh Routes capital component [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md, documents/project-summary.md].

**Geographic fit (Policy §4) — no under the standard test.** Zero of 12 grants paid are to Pennsylvania organizations, and neither the website's purpose section, the website's application instructions, nor the 990-PF restrictions block name Pennsylvania as part of the trust's giving area [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md, documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. The trust's history ties it to "the Carolinas and Virginia." The Foundation's own Philadelphia mailing address is not, by itself, evidence of fit (Policy §4). A standard fit cannot be made.

However, the 990-PF and the website also do **not** state an explicit geographic limit (the 990-PF lists only "grants only to organizations exempt under section 501(c)(3); no grants to individuals"). Policy §4 then permits a single inquiry — by email or telephone — asking whether the trust would consider a request from a Pennsylvania-headquartered food bank. **No proposal, LOI or other written request from Millstream is prepared until the inquiry is answered in writing.**

**Type of support (Policy §5) — both, contingent on geography.**
- **Capital — yes.** Refrigerated warehouse expansion ($90,000) and refrigerated truck purchase ($65,000).
- **Program — yes.** Mobile food pantry, mobile farmers market, gleaning, home-delivered meals, backpack meals, general operating support, meal program, etc.
- Should the geographic inquiry return a written "yes," Calloway-Deane would be a Type fit for both components, and the **capital precedent** is unusually strong for a cold-storage-and-truck project of this size.

**Board/staff relationship (Policy §3) — none.** Officers, directors, trustees and foundation managers are Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company (Corporate Trustee, Philadelphia), Harriet Deane Pruitt (Charlotte, NC) and Samuel R. Deane (Richmond, VA) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. None is on the Millstream board or staff [documents/board-roster.md]. (The trust was established under the will of Thomas A. Calloway (1921–2004). Dr. Owen M. Calloway, who sits on the Millstream board and named the trust at the 2026-09-10 meeting [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md, documents/calloway-deane-website.md], is not a trustee, director, officer or foundation manager of Calloway-Deane per the 990-PF; there is therefore no Policy §3 relationship.)

**Ask range (Policy §6) — not set.** Until a written "yes" comes back, no PA-recipient comparable grants exist; the comparable set is empty and no ask range can be drawn. If the inquiry is answered "yes," the ask range is recomputed from that point forward.

**Recommended approach.** The Director of Development sends a **single email** to grants@callowaydeanetrust.org within one week [documents/calloway-deane-website.md], asking one question only: "Would the Trust consider a grant request from a food bank headquartered in Pennsylvania?" If no response within ten business days, follow up by telephone to the grants administrator at (215) 555-0143 [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. **Record the answer in writing.** Only on a written "yes" do we proceed — and only then with a follow-up recommendation to the Development Committee before any application is prepared for the **October 15, 2026** cycle or the April 15, 2027 cycle.

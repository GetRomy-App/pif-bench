### Note A — D2 ("At least 75% of enrolled learners")

The literal text is "at least 75% of **enrolled** learners." With actual enrollment of 131 [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md], the strict denominator is 131; 75% × 131 = 98.25, which requires at least 99 completers. We have 94, which is short under that reading. Marcus's talking points use 75% of the D1 *target* (120), giving 90 as the implied numeric benchmark — under that more favorable reading 94 ≥ 90 and D2 is met [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. Recommend confirming with Foundation program officer Gwen Halloran which denominator the Foundation intends; the number is close enough that a clarifying email should resolve it before Marcus finalizes the report.

### Note B — D5

Three meetings were held in the right Year 1 quarters (Sep 18, Dec 11, Mar 19) [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. The fourth (originally June 11, 2026) was postponed because two employer members were unavailable and was rescheduled to **July 16, 2026** — outside the Year 1 period that ended June 30, 2026 [documents/grant-agreement.md, documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. As of today the meeting has not been held. Per Section 3 ("A deliverable is met only if the result for the year reaches the target as defined above"), D5 is not yet demonstrably met. Marcus's framing in his talking points that "it still counts for Q4" is a position the Foundation needs to accept in writing for the report to show D5 as met.

The report's calculation for D3 in Marcus's note also uses a different denominator (61 of 73 post-tested = 83.6%) than the agreement's text (60% of *completers*, 94). On the agreement's denominator the result is 61/94 = 64.9%, which still clears 60% [documents/grant-agreement.md, documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]. Either reading supports "Met" but the agreement-language number should be the one in the report.

### Two lines exceed the 10% variance threshold

- **Learner supports — over by 13.0%** (driven by higher-than-projected bus passes and childcare stipends because enrollment came in ahead of plan [documents/year1-outcomes-from-marcus.md]). Section 5 requires the Foundation's prior written approval for any variance greater than 10% above or below a line's approved amount. None was requested or received this year [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md].
- **Curriculum & materials — under by 13.5%** (spent $6,920 against $8,000 approved). Same Section 5 approval applies, and the under-run still must be acknowledged before funds can carry forward cleanly under Section 9.

Without retroactive Section 5 approvals in writing, the interim report cannot supply the "copies of any written approvals under Section 5" that Section 7.3 requires.

### One unallowable cost: $640 of alcohol

Line item **AP-2242, Harborview Taproom, "beer and wine service" at the spring tutor-training wrap-up, $640, posted 2026-05-29** [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]. **Section 6.3** expressly prohibits use of grant funds for alcoholic beverages [documents/grant-agreement.md]. **Section 6.4** requires this cost to be removed from the grant's accounts and paid from the Grantee's own unrestricted funds. This is a required accounting move, not optional.

After reclassifying the $640 to unrestricted funds, Tutor training's allowable expenditures are $4,700 against $5,000 approved (−6.0%, within the 10% tolerance), and total allowable Year 1 spending becomes $118,666.

The other tutor-training line items — AP-2025 (coffee, juice and snacks, $260) and AP-2241 (dinner buffet, 34 guests, $1,020) — appear on their face to be allowable food and non-alcoholic refreshment under Section 6.2, but Marcus should confirm that the Hearthside dinner buffet contained no alcohol before Tony signs off.

## 1. Request exceeds the $75,000 cap [RFP §3]

> "Requests may not exceed $75,000. Requests above $75,000 will not be considered."

- Draft v3 request: **$83,847**
- Cap: **$75,000**

**Fix.** Reshape to the cap or below. Combined with Issue #2 (indirect cap), the cleanest defensible numbers are:

- Total direct costs: ≤ **$68,182**
- Indirect (10% of direct): ≤ **$6,818**
- **Total request: ≤ $75,000**

This is a **$3,728 cut to direct costs** from the line-item base of $71,910. Candidate cuts: mileage miles from 12,000 to about 5,500 (–$2,925); interpretation hours from 60 to about 30 (–$750); modest trim to screening supplies.

The "RFP allows some flexibility" framing from the August info session cannot be relied on; the written cap is what the staff screen runs on. RFP §3 is explicit.

## 2. Indirect costs exceed 10% of direct [RFP §4]

> "Indirect costs may not exceed 10% of the total direct costs in the request."

- Draft v3 indirect: **15%**, $10,937
- Cap: **10%**

**Fix.** Recalculate indirect at 10% of the (revised) direct base. The note in the cover note that "this is our federal de minimis rate, which we use on all our grants" is true but is overridden by the funder's cap; the application cannot lead with the 15% justification [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md]. New indirect figure: **$6,818** on a $68,182 direct base (yielding the $75,000 total in Issue #1), or less if direct is cut further.

- Statement of Need: 585 → ~500.

Approximate revised total: **2,460**. Final pass under Word's count must come in under 2,500 after the new section is written.

## 4. Sustainability Plan section is missing [RFP §5]

> "The narrative must contain the following sections, in this order and under these headings: … 5. Sustainability Plan — how the work will continue after the grant period, including the sources of future funding. … Applications that exceed the word limit **or omit a required section** will be returned without review." [emphasis added]

Draft v3 has only four narrative sections. This omission **alone** is a return-without-review trigger.

**Fix.** Add Section 5, titled **Sustainability Plan**, at the end of the narrative before the budget-narrative portal field. Move the following sentence out of Evaluation Plan, where it currently lives, and put it in Section 5:
- "After 2027, we expect to sustain the CHW positions through Medicaid managed-care reimbursement for CHW services." [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]

Strengthen it: name the specific NM Medicaid pathway (e.g., Centennial Care 2.0 CHW benefit), name the CHW certification framework under NM DOH, name the contracted managed-care organizations Puente intends to bill, and quantify the gap (if any) between post-grant CHW revenue and ongoing costs. Keep the section to ~250 words.

## 6. Cash match falls below the 50% threshold [RFP §4]

> "Applicants must document a cash match of at least $1 for every $2 requested (50% of the request amount)."

Documented cash on hand at the deadline: **$34,000** ($20,000 VCHC + $14,000 Puente board-designated). In-kind does not count.

| Scenario | Request | Cash required (50%) | Documented cash | Match % | Flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| As drafted | $83,847 | $41,924 | $34,000 | 40.5% | Fails |
| $83,847 + Rotary (hypothetical only — won't arrive in time) | $83,847 | $41,924 | $39,000 | 46.5% | Still fails |
| Reduced to $75,000, no Rotary | $75,000 | $37,500 | $34,000 | 45.3% | Fails |
| Reduced to $68,000, no Rotary | $68,000 | $34,000 | $34,000 | 50.0% | Barely passes; no margin |
| **$66,000 request, $34,000 documented cash** | **$66,000** | **$33,000** | **$34,000** | **51.5%** | **Passes with cushion** |

**Fix.** Either (a) cut the request to a level at which the existing $34,000 in signed letters covers ≥50%, **and/or** (b) raise documented cash by ≥$3,500 via a fresh treasurer letter (see Issue #5). The safe path is both — cut the request to the cap and add a small additional board designation.

## 7. Submission timing is wrong [RFP §7]

> "Applications must be submitted through the Foundation's online portal by 5:00 p.m. **Mountain Daylight Time (MDT, UTC−6)** on Thursday, October 1, 2026. The portal closes automatically at the deadline and will not accept late or partial submissions. Technical support is available until 4:00 p.m. MDT on the deadline day."

Arizona does **not** observe daylight saving time. Phoenix is on MST (UTC−7) year-round [documents/summit-itinerary.md]. Converting:

| Plan | Phoenix local time | Equivalent MDT (UTC−6) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lucia's plan: "4:30 p.m. … well before the 5:00 deadline" [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md] | 4:30 p.m. MST | **5:30 p.m. MDT** | **30 minutes past close — invalid** |
| Latest legal MST equivalent of the deadline | **4:00 p.m. MST** | 5:00 p.m. MDT | Latest acceptable upload |
| Technical support closes | 3:00 p.m. MST | 4:00 p.m. MDT | No recovery available after this |

The summit schedule on Oct 1 puts Lucia in breakouts **1:30–4:15 p.m. Phoenix time**, with her own presentation at 2:45 p.m. [documents/summit-itinerary.md]. Submitting at 4:30 p.m. Phoenix time is impossible to square with the deadline.

**Fix.** Three viable alternatives — pick one:

1. **Submit at the Thursday lunch break**, between sessions ending at 12:00 p.m. and breakouts starting at 1:30 p.m. Phoenix time. Portal support is open; deadline is six hours away.
2. **Submit by 8:00 a.m. Phoenix time Thursday**, after a final read-through earlier in the week. Support is open; full workday of cushion.
3. **Designate an Albuquerque-based backup user** with portal credentials, a copy of the final PDF, and matching sign-off authority, who can upload by **5:00 p.m. Mountain time (no DST)** on Oct 1 — i.e., the deadline hour at the home office. This removes the travel risk entirely and removes the UTC conversion entirely.

Whichever path is chosen, do not plan on a 4:30 p.m. Phoenix upload.

## 8. Budget table arithmetic is internally inconsistent [RFP §4]

> "The budget table must show each line item, total direct costs, indirect costs and the total request. Figures in the budget table, the budget narrative and the application cover sheet must agree."

[documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] shows:

| Source | Direct costs | Indirect | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sum of line items (Personnel $48,000 + Fringe $10,560 + Travel $5,400 + Supplies $3,850 + Training $2,600 + Contractual $1,500) | **$71,910** | — | — |
| Stated "Total direct costs" line in budget table | **$72,910** | — | — |
| Implied indirect (15% of $72,910) | — | **$10,937** | — |
| Total implied by table arithmetic ($72,910 + $10,937) | — | — | **$82,847** |
| Application cover sheet [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] | — | — | **$83,847** |
| **Discrepancy** | **$1,000** | — | **$1,000** |

If the line items are correct (they reconcile with the budget narrative), the "Total direct costs" row and the cover sheet are each overstated by $1,000. If the "Total direct costs" is the intended figure, a $1,000 line item is unaccounted for. Either way the package fails the agreement test in RFP §4.

**Fix.** Once the direct-cost baseline is settled in Issue #1, mirror it identically in (i) every line item, (ii) the "Total direct costs" row, (iii) the indirect calculation (10% of direct), and (iv) the cover sheet. Triple-check with Word's character count or a hand add.

Attachment E-3 (Rio Grande Medical Supply, 2026-09-03) is **in-kind** [documents/attachments-folder.md]. There is no designated attachment slot for in-kind in the RFP. Bundling it into E muddies the cash-match story for the staff screen.

**Fix.** Remove E-3 from the E series. Reference the $12,000 in-kind donation from the budget narrative (where it already appears [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]) and from the Match table labeled "In-kind — does not count toward the 50% threshold." Unlikely to trigger return, but clean labeling avoids questions at screening.

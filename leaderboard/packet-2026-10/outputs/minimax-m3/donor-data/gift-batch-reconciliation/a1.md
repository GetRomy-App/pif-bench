**Issue.** Per Standards §2, an envelope postmarked on or before June 30 and received in the 7/1–7/15 year-end window belongs to fiscal year 2026. The year-end batch **B-2606-YE** (open through 2026-08-14) is the correct batch — never a July batch. The CRM row is also booked with the wrong gift date (received date, not postmark date).

**Adjusting entry.** Move G-41803 from B-2607-01 to B-2606-YE; change gift_date from 2026-07-02 to **2026-06-30** (the postmark). Donor, amount, fund, reference, and check date stay the same; ack_sent 2026-07-08 stands.

**Effect on July batch total:** −$1,000.00 (this gift is excluded from the July corrected total under Standards §5(c)).

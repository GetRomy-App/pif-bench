## Method (from [documents/metrics-definitions.md])

- **Fiscal year** = July 1 – June 30, named for the year in which it ends. FY2026 ran Jul 1, 2025 – Jun 30, 2026 (the most recent complete fiscal year). FY2027 began Jul 1, 2026; "to date" here means up to 2026-09-15.
- **Unit = household.** Constituents sharing a `household_id` count once. Joint-account gifts — whether entered on one spouse with a soft credit to the other, or split between the two spouses' records — count once for the household.
- **Countable gift** = hard-credit, Posted gift to an individual constituent. Soft credits, refunded gifts, and organization gifts are **not** countable. The hard credit on a matching-gift record is the company's gift; the employee gets only a soft credit.
- **Organizations** (Northgate Systems, Thistlewood Family Foundation, Sourwood Garden Club, Ferncliff Brewing Co.) are excluded from every count.
- **LYBUNT** = household with a countable gift in FY2026 and none in FY2027 to date.
- **SYBUNT** = household with a countable gift in FY2021–FY2025, none in FY2026, and none in FY2027 to date. (A household whose last gift is before FY2021 is "lapsed," not SYBUNT. No household is both LYBUNT and SYBUNT.)
- **Retention rate** is reported for the most recent complete fiscal year (FY2026): # households with a countable gift in both FY2025 and FY2026 ÷ # households with a countable gift in FY2025, to one decimal place.

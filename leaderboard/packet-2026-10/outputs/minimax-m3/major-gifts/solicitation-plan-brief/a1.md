**Ask = $80,000.** Stated as a single figure, not a range (§1.6).

Household-gift accounting (§1.5): the $25,000 2023 Spring Gala presenting sponsorship paid by Vandermolen Marine Supply, Inc. is a **corporate** gift and excluded; the $2,500 2026 Spring Gala table purchase is an **event** purchase and excluded [documents/crm-record.md]. ## 2. Purpose

The **Adult Literacy Program** — the only area of Foundation work the Vandermolens have supported, and the only one they will be asked about.

Celeste and Martin have explicitly declined capital and naming interests. On 2026-06-10 Celeste said: "we don't do buildings, and we don't do names. Their giving goes to adult literacy. Anything they give is to be anonymous: no plaques, no lists, no announcements." [documents/contact-reports.md, 2026-06-10] On 2024-06-03 Celeste asked that their gifts no longer be listed in the annual report [documents/contact-reports.md, 2024-06-03]. The 2025-10-14 DAF grant letter asked that the gift be listed as "Anonymous" [documents/contact-reports.md, 2025-10-14].

**Recommended direction:** the **Upriver evening tutoring site pilot**, opened September 2026, at $38,000 a year [documents/funding-opportunities.md]. Celeste asked how Upriver was going on 2026-09-15 and was promised an update at this fall's meeting, which gives the conversation a natural entry point [documents/contact-reports.md, 2026-09-15]. Three years × ~$26,700/yr = $80,000 underwrites most of the pilot's three-year cost.

**Alternative direction:** the **Adult Literacy Endowment** (any amount) [documents/funding-opportunities.md] — a permanent, unrestricted instrument closer in spirit to Martin's original 2022 wish for "something that lasts" [documents/contact-reports.md, 2022-05-12] without the named building he then proposed.

**Not on the table.** Open Doors / Eastside Branch campaign opportunities of any size, including the Adult Literacy Learning Lab ($75,000) and Quiet Study Rooms ($25,000 each) [documents/funding-opportunities.md]. The Vandermolens do not support buildings or naming; §2 of the Solicitation Policy forbids proposing a purpose the prospect has said they will not support, whatever the Foundation's current campaign priorities. The campaign chair asked staff to "look for Open Doors asks … wherever a donor's interests allow" [documents/board-minutes-2026-07-15.md, item 7]; donor interests do not allow it here.

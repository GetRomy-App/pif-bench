The Castellane Foundation is the only rated, non-N prospect in the pipeline at the lead tier; trustees at the April retreat questioned whether a $2,000,000 lead gift was in reach for Asheville, and identification effort at this tier is the most constrained [documents/april-retreat-draft-chart.md], [documents/prospect-pipeline.csv].

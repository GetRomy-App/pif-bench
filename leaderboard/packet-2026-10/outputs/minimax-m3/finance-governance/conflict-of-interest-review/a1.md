## 2. Whether each transaction is a conflict of interest

### 2a. BrightStage AV Services LLC (Director Priya Ramaswamy)

A "Financial Interest" exists under Article II, Section 2(a) and (c): Director
Ramaswamy has an indirect ownership interest, through a Family Member (her
brother Arun), in an entity that submitted a bid to the Theatre
[documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. The February 12, 2026 minutes
record the disinterested directors' determination that the interest is a
"conflict of interest under the policy" [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].

This conflict was handled correctly:

- **Annual statement (Article VI).** Ramaswamy disclosed the BrightStage
  interest on her January 14, 2026 statement, Question 2
  [documents/annual-statements-2026.md].
- **Pre-meeting written notice (Article III, Section 1).** She emailed the
  Chair on February 2, 2026, ahead of the meeting at which the bid was
  considered [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **Oral disclosure at the meeting.** Made at the call of the Chair before
  discussion [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **Recusal (Article III, Section 3(a)).** "Ms. Ramaswamy…left the room at
  7:12 pm…Ms. Ramaswamy did not take part in the discussion or the vote and
  returned to the meeting at 7:24 pm" [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **Competing written bids (Article III, Section 4).** Three written bids were
  compared — BrightStage $18,250; Coastline $21,400; Meridian $23,900
  [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].
- **Best-interest / fair-and-reasonable finding (Article III, Section 3(d)).**
  The minutes record that the disinterested directors "found that no more
  advantageous arrangement was available, that BrightStage's bid was the
  lowest, and that the contract is in the Theatre's best interest and fair and
  reasonable" [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **Vote.** 10–0 in favor; Ramaswamy absent
  [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].

**Finding.** BrightStage was a conflict of interest, and the policy was
followed. No further action is required under Article IV with respect to this
transaction.

### 2b. Saffron & Sage Events LLC (Director Thomas Aldana)

A "Financial Interest" exists on the same analysis: Director Aldana has an
indirect ownership interest, through a Family Member (his wife Celeste, a
spouse under Article II, Section 3), in an entity that submitted a bid to the
Theatre [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. Celeste M. Aldana is the
100% owner, registered agent, signatory of the bid, signatory of the W-9 and
signatory of the contract
[documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md; documents/state-business-registry.md].

This conflict was **not** properly handled:

- **Annual statement (Article VI) was inaccurate.** Aldana signed his 2026
  statement on January 20, 2026 and answered "No" to Question 2
  [documents/annual-statements-2026.md]. Saffron & Sage's bid was received on
  January 8, 2026 [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md] — twelve days before
  Aldana signed. The bid was signed by Celeste M. Aldana, Managing Member, at
  88 Orchard Terrace — the same address as Director Aldana on the board roster
  [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md; documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].
  Under Question 3 Aldana identified his spouse as "Celeste Aldana — self-
  employed" [documents/annual-statements-2026.md].
- **No pre-meeting written notice (Article III, Section 1).** No email from
  Aldana to the Chair is in the record; the meeting minutes state "No other
  director made a disclosure" [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **No oral disclosure at the meeting.** Same source: "No other director made
  a disclosure" [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **No recusal (Article III, Section 3(a)).** Aldana did not leave the room
  during the discussion of the catering bid; on the contrary, he **moved** the
  motion to award the contract to Saffron & Sage and voted in favor of it
  [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md]. The motion carried 6–5
  [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].
- **The competitive-bid requirement (Article III, Section 4) was technically
  met** — three written bids were on file for the same specification
  [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].
- **The best-interest / fair-and-reasonable determination (Article III,
  Sections 3(c) and 3(d)) was made by a board that included an Interested
  Person.** The minutes do not reflect the analysis required by Section 3(c)
  — that the disinterested directors "determine whether the Theatre can
  obtain, with reasonable efforts, a more advantageous transaction or
  arrangement" from a non-conflicted bidder. The bid comparison on its face
  shows a more advantageous arrangement: Harborview Provisions bid $52,900,
  Tidewater $55,750, and Saffron & Sage $68,400, a $15,500 spread
  [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md]. The Gala Committee had, on January
  29, recommended Harborview on price and prior performance
  [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].

**Finding.** Saffron & Sage was a conflict of interest that was not disclosed
or addressed in the manner the policy requires. The contract was approved
without disclosure and recusal, which is the condition that triggers the
Article IV re-review and corrective-action process described in Section 4.

## 3. Schedule L (Form 990) reporting

The Theatre's fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, so the reportable year is the
**2025 Form 990**, due November 16, 2026
[documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md; documents/controller-email.md].
Total revenue on the draft return (Part VIII, line 12) is $3,940,000, so
1% of revenue is **$39,400**, and the threshold (b) figure is the greater of
$10,000 or $39,400 = **$39,400** [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md;
documents/controller-email.md].

Both entities qualify as "interested persons" for Schedule L Part IV: Saffron
& Sage is more than 35% owned by a family member of a director; BrightStage
is 80% owned by a family member of a director
[documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md; documents/state-business-registry.md].
Reporting is then driven by the dollar thresholds.

- **BrightStage AV Services LLC — $18,250 paid.** Below the $100,000 threshold
  (a) and below the $39,400 threshold (b). **Not reportable** on Schedule L
  Part IV for FY 2026.
- **Saffron & Sage Events LLC — $71,860 paid.** Below $100,000 (a) but
  **above $39,400 (b)**. **Reportable** on Schedule L Part IV. The
  transaction must also be flagged on Form 990, Part IV (the line 27 / line 28
  questions share the same thresholds)
  [documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md].

Article VII of the Conflict of Interest Policy directs the Controller to give
the Theatre's Form 990 preparer, each year, "a list of every transaction
during the fiscal year with a director, officer or key employee, with their
Family Members, and with entities they or their Family Members own or control,
so that Schedule L and Part VI of the Form 990 are completed accurately"
[documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. The Controller, Wesley Tran, has
flagged this and is awaiting the Governance Committee's review before acting
[documents/controller-email.md].

## 4. What the policy requires the committee to do next

The following steps are mandatory under Article IV of the policy
[documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

### 4a. Article IV, Section 1 — Notice and opportunity to explain

The committee must inform Director Aldana in writing of the basis for the
committee's reasonable cause to believe he failed to disclose an actual or
possible conflict of interest, and give him 15 days to explain the failure to
disclose, in writing or at a meeting of the committee. The Controller's
August 14 note and attachments, and the W-9 and state-records printout dated
August 13, supply the evidentiary basis
[documents/controller-email.md; documents/controller-email.md attachments].

### 4b. Article IV, Section 4 — Recusal from the committee

Director Aldana must take no part in the Governance Committee's
deliberations or votes on this matter, "including as a member of the Governance
Committee" [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. He is named to the
committee in the board roster [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md] and would
otherwise be one of its three members. For the September 3 meeting this
leaves a committee of two voting members (Pettigrew as chair, Sato). A
recommendation to the full board is still possible.

### 4c. Article IV, Section 2 — Hearing, finding and recommendation

After hearing Director Aldana's response and making any further investigation
the circumstances warrant, the committee reports its findings to the board.
The board, acting by a majority of the disinterested directors, determines
whether he failed to disclose an actual or possible conflict. If the board so
determines, it "shall take appropriate disciplinary and corrective action,
which may include a written reprimand, removal from committee or officer
positions, a request for resignation, or removal under Bylaws Article IV,
Section 7."

### 4d. Article IV, Section 3 — Re-review of the Saffron & Sage transaction

Because the catering contract was "approved without the disclosure and
recusal this policy requires," the disinterested directors must re-review
the transaction under Article III, Sections 3(c) and 3(d). The re-review
must determine, on the existing bid record, whether a more advantageous
arrangement was reasonably available from a non-conflicted bidder. The
factual record before the committee on this point includes:

- Three written bids for the same specification: Harborview $52,900;
  Tidewater $55,750; Saffron & Sage $68,400
  [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].
- The Gala Committee's January 29 recommendation of Harborview on price and
  the 2024-gala reference [documents/gala-bids-and-contracts.md].
- On-site and contractual additions of $3,460 to the Saffron & Sage bill
  [documents/payment-register-fy2026-gala.md].

If the re-review finds that the Theatre paid more than fair market value, the
board "shall consult legal counsel on whether the payment may be an excess
benefit transaction under Internal Revenue Code section 4958 and on recovering
the excess" [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]. Section 4958 includes
sanctions taxes on the disqualified person and on any board members who
participated in the decision knowingly; legal counsel should be brought in
before, not after, any recovery discussion.

### 4e. Article IV, Section 5 — Corrected annual statement

If the board determines that Director Aldana failed to disclose an actual or
possible conflict, he "shall file a corrected annual statement (Article VI)
within 30 days of the board's determination" [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
The corrected statement will need to identify Celeste M. Aldana's interest in
Saffron & Sage Events LLC.

### 4f. Article V — Records of proceedings

Minutes of the September 3 Governance Committee meeting and of any
subsequent board action must record: the names of persons who disclosed or
were found to have a Financial Interest and the nature of the interest; any
action taken to determine whether a conflict was present and the board's
decision; and the names of the persons present for the discussion and votes on
the transaction, the content of the discussion (including alternatives
considered), and a record of the vote [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
The Article IV.3 re-review of the Saffron & Sage transaction is itself a
"transaction" within the meaning of Article V and must be minuted in the same
form.

### 4g. Form 990 / Schedule L

Article VII of the policy directs the Controller to give Pryce & Elwood the
list of interested-person transactions so that Schedule L and Part VI of the
2025 Form 990 are completed accurately [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
The Saffron & Sage transaction ($71,860) is reportable on Schedule L, Part IV
[documents/schedule-l-part-iv-excerpt.md]. The BrightStage transaction
($18,250) does not meet either Schedule L threshold and need not be reported,
though it should remain in the supporting working papers. The Controller has
asked to hold the list pending committee review
[documents/controller-email.md]; once the committee confirms the analysis
above, he should be instructed to release the updated list to the preparer
before the November 16 deadline.

## 5. Other items worth noting for the committee

- **No other director is presently identified as having a family-member
  interest in a Spring Gala 2026 vendor.** Of the directors voting in favor
  of the Saffron & Sage motion (Aldana, Brandt, Castellanos, Fairweather,
  Siddiqui, Whitaker), only Aldana's statement and the vendor's ownership
  records connect him to the bidder
  [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md; documents/annual-statements-2026.md].
  The Controller's vendor-by-vendor cross-check covered the two vendors
  flagged; it may be prudent, before the board acts on Article IV.2, to have
  the Controller apply the same W-9 / state-registry check to the other
  paid gala vendors (Harbor Grand Hotel, Lantern Press, Bayside Florals,
  Coastal Valet Co.) on the off chance of an undisclosed family connection.
- **Committee membership.** The board roster lists three Governance Committee
  members [documents/board-roster-2025-26.md]. Article IV.4 will require
  Aldana's recusal; the remaining two members can still constitute a quorum
  for purposes of the investigation and a recommendation to the board, but
  the committee may wish to consider whether to recommend that the board
  expand the committee or appoint a disinterested director to participate in
  the Article IV work.
- **Form 990, Part VI.** Independent of Schedule L Part IV, Part VI of the
  return asks about the organization's conflict-of-interest policy and
  compliance — including whether the organization regularly and consistently
  monitors and enforces compliance. The Article IV process now underway, and
  its documentation, is the answer the preparer will need for that question
  as well.
- **Records limitation.** The annual-statement file in the Theatre's records
  reproduces only three of the thirteen 2026 statements; the controller's
  August 14 note relied on the same three plus the registry cross-check
  [documents/annual-statements-2026.md; documents/controller-email.md]. If
  the committee wishes to verify the absence of other undisclosed family
  interests during the period in question, it should request the remaining
  ten 2026 statements from the Secretary, who collected them
  [documents/board-minutes-2026-02-12.md].

### Treatment notes

- **"Person" aggregation** ([documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md], [documents/finance-manager-notes.md]): Marguerite, Dellwood, Hollins-Pryor, Benedict, Juniper, the Kowalczyks, Bramble, Upper Alder, and Oregon DEQ are each one "person." The Kowalczyks give jointly and are already listed as one donor. Dellwood is publicly held and not controlled by any individual donor on the list; no other two donors on the list are related.
- **The 2% cap exception** ([documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md]) applies to grants from governmental units and to contributions from other 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) organizations **unless** a donor earmarked the money through that organization for us. Oregon DEQ is a governmental unit; Upper Alder qualifies because (a) it is recognized under section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) on IRS TEOS (checked 2026-09-09), (b) all three grants came from its competitive Watershed Fund decided by its own grants committee, (c) none came from a donor-advised fund, and (d) no donor earmarked any of them for us ([documents/finance-manager-notes.md]).
- **Hollins-Pryor and Juniper Ridge are *not* eligible for the exception.** Both are private non-operating foundations (Form 990-PF), not 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) public charities. Only Hollins-Pryor's $225,000 five-year total exceeds the $73,432 threshold, so its excess is the only private-foundation excess on Line 5 ([documents/finance-manager-notes.md], [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md]).
- **The $35,000 from Benedict Sarkis for the Cedar Flats culvert** is a contribution for one of our projects; it is not a fee-for-service payment, so it stays on Line 1 in full ([documents/finance-manager-notes.md]).

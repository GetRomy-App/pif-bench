# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Rosalind Achterberg, Chair, Finance Committee
**FROM:** CFO's Office
**DATE:** September 9, 2026
**RE:** Financial Health Indicators — Fiscal Year 2025 (year ended December 31, 2025)

This memo presents the three financial health indicators defined in the board's Financial Health Indicators Policy ([documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md], adopted 2023-03-21, revised 2025-11-18) for fiscal year 2025, computed from the Form 990 filed 2026-08-11 ([documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]) and from the notes to the audited financial statements (auditor's report dated 22 April 2026) ([documents/audit-notes-2025.md]). It is provided in advance of the 15 September 2026 finance committee meeting and accompanies the meeting materials released today, as required by Policy §4.1.

Per Policy §2.2, gifts in kind (contributed nonfinancial assets recognized in the audited statements, Note 9) have been removed from every revenue and expense figure used here. Gifts in kind recognized in FY2025 were:

- Donated food distributed, $14,180,000 — Part IX, line 24a, column (B); Note 9 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md, documents/audit-notes-2025.md]
- Donated food added to inventory, $70,000 — Note 5 / Note 9 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]
- Donated legal services, $95,000 — Part IX, line 11b, column (C); Note 9 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md, documents/audit-notes-2025.md]

Gifts in kind expensed in FY2025 total $14,275,000 ($14,180,000 + $95,000). Together with the $70,000 inventory add, this reconciles to the $14,345,000 of noncash contributions reported on Part VIII, line 1g ([documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]).

---

## 1. Program expense ratio (Policy §3.1)

**Formula.** (Program service expenses − gifts in kind expensed in program services) ÷ (Total functional expenses − all gifts in kind expensed).

**Inputs.**

| Item | Amount | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Program service expenses | $19,066,000 | Form 990, Part IX, line 25, column (B) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] |
| Less: gifts in kind expensed in program services | (14,180,000) | Form 990, Part IX, line 24a, column (B); Note 9 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md, documents/audit-notes-2025.md] |
| **Adjusted program service expenses** | **$4,886,000** | |
| Total functional expenses | $20,515,000 | Form 990, Part IX, line 25, column (A) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] |
| Less: all gifts in kind expensed | (14,275,000) | Donated food $14,180,000 (Part IX, line 24a, column (B)) plus donated legal services $95,000 (Part IX, line 11b, column (C)); Note 9 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md, documents/audit-notes-2025.md] |
| **Adjusted total functional expenses** | **$6,240,000** | |

**Calculation.** $4,886,000 ÷ $6,240,000 = 0.7830 = **78.3%**.

**Target.** 80.0% or higher.
**Result.** **Not met** — 78.3% is 1.7 percentage points below the 80.0% target.

## Next steps under the policy

- **§4.2 corrective plans.** The program expense ratio (78.3%) and the cost to raise a dollar ($0.22) are below their targets. Management will present a corrective plan for each to the committee within 90 days of this memo.
- **§3.2 external review.** Not triggered: the cost to raise a dollar ($0.22) does not exceed the $0.25 threshold. No other bequest or estate distribution besides the $600,000 Okafor distribution was received in FY2025 (Note 10) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
- **§3.3 treasurer notification.** Not triggered: 3.1 months is well above the 1.5-month threshold.
- **§4.3 external publication.** Any external use of these indicators (annual report, website, grant applications) must be on the basis defined in the policy; CFO's office will route proposed uses to the committee.

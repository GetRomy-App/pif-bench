## 1. Summary

Marcus D. Ellery and his spouse Renée L. Ellery have supported SCYT since 2021, with personal Annual Fund gifts totaling $15,000 over the past five years and a five-year pattern of steady escalation. A minor child in the household is currently enrolled in SCYT's 2026 Summer Intensive. The household is engaged across Winston-Salem arts, education and housing nonprofits, and Marcus serves on two boards. Marcus and his partner founded Ellery Mechanical Contractors, LLC in 2004; he also controls EMC Properties, LLC as sole member. Five-year giving capacity calculated under the SCYT method is $313,750, yielding a B rating.

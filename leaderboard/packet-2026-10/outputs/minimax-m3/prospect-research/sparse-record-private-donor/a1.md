## 2. The *Colorado Business Ledger* Emily Hartwell is not constituent 4471

Our research standards allow a record to be attributed to a constituent only when at least two of the five specified identifiers match **and none conflicts**. A "shared name, a shared home state or region, and a constituent's own general remarks are not identifiers" [documents/research-standards.md §1]. Applying the rule to the 2026-08-19 story [documents/news-article.md]:

| Identifier | Constituent 4471 [documents/crm-record.md; documents/public-records.md] | Article subject [documents/news-article.md] | Match / Conflict |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Full name + middle initial | Emily **R.** Hartwell | Emily Hartwell (no initial given) | Missing — not a conflict |
| (b) Home / mailing address | 48 Foxcroft Ln, Middlebury, VT 05753 | Denver's Highland neighborhood (loosely) | **Conflict** |
| (c) Spouse's name | Daniel P. Hartwell | Andre Hartwell (high-school chemistry teacher) | **Conflict** |
| (d) Age within 3 years | 58 in 2026 (b. 1968, self-reported) | 34 on 2026-08-19 (≈ b. 1992) | **Conflict — off by ~24 years** |
| (e) Occupation / employer | not recorded in CRM | CEO, Trailhead Analytics (Denver) | Missing — not a conflict |

Two identifiers conflict outright (spouse, age), and the third (address) is consistent with the rejection. Per §1 of the standards, the entire article is rejected, and **none** of its facts — the $84 million sale, the roughly $25 million stake, the Trailhead business, the role and compensation information — may be attached to constituent 4471's record or used in any estimate.

The reasoning in the 2026-09-04 CRM note ("The founder spent summers on Lake Dunmore, fifteen minutes from Foxcroft Lane, and Emily said she used to be in software. It has to be her!" — [documents/crm-record.md]) is precisely the kind of evidence §1 says does **not** count as identification: a shared regional connection and a constituent's own general self-description (her remark to you on 2024-10-26 that she "used to be in software, a long time ago," [documents/crm-record.md]). There is indeed a different Emily Hartwell who grew up at her grandparents' Lake Dunmore camp and now owns it — and she is not the constituent (see §3).

## 5. Ask amount for the Harvest Gala on October 24, 2026

For unrated prospects, "every personal ask — renewal, appeal, gala paddle-raise and event-sponsorship asks included — is 1.5 × the donor's last gift, rounded up to the next multiple of $250" [documents/research-standards.md §5]. "Last gift" means the most recent **outright hard-credited gift** to the donor. Event tickets, other purchases, matching gifts and soft credits are not gifts; that explicitly includes soft credits for gifts from a business the donor owns.

The relevant entries on Emily R. Hartwell's record [documents/crm-record.md] are:

- **2026-04-03 · $750 · Check · Spring Appeal · Hard** — last outright gift.
- 2026-05-14 · $150 · Event purchase (Spring Recital reception tickets, 2) · Hard — event purchase, **not a gift**.
- 2026-07-02 · $2,000 · Soft credit · Summer Concert Series sponsorship — gift of Hartwell Bookkeeping & Tax, LLC · Soft — soft credit from a business she owns, **not a gift**.

Calculation: 1.5 × $750 = $1,125 → rounded up to the next multiple of $250 = **$1,250**.

**Harvest Gala paddle-raise ask: $1,250.** No ask above this amount — including the $25,000 Presenting Sponsorship you noted on 2026-09-04 — may be made to constituent 4471 for the gala or for any other School purpose until a discovery visit has taken place and a rating has been issued [documents/research-standards.md §5, §6].

Following the visit, request re-rating.
3. **At the gala, the paddle-raise ask is $1,250.** A Presenting Sponsorship is not within the unrated-prospect ceiling and should not be proposed this cycle.

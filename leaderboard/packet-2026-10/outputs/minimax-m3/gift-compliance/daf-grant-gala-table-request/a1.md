- **Soft credit:** Margaret and Erik Halvorsen (constituent 20417).
- **Amount / method:** $15,000, Kestrel check #0880413, grant KCT-2026-0418833 [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].

### 2. Application to the Halvorsen Statement of Intent

The Halvorsens signed the Studio Wing Campaign Statement of Intent on 2024-11-12 on our current form, SW-SOI rev. 2023-02 — total intended $40,000, 2025 through 2028 [documents/statement-of-intent.md]. That form is a non-binding statement of intent under Policy §4.4(1) [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].

- **Apply the full $15,000 to the SOI.** Policy §4.4(1) allows a DAF grant recommended by the person who signed the SOI to be soft-credited toward it, and the open balance is reduced by the full amount of the grant [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
- **Open SOI balance before this grant:** $20,000. (The 2025-01-14 personal check of $10,000 and the 2025-12-19 Kestrel DAF grant of $10,000 applied; the 2025-10-25 personal-card payment of $2,800 for the 2025 gala table did not apply because event purchases are not applied to a campaign SOI under Policy §4.5) [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md, documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
- **Open SOI balance after this grant:** $5,000.
- **Do not reference the SOI in any correspondence with Kestrel Charitable Trust** — acknowledgment, grant requests, status inquiries (Policy §4.4(3)) [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md]. As a matter of practice, we will also not reference it in the thank-you letter to Peggy, so we do not blur the line between the legal donor (Kestrel) and the advisor (the Halvorsens).

### 3. The Starlight Gala 2026 table — INV-G26-0112 ($3,000)

We cannot apply any portion of the Kestrel grant to this invoice. Two independent reasons:

1. **Policy §4.3.** DAF funds may not pay for any part of a ticket, table, sponsorship, membership or other benefit, neither in full nor above fair market value; we do not split ("bifurcate") the price of a benefit between a DAF grant and the advisor; an advisor who wants seats pays for them personally (personal check, card or other personal funds — never a DAF); we do not accept a DAF grant designated for an event purchase [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
2. **Kestrel grant condition 2.** "[T]he grant may not be used, in whole or in part, to provide any benefit to the fund's advisors, their family members or any other person — including event tickets or tables, dinners, memberships, or other goods or services" [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].

Therefore:

- **Keep invoice INV-G26-0112 open and unpaid** — table of 8, table 3, Starlight Gala 2026, $3,000, due 2026-10-02, reserved by phone on 2026-08-20 [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md]. Table invoices are due 2026-10-02 so the caterer can be given final numbers [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md].
- **Do not suggest to Peggy that a second Kestrel grant earmarked for the table would work.** It would not — Kestrel's own grant terms forbid a DAF grant paying for a benefit, and Policy §4.3 prohibits us from accepting a DAF grant designated for an event purchase [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md, documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].
- **Once Peggy pays the $3,000 personally**, book it as follows:
  - **Hard credit:** Margaret and Erik Halvorsen.
  - **Designation:** "Starlight Gala 2026 — table of 8 (table 3)."
  - **Do not apply to the SOI** (Policy §4.5) [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
  - **§6115 / §4.6 disclosure.** The 2026 gala-seat FMV is $185 per guest, CFO-approved 2026-07-30 [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md]. FMV for the table = 8 × $185 = $1,480. Deductible amount = $3,000 − $1,480 = $1,520. The acknowledgment must state the payment, describe the goods and services, give the CFO-approved FMV, include the disclosure and state the resulting deductible amount [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md]. Program listings are recognition and carry no value [documents/starlight-gala-fact-sheet.md].

## Draft reply to Peggy

> **Subject:** Kestrel grant and your gala table
>
> Dear Peggy,
>
> Thank you for your note — and for the wonderful news that the Kestrel grant has arrived. Erik and you should know how much that does for the Studio Wing. We will be sending you a separate thank-you letter, under separate cover, for recommending the grant.
>
> I want to be upfront about three things in your note, because they affect what we can do with the gift and the table.
>
> **The $15,000 Kestrel grant.** Kestrel Charitable Trust is the legal sponsor of The Halvorsen Family Fund and is the donor of record for this gift — not you and Erik. Both Kestrel's grant letter and our gift-acceptance policy require that we (1) use the entire $15,000 for the Studio Wing Campaign, the purpose Kestrel specified, and (2) acknowledge the gift to Kestrel. So we will be applying the full $15,000 to the Studio Wing Campaign and sending our written acknowledgment to Kestrel. We are not able to redirect any portion of the grant to your gala table, even though that would obviously be simpler.
>
> **The $3,000 for your gala table.** Your table for eight — table 3, by the stage — is reserved, and we very much want to keep it that way. To do so, you will need to pay the $3,000 from you and Erik personally — personal check, personal card, or other personal funds. We are not able to apply any portion of a donor-advised fund toward a ticket, a table, a sponsorship or a membership: federal rules and our own policy treat gala seating and dinner as more than an incidental benefit, and Kestrel's own grant letter specifically prohibits it as well. We do not split the price of a table between a DAF grant and a personal payment, and we cannot accept a DAF grant earmarked for an event purchase. Asking Kestrel to send a second, smaller grant just for the table would not work either, for the same reasons.
>
> If it would help, I can send you a fresh invoice or a credit-card link for the $3,000 right now — just say the word and we'll get it sorted before the 2026-10-02 due date the caterer needs.
>
> **A receipt for the full $15,000.** I know your tax preparer likes to have everything in hand well before year-end, and I completely understand the instinct. We will not be able to send you a tax receipt, because any income-tax benefit associated with The Halvorsen Family Fund was received when you and Erik contributed to the fund. If it would help with the file, I'm happy to share the Kestrel grant letter we received and the date the gift was deposited, and to send a courtesy copy of our acknowledgment to Kestrel once it goes out, so your tax preparer has the underlying documentation.
>
> Erik and you — we cannot wait to see you on the 17th.
>
> Warmly,
> Jordan
>
> Jordan Ellery

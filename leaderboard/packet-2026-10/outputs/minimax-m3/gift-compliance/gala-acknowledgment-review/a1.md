## P-05 — Denise Okafor — $300.00 (online credit card at the gala)

- **Deductible amount:** **$200.00** (the gift component only)
- **Raffle component:** **$100.00** — not a deductible charitable contribution, per §3.3
- **Template:** the $200 gift portion is GALA-GIFT; the $100 raffle portion is presented alongside it with the required disclosure that raffle purchases are not deductible, per §6.
- **Reason.** §3.3: *"Amounts paid for raffle tickets are not deductible charitable contributions; the buyer pays for a chance to win a prize. A raffle purchase is never included in a contribution amount, and no acknowledgment states a deductible amount for it."* §6 requires combined transactions to be addressed separately; the contribution amount excludes any raffle purchase, and the acknowledgment must say so where a raffle purchase appears on it. Dana's note recorded this correctly ("$200 gift plus 5 raffle tickets for $100") but the CRM booked it as one gift with a $300 deductible, which is not allowed.
- **Acknowledgment must state:** gift of $200 to Books for Every Backpack, no goods or services provided, deductible contribution $200; **separately**, the $100 raffle purchase is acknowledged *as a purchase*, with the statement that raffle ticket purchases are not charitable contributions and are not deductible. Total payment: $300. Only $200 is deductible.
- **Acknowledgment must not state:** deductible contribution $300; a deductible amount of any size for the $100 raffle portion; that the raffle is a "gift."
- **CRM corrections the processor must make:** split the row — book the $200 gift on a GALA-GIFT row with benefit $0 and receipt $200, and book the $100 raffle purchase so it is **excluded** from the deductible total, with language reflecting §3.3/§6 on the accompanying letter. (Per §8, this change requires CFO or Director of Development approval; approval is granted by signing off this review.)

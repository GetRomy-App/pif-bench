- **Real solution.** The Operating Reserve ($240,000, board-designated) can be released by majority vote of the full Board at the October 21 meeting, no donor consent and no AG notice required [documents/fund-statements.md; documents/endowment-policy.md §5.1].

## 2. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment

**Facts on which the analysis rests** (gift agreement, fund statement, registrar):

- Permanent endowment established March 15, 2017; original gift $400,000; purpose restricted by §3 of the gift agreement to "scholarships to students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) program," with first-in-family preference [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md §1, §3].
- Founded in memory of Thomas A. Ruiz, R.N., by Ellen M. Ruiz [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- Market value $512,860 at August 31, 2026, with twelve quarter-end values supplied [documents/fund-statements.md].
- ADN is active: 84 enrolled Fall 2026, 31 expected to graduate May 2027, approved by the State Board of Nursing through 2031 [documents/program-status.md].

### What we cannot do

- **Move corpus, accumulated earnings, or any portion of the FY2027 distribution into general operations.** Policy §4.1 directs that donor-restricted funds are used only as the gift instrument directs, and §3.2 says each distribution may be spent only for the purpose of the fund it comes from [documents/endowment-policy.md §3.2, §4.1; documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md §3].
- **Make an inter-fund loan from the Ruiz endowment to operations.** Policy §4.2 expressly forbids it: "Donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund, whatever the term or rate of the loan" [documents/endowment-policy.md §4.2]. That language answers Joan's loan proposal directly.
- **Release or modify the purpose restriction at the October 21 meeting.** UPMIFA allows release or modification of a donor restriction only with the donor's consent under §6(a), or by court order (§6(b) management/investment, §6(c) cy pres on purpose) with notice to the Attorney General [documents/upmifa-summary.md §6(a)–(c)]. We have no donor consent on file, no AG notice has been sent, and counsel's summary tells us that "a budget shortfall at the institution does not, by itself, make a fund's purpose impracticable or impossible" [documents/upmifa-summary.md, practice notes].

### What we can do

- **Make the FY2027 distribution to ADN scholarships, on the schedule we already use.** Under Policy §3.1, the FY2027 distribution is 4% of the average of the twelve calendar quarter-ends preceding July 1, 2026. Using the values in the fund statement:

  $468,200 + $476,900 + $483,500 + $480,100 + $492,300 + $495,700 + $489,400 + $501,800 + $510,600 + $516,300 + $508,900 + $506,500 = **$5,930,200**
  Average = $5,930,200 / 12 = **$494,183.33**; 4% = **$19,767.33** (rounded to the cent) [documents/endowment-policy.md §3.1; documents/fund-statements.md].

  That $19,767.33 must be paid out as scholarships to ADN students this fiscal year. It is not available for operations.

- **If the ADN program ever closes**, gift agreement §5 already lets the Board redirect distributions to the College's closest health-sciences program consistent with the donor's intent, subject to UPMIFA [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md §5]. That is not the situation today.

**Conclusion for the October 21 packet: take no action on the Ruiz endowment.**

## 3. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund

**Facts on which the analysis rests** (gift letter, board minutes, fund statement, registrar, treasurer's note attached to donor file):

- Established May 26, 1994, by gift letter of May 12, 1994, with $5,000 from Margaret E. Calloway. The earnings are to fund "an annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the College's Secretarial Studies certificate program," so that "a young person entering office work may begin with confidence and a little help" [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].
- Donor died February 9, 2002. The letter names no successor or advisor [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md, donor file note].
- Market value $18,420 at August 31, 2026. No award has been made since 2011; earnings have accumulated in the fund [documents/fund-statements.md].
- The Secretarial Studies certificate was discontinued by the Board of Trustees on June 30, 2011; no student can enroll or graduate from it. The active successor program is the Office Administration A.A.S. (47 enrolled Fall 2026) [documents/program-status.md; documents/fund-statements.md].

### What we cannot do

- **Fold the Calloway fund into general operations by a simple Board vote at the October 21 meeting.** The fund is donor-restricted, and Policy §4.1 says restricted funds are used only as the gift instrument directs [documents/endowment-policy.md §4.1; documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].

### What we can do

- **Use UPMIFA §6(d), the small-old-fund release.** All three conditions are met and we have the facts to support them:

  1. Fund value under $25,000 — $18,420 [documents/fund-statements.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md §6(d)(1)].
  2. More than 20 years since established — 32 years (May 26, 1994 → September 28, 2026) [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md §6(d)(2)].
  3. The original purpose is impossible to achieve — the Secretarial Studies certificate is discontinued and no student can graduate from it [documents/program-status.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md §6(d); documents/calloway-gift-letter.md].

  §6(d) also requires that "the institution uses the property in a manner consistent with the charitable purposes expressed in the gift instrument" [documents/upmifa-summary.md §6(d)]. Mrs. Calloway's charitable purpose, read from her letter, is to help a young person entering office work. The Office Administration A.A.S. is the College's current office-careers program [documents/program-status.md]. Repurposing the Calloway fund to an annual award for an Office Administration A.A.S. graduating student is, on the face of the record, consistent with her purpose.

- **An important limitation the President and Treasurer should see clearly:** §6(d) does not let the Board redeploy Calloway money to general operations. Even after release, the funds must be used in a manner consistent with the donor's purpose — i.e., to support office-career students. So the practical effect of a §6(d) release is to redirect roughly **$18,420** of principle-plus-earnings from a defunct program to a live one. It does not put money toward the FY2027 operating deficit.

## 4. Joan's loan proposal — operations borrow $180,000 from Ruiz

Joan's note describes an interest-bearing, 18-month repayable loan from the Ruiz endowment to operations, with the donor's intent preserved [documents/treasurer-note.md].

This is precluded by Endowment Policy §4.2, on its face. §4.2 reads in full: "Donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund, whatever the term or rate of the loan" [documents/endowment-policy.md §4.2]. An interest-bearing loan from Ruiz to operations is exactly the structure §4.2 forbids, regardless of term or rate. UPMIFA does not point to a release/modification route that would authorize it either [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

I do not recommend pursuing it.

## 5. Other paths to close the $180,000 FY2027 gap

- **Operating Reserve — $240,000 (board-designated, unrestricted).** This is by far the cleanest answer. Policy §5.1 says a board-designated fund is unrestricted and the Board may change or remove its designation and use the fund for any purpose of the Foundation, by majority vote of the full Board. **No donor consent; no Attorney General notice; no waiting period** [documents/endowment-policy.md §5.1; documents/fund-statements.md]. Releasing $180,000 leaves $60,000 in reserve for the remainder of FY2027.
- **General operating fund.** The fund statement projects a $(41,300) balance at June 30, 2027 before any action, which is the gap, not a solution [documents/fund-statements.md].
- **Donor engagement.** If the Board at some point wishes broader relief on Ruiz, the cleanest long-term route is written donor consent under UPMIFA §6(a), which could authorize a defined modification. That requires outreach to Ellen M. Ruiz and is not on the October 21 timetable [documents/upmifa-summary.md §6(a); documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].
- **Endowment spending policy.** Policy §3.1 sets the 4% rate by Board action; adjusting the rate is a policy choice, not a way to redirect restricted funds to operations [documents/endowment-policy.md §3.1]. Even at a higher historic rate, Policy §3.2 channels each distribution only to the purpose of its fund.

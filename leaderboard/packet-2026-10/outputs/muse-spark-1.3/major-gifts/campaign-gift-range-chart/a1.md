## 1. Chart

Named tiers follow the counsel method [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]:

- Lead gift is one gift equal to 20% of the goal [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- Each lower tier is half the amount and double the number of the tier above [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- Tiers continue while the gift amount is $250,000 or more; a tier below $250,000 is not charted [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- Prospects needed = gifts still needed × 3 prospects per gift at tiers of $1,000,000 or more, and × 4 prospects per gift at tiers below $1,000,000 [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].
- The All other gifts line closes the chart at the goal; no prospects-needed figure is calculated for it [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].

Tier structure, tier totals, cumulative totals, and the $2,000,000 All other gifts amount (goal minus $8,000,000 named-tier total) follow sections 1.1–1.4 of the counsel method [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. Gifts in hand, gifts still needed, prospects needed, prospects identified, and gaps follow sections 2–5 of the counsel method [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].

## 2. Gifts in hand

A signed pledge agreement or gift received is a gift in hand and counts as one gift at the highest named tier whose gift amount it meets or exceeds; a gift smaller than the lowest named tier counts toward All other gifts [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. A verbal intention, oral promise, or unsigned letter of intent is not a gift in hand [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].

## 3. Prospects identified — who was counted at each tier

Each prospect is counted once as one household, family foundation, donor-advised fund, or company, placed at the highest named tier whose gift amount is at or below the bottom of the prospect’s capacity rating band [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. Prospects with inclination code N (none) are not counted [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. Inclination codes are H — high, M — medium, L — low, N — none [documents/rating-key.md]. Pipeline stage does not matter [documents/counsel-chart-method.md]. A prospect whose rating band starts below $250,000 is not counted at any named tier [documents/counsel-chart-method.md].

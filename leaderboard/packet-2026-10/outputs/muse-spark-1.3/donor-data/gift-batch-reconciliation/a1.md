## 2. Discrepancies found

### D-1 — Wrong fiscal period and gift date: G-41803, Gerald & Mae Lindgren, $1,000.00

- CRM records G-41803 as batch B-2607-01, gift_date 2026-07-02, date_received 2026-07-02, postmark 2026-06-30, Check 9014, $1,000.00, General Fund [documents/crm-july-batches.csv].
- Bank deposited Check 9014 for $1,000.00 on 2026-07-06 in RDC-0706-1 [documents/bank-deposit-detail.md].
- Standards: a mailed gift postmarked on or before June 30 belongs to the fiscal year that ended that June 30, even when received or deposited in July [documents/gift-processing-standards.md]; for envelopes received July 1–15 the gift date is the postmark date [documents/gift-processing-standards.md]; June-postmarked gifts are posted to year-end batch B-2606-YE, which stays open until August 14, 2026 — never to a July batch [documents/gift-processing-standards.md].
- Finding: gift_date should be 2026-06-30 (postmark), batch should be B-2606-YE (FY2026), not B-2607-01 (FY2027). The July acknowledgment dated 2026-07-08 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv] was sent from the wrong period.
- Check date of 2026-06-27 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv] does not decide gift date or fiscal year [documents/gift-processing-standards.md].

### D-2 — Keying error (amount): G-41809, Marisol Etxeberria, recorded $15,000.00 vs. source $1,500.00

- CRM records G-41809 as batch B-2607-02, gift_date 2026-07-06, Check 4410, $15,000.00, General Fund, ack sent 2026-07-13 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv].
- Bank deposited Check 4410 for $1,500.00 on 2026-07-10 in RDC-0710-1 [documents/bank-deposit-detail.md].
- Finding: $13,500.00 overstatement. Check images were not included in the files provided; under Standards a keying error is corrected to match the source document — the check image or the bank's deposit item [documents/gift-processing-standards.md]. This memo uses the bank's deposit item as the source.
- No file explains the extra zero; the files do not state donor intent to give $15,000.

### D-3 — Duplicate posting: G-41822, Priyanka Desai, $250.00

- CRM contains two $250.00 Card gifts for Priyanka Desai with gift_date 2026-07-14 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv]:

  - G-41822, entered 2026-07-16 by kpaulsen, Card with no reference, General Fund, no ack, notes "Donor emailed her receipt and asked for a tax letter" [documents/crm-july-batches.csv].

### D-4 — Missing reversing adjustment for returned check: G-41810, Hollis Grant, $500.00

- CRM records G-41810 as batch B-2607-02, gift_date 2026-07-06, Check 2231, $500.00, Backpack Program, ack sent 2026-07-13 [documents/crm-july-batches.csv].

# Cedar Hollow Land Trust — Duplicate-Scan Decision List — August 2026

## Review method

- Each flagged group was decided by a person applying Section 4.1–4.5. Scan score and "matched on" are leads only, not evidence, and a group can end in more than one decision [documents/data-standards.md].
- Two individual records are the same person only when (a) names are consistent, (b) at least two of birth year, email, phone, preferred address match exactly, and (c) nothing conflicts; failure on any one means different people and never merged [documents/data-standards.md].
- Name consistency allows listed short forms — Robert/Bob, Thomas/Tom, Harold/Hal, Anne/Ann, Katherine/Kate — and surnames same or differing only by keying error of one or two letters added, dropped or transposed, or a married/former surname documented by a spouse link or note [documents/data-standards.md].
- Conflict is only a different birth year, a different generational suffix Sr./Jr./II/III, or a different spouse on record; blank is never a conflict and no suffix does not conflict with a suffix [documents/data-standards.md].
- Surviving record is the lowest constituent ID among merged records [documents/data-standards.md]. Preferred address is the address with the most recent "address updated" date among merged records [documents/data-standards.md]. Surviving record keeps its own name unless another merged record carries a newer married surname, in which case it takes the newer surname and keeps the older as former name [documents/data-standards.md]. Lifetime giving after merge is the sum of lifetime giving of every merged record [documents/data-standards.md]. Spouse/household links on a retired record move to the survivor [documents/data-standards.md]. If any merged record is deceased, the survivor is marked deceased with the same date [documents/data-standards.md].
- Spouses/domestic partners are householded, never merged; where not yet linked, link them [documents/data-standards.md]. Parent/adult child or siblings sharing an address keep separate records and are not householded [documents/data-standards.md].
- Deceased flag goes only on the confirmed decedent, never copied to a spouse; when one spouse dies the household stays linked and the living spouse becomes primary contact, active and solicitable [documents/data-standards.md].
- Organizations merge only when EINs match; a club/company/congregation and its foundation are separate constituents even with shared address/email [documents/data-standards.md].

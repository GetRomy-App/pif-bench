## Method required by definitions
- Fiscal year is July 1 to June 30, named for the calendar year in which it ends. FY2026 ran July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026, and FY2027 began July 1, 2026 [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
- “This year” is FY2027 to the report date (July 1, 2026–September 15, 2026); “last year” is FY2026 [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
- Donors are counted as households sharing a household ID, not as individual records. A household gave in a fiscal year if any member has a countable gift dated in that year. Organizations are excluded [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
- A countable gift is a hard-credit gift to an individual with status Posted [documents/metrics-definitions.md]. Excluded are soft credits, refunded gifts, employer matching gifts hard-credited to the company, and organizations [documents/metrics-definitions.md]. Joint-account gifts count once for the household, whether recorded with a soft credit or split between spouses [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
- LYBUNT: household with at least one countable gift in FY2026 and none in FY2027 to date [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
- SYBUNT: household with at least one countable gift in FY2021 through FY2025, none in FY2026, and none in FY2027 to date. A household whose last countable gift is before FY2021 is lapsed, not SYBUNT. No household is both [documents/metrics-definitions.md].
- Retention: households with a countable gift in both FY2025 and FY2026 divided by households with a countable gift in FY2025, to one decimal place. FY2027 is not used [documents/metrics-definitions.md].

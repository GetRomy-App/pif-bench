# Memorandum — Use of Restricted Funds to Cover Operations

**To:** Dr. Renata Oyelaran, President, and Joan Whitacre, Treasurer, Board of Directors [documents/ed-email.md]
**From:** Malcolm Ferreira-Lund, Director of Finance and Gift Administration [documents/ed-email.md]
**Date:** September 28, 2026
**Re:** What the Ruiz Endowment and the Calloway Fund can and cannot do for the FY2027 operating deficit; steps, routes and dates; other sources

You asked for a memo on closing the Foundation's FY2027 operating-budget deficit, now showing $180,000, before the October 21 board meeting, addressing (1) moving the Ruiz Nursing Scholarship Endowment into general operations or using this year's earnings and distribution for operations, and (2) folding the old Calloway award fund into general operations, plus what notice must go to anyone if outside counsel can send a letter on Monday, October 5, and what else could close the gap [documents/ed-email.md].

**Summary answer:** Neither restricted fund can be voted into general operations on October 21. The Ruiz fund cannot be used or lent for operations at all on these facts. The Calloway fund cannot be used by October 21 and cannot be turned into general operations; at most it can be redirected to a purpose consistent with its gift after a 60-day Attorney General notice and Board action, which runs past October 21. The Operating Reserve, a $240,000 board-designated unrestricted fund, can be released by the Board on October 21 and can cover the deficit [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md].

The Finance Office projects the general operating fund at ($41,300) at June 30, 2027 before any action [documents/fund-statements.md]. The files do not explain how that projection relates to the $180,000 operating-budget deficit for FY2027 stated in your request [documents/ed-email.md]. Finance should reconcile those two figures before the board meeting; this memo does not estimate or bridge them.

FY means July 1–June 30 [documents/fund-statements.md].

## 1. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment — cannot be used for operations

**What it is.** Donor-restricted permanent endowment established March 15, 2017 by Ellen M. Ruiz with $400,000 cash in memory of Thomas A. Ruiz, R.N., market value $512,860 at August 31, 2026 [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].

**Purpose restriction.** Distributions shall be used **only for scholarships to students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) program of Alder Bend Community College**, with preference for students who are the first in their families to attend college [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. Each year the Foundation distributes the amount under its endowment spending policy as in effect from time to time, and all distributions, and all investment return, are subject to that purpose [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].

That matches Foundation policy: the annual distribution may be spent only for the purpose of the fund it comes from, and donor-restricted funds — gifts, investment return and distributions — are used only as the gift instrument directs [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Counsel's summary states the same rule: what is appropriated from an endowment fund carries the fund's purpose restriction [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

**What that means for the two proposals:**

- The Board cannot vote to "move it into general operations" — whole fund or otherwise [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
- The Board cannot vote to "use this year's earnings and distribution to cover operations until enrollment funding recovers" [documents/ed-email.md]. The FY2027 distribution must go to ADN scholarships only.

**FY2027 distribution calculation for information only.** Policy 3.1 sets the annual distribution at **4% of the average of the fund's market values at the twelve calendar quarter-ends preceding the start of that fiscal year**, rounded to the cent [documents/endowment-policy.md]. For FY2027 (starting July 1, 2026), the twelve quarter-ends are September 30, 2023 through June 30, 2026, whose values total $5,930,200 [documents/fund-statements.md]:

$468,200 + $476,900 + $483,500 + $480,100 + $492,300 + $495,700 + $489,400 + $501,800 + $510,600 + $516,300 + $508,900 + $506,500 = $5,930,200 [documents/fund-statements.md].

Average: $5,930,200 / 12 = $494,183.33; 4% = **$19,767.33** [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md]. That $19,767.33 may be spent only for ADN scholarships [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md].

**Change-in-circumstances clause does not help.** If the ADN program ceases to be offered, the Board may redirect distributions to scholarships for students in the College's health-sciences program that most closely reflects the Donor's intent [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md]. The ADN program is **active**, with 84 students enrolled for Fall 2026, 31 expected to graduate in May 2027, and approval through 2031 [documents/program-status.md]. The clause is therefore not triggered, and even if it were, it permits only health-sciences scholarships, not general operations [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md].

**Loan instead of a move is prohibited.** You asked whether operations could borrow $180,000 from the Ruiz endowment for 18 months and repay with interest once state funding recovers, leaving the fund whole [documents/treasurer-note.md]. No. Policy 4.2 provides: **donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund, whatever the term or rate of the loan** [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Interest and repayment do not cure the breach.

**Could a UPMIFA release allow operations use? No route is available on this record before October 21:**

- **(a) Donor consent.** An institution may release or modify a restriction if the donor consents in a record, but the release may not allow use for a purpose other than a charitable purpose of the institution [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The files do not state whether Ellen M. Ruiz is living, available, or willing to consent, nor whether covering this deficit would count as a charitable purpose of the institution within that rule. The files do not answer those points, so this route cannot be counted on for October 21 without further information and counsel advice. Any release would also require Board resolution [documents/endowment-policy.md].
- **(b) Court — management and investment.** A court may modify a management-or-investment restriction if it has become impracticable or wasteful, impairs management or investment, or, because of circumstances not anticipated by the donor, modification will further the fund's purposes, with notice to the Attorney General and opportunity to be heard [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The restriction you seek to change is a purpose restriction (ADN scholarships only), not a management or investment restriction, and the files contain no facts meeting that test.
- **(c) Court — purpose (cy pres).** A court may modify purpose or use restriction if the charitable purpose or restriction becomes unlawful, impracticable, impossible to achieve, or wasteful, in a manner consistent with the charitable purposes in the gift instrument, with Attorney General notice and opportunity to be heard [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The ADN purpose is being achieved — the program is active and enrolling [documents/program-status.md]. Counsel's practice note states that a budget shortfall at the institution does not, by itself, make a fund's purpose impracticable or impossible [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
- **(d) Small, old funds without court.** Available only if the fund totals less than $25,000, more than 20 years have elapsed since establishment, and the use remains consistent with the charitable purposes in the gift instrument, after 60 days' notice to the Attorney General [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The Ruiz fund fails the first two tests: $512,860 at August 31, 2026 and established in 2017 [documents/fund-statements.md].

**Steps and dates for Ruiz.** There are no steps that make Ruiz money available for operations by October 21. The only Board action consistent with the files on October 21 is to appropriate the FY2027 distribution of about $19,767.33 for ADN scholarships as prudent for that purpose [documents/upmifa-summary.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/fund-statements.md].

## 2. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund — cannot be folded into operations; limited redirection only after 60-day notice

**What it is.** Donor-restricted fund established May 26, 1994 when the Board accepted Margaret E. Calloway's $5,000 gift on the terms of her May 12, 1994 letter, market value $18,420 at August 31, 2026 [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md] [documents/fund-statements.md]. The letter directs that the fund be kept and invested, the earnings of which shall provide an **annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the College's Secretarial Studies certificate program** [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]. Its earnings have accumulated in the fund, and it has made no award since 2011 [documents/fund-statements.md].

**Why the purpose cannot now be performed.** The Secretarial Studies certificate was closed by the Board of Trustees on June 30, 2011 after last admissions in Fall 2010, and no student can now enroll in or graduate from it [documents/program-status.md]. The files therefore support a Board determination that the restriction is impossible to achieve or impracticable/wasteful in its current form, which is the predicate for both court cy pres and small-fund release [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. That determination itself requires counsel advice and Board action; a budget shortfall alone would not suffice [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

**What the Board cannot do on October 21:** vote by majority to fold the fund into general operations as if it were unrestricted [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Donor-restricted funds are used only as the gift instrument directs, and a restriction may be released or modified only as UPMIFA allows and only by Board resolution [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Board-designated-fund rules (majority vote, no donor consent, no Attorney General notice) do not apply to this donor-restricted fund [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

**Available routes:**

- **(a) Donor consent — unavailable.** Mrs. Calloway died February 9, 2002, left no other gift to the Foundation, and her letter names no successor or advisor [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]. There is no donor of record to consent.
- **(d) Small, old funds without court — appears to fit, but not by October 21 and not for general operations.** All three statutory tests appear met on the files: total value $18,420 (less than $25,000); established May 26, 1994 (more than 20 years before 2026); and the Secretarial Studies purpose can no longer be achieved as described above [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/program-status.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. If the Board so determines, the institution, **60 days after notification to the Attorney General**, may release or modify the restriction in whole or part [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The property must then be used **in a manner consistent with the charitable purposes expressed in the gift instrument** — here, helping "a young person entering office work" begin "with confidence and a little help" through an annual award [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The files do not define what the Attorney General or a court would accept as consistent, so counsel must advise whether any particular new use qualifies. Folding the money into general operations as unrestricted operations support does not on its face satisfy that consistency requirement; the files provide no authority for that use under this subsection.
- **(c) Court cy pres — longer route, same consistency limit.** Upon application, with Attorney General notice and opportunity to be heard, a court may modify purpose or use restriction found unlawful, impracticable, impossible or wasteful, in a manner consistent with the charitable purposes in the gift instrument [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. The files give no timeline for a court proceeding, but it necessarily involves application, notice and hearing and cannot be completed by October 21. It also could not authorize general-operations use inconsistent with the gift's charitable purposes [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Counsel must advise.

The Office Administration (A.A.S.), created in Fall 2012 as the College's program preparing students for administrative and office careers with 47 students enrolled for Fall 2026 [documents/program-status.md], is the kind of consistent purpose counsel and the Board may wish to consider as an example. The files do not decide that question; the Board cannot assume it without counsel advice and the required procedure.

**Steps and dates for Calloway:**

1. President authorizes outside counsel to send any UPMIFA notice required before the Board meets [documents/endowment-policy.md]. You noted counsel can send a letter on Monday, October 5 [documents/ed-email.md].
2. If counsel sends the Attorney General notification on October 5 under §6(d), the 60-day period runs to **December 4, 2026** [documents/upmifa-summary.md] [documents/ed-email.md]. The fund cannot be released or reused before that date expires.
3. On October 21, the Board can (a) record its determination that the Secretarial Studies restriction is impossible to achieve / impracticable, (b) direct and ratify the notice, and (c) defer final release/modification and spending decisions to a later meeting after December 4, by resolution as policy requires [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md].
4. Even then, the amount at stake ($18,420) [documents/fund-statements.md] would not close a $180,000 deficit [documents/ed-email.md], and it could not be spent as general operations under the consistency rule [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

## 3. What else could close the gap

**Operating Reserve — available October 21.** The $240,000 Operating Reserve is **board-designated (unrestricted)**, designated by board resolution 2019-07 and releasable by majority vote of the full board [documents/fund-statements.md]. A board-designated fund is unrestricted; the Board may change or remove its designation and use the fund for any Foundation purpose by majority vote of the full Board, with no donor consent and no Attorney General notice [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Section 6 of UPMIFA does not apply to it [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

The Board can therefore vote on October 21 to undesignate up to $180,000 of the $240,000 reserve and apply it to FY2027 operations [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/fund-statements.md] [documents/ed-email.md]. The files do not address replenishment terms, investment effects, or whether the 2019-07 resolution imposes additional conditions beyond majority vote; the Board should have counsel and finance confirm the resolution text before voting.

No other fund in the statements is available for operations on these files: the Ruiz and Calloway funds are donor-restricted as above, and the general operating fund itself is the fund in deficit [documents/fund-statements.md].

**What the files do not provide:** donor contact information or consent, Attorney General notice forms or addresses, court timelines or costs, fundraising or borrowing options outside these funds, or authority to run an operating deficit. Those questions require counsel and finance advice, not assumptions.

### Recommended motions for October 21

- Decline to appropriate Ruiz principal, earnings or the FY2027 distribution for operations; appropriate the $19,767.33 distribution for ADN scholarships only, subject to prudence review [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/fund-statements.md].
- Decline to fold Calloway into operations; authorize and ratify the October 5 Attorney General notice and direct counsel to bring back a §6(d) or cy pres plan for a use consistent with Mrs. Calloway's office-work purpose after the 60-day period [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md] [documents/upmifa-summary.md] [documents/endowment-policy.md].
- Release the needed amount from the board-designated Operating Reserve by majority vote of the full Board to balance FY2027 operations [documents/endowment-policy.md] [documents/fund-statements.md].

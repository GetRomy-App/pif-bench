## 1. Rules applied to this review

- Every contribution receives a written acknowledgment, whatever its amount, within 30 days of receipt [documents/receipting-policy.md]. Payments connected with a benefit event are acknowledged in one batch after the event, once the CFO has approved the final good-faith estimates, and the CFO or Director of Development reviews every event batch before it is sent [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- The CFO's estimates in the final fact sheet dated 2026-08-28 are the values to use on every acknowledgment [documents/gala-fact-sheet.md]. The good-faith estimate is fair market value — what comparable dinner, open bar and live entertainment would cost a member of the public — not cost to us and not ticket price [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Acknowledgment contents follow Internal Revenue Code §170(f)(8) and §6115 as stated in policy §3.1–§3.2 [documents/receipting-policy.md]. We apply the §170(f)(8) content rules to every acknowledgment, including gifts under $250 [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- When payment of more than $75 is partly contribution and partly payment for goods or services, we must inform the donor the deductible amount is limited to the excess over the value of goods or services and give our good-faith estimate; the $75 test is on the payment, not the deductible portion [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Raffle ticket purchases are not deductible contributions, are never included in a contribution amount, and no acknowledgment states a deductible amount for them [documents/receipting-policy.md]. Where one payment combines a gift with a purchase, each part is treated separately, and any mention of raffle must say raffle purchases are not charitable contributions and are not deductible [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Name in printed program, stage signage, closing slide or annual report is donor recognition, assigned no value and not described as a benefit [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Sponsorships are valued benefit by benefit: every seat carries the per-guest seat value, every other benefit carries its own approved per-guest value [documents/receipting-policy.md]. A seat a sponsor fills with a guest is the sponsor's benefit on the sponsor's acknowledgment, not deducted from any separate gift the guest makes [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Declined benefit: donor who declines in writing at time of payment — for this gala by checking the reply card's "I will not attend; please do not reserve a seat" box — and for whom no seat is reserved received no goods or services; full payment is a contribution [documents/receipting-policy.md]. Unused benefit: donor who paid, had a seat reserved and available, but did not attend, or who asks after the event to be treated as a gift, still received the benefit; deductible is not increased [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Every acknowledgment must state donor name and date received, amount of cash received, and either "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution" **or** description of goods/services, good-faith estimate, §3.2 disclosure where payment is more than $75, and resulting deductible amount [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- No acknowledgment may describe payment as fully deductible when goods/services were provided, state a deductible amount for raffle or count raffle in contribution, state a value for noncash property, or offer tax advice [documents/receipting-policy.md].
- Correcting a template, benefit value or receipt amount requires CFO's or Director of Development's approval before send [documents/receipting-policy.md]. This review is that written approval for the corrections below only. Staff do not depart from policy without CFO written approval [documents/receipting-policy.md].

### 3. Why no DAF dollars may go to the gala table

Kestrel's Conditions state: The grant may not be used, in whole or in part, to provide any benefit to the fund's advisors, their family members or any other person — including event tickets or tables, dinners, memberships, or other goods or services — and may not be used to satisfy a legally binding pledge or other obligation of any individual [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].

Our policy restates the same bar:

- Federal law (Internal Revenue Code §4967) imposes penalty taxes when a DAF distribution provides an advisor, or a member of the advisor's family, with more than an incidental benefit. We treat gala seats, dinners and memberships as more than incidental [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
- DAF funds may not pay for any part of a ticket, table, sponsorship, membership or other benefit — neither the full price nor the portion above fair market value. We do not split ("bifurcate") a benefit's price between a DAF grant and the advisor [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
- An advisor who wants seats pays for them personally (personal check, card or other personal funds — never a DAF) [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].
- We do not accept a DAF grant designated for an event purchase, and we never redirect any part of a DAF grant to one [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md].

Therefore:

- Do not move $3,000 of the $15,000 grant to the gala invoice [documents/halvorsen-email.md] [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md].
- Do not accept a second, smaller grant just for the table [documents/halvorsen-email.md]. If such a check arrives, hold in suspense and alert Jordan and the CFO; the grant letter directs us to return the check If your organization cannot accept this grant on these terms, or if it will not be used for the stated purpose [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].
- We can accept this $15,000 grant on its terms only by using all of it for the stated Studio Wing Campaign purpose [documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md], which is consistent with a non-binding statement of intent as described above.

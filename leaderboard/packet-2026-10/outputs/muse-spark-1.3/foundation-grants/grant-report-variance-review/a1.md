### 3.2 What the ledger shows and the one required removal

- Total charged to Fund 310 for Year 1 in the ledger is **$119,306.00** [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md], which agrees to the sum of the ledger lines [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].
- The April journal entry JE-0412 moved 40 hours of the coordinator's March time ($1,240 of salary and $248 of fringe) to Fund 220, the city workforce contract, where that work belonged; the negative amounts are in the export [documents/finance-note-from-renee.md] [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].
- The ledger includes AP-2242, Harborview Taproom, "Spring tutor training wrap-up session — beer and wine service," **$640.00** [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].
- Grant funds may not be used for alcoholic beverages [documents/grant-agreement.md].
- A cost that is not allowable may not be reported as a grant expense, and any unallowable cost charged to the grant must be removed from the grant's accounts and paid from unrestricted funds [documents/grant-agreement.md].
- Therefore the $640.00 must be removed from grant expenses and paid from unrestricted funds; allowable Tutor training and total expenses are $640.00 below the ledger-charged amounts [documents/grant-agreement.md] [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv].
- Other ledger descriptions do not identify lobbying, fundraising solicitation, pre-July 1, 2025 or post-grant-period costs, or any single equipment item over $5,000; the ledger descriptions for the remaining items are bus passes, childcare stipends, curriculum items, trainer fees and manuals, coffee/juice/snacks for a winter training day, dinner buffet for a spring tutor training wrap-up session, mileage, payroll/fringe allocations, and quarterly evaluation services [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv]. Whether the dinner buffet qualifies as food at an instructional or training event under Section 6.2 is not further documented in the files beyond its description as a "Spring tutor training wrap-up session — dinner buffet, 34 guests" [documents/fund-310-ledger-year1.csv] [documents/grant-agreement.md].

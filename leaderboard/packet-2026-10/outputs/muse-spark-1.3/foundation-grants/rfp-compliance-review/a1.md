## 2. Fatal issues — would cause return / non-consideration / screening failure

### Issue 1 — Request exceeds the hard maximum
- Draft requests $83,847 on the cover sheet and in the budget table [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Lucia notes the RFP says up to $75K but cites an August info-session comment about "flexibility for really strong proposals" [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
- RFP states requests may not exceed $75,000 and requests above $75,000 will not be considered; the request is direct plus indirect [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- **Fix:** Reduce total request to ≤$75,000 and make cover sheet, budget table, and budget narrative agree, as RFP requires agreement among those three [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. A verbal flexibility comment does not override the written "will not be considered" rule. See §3: with only the currently documented match, the request must in fact be ≤$68,000.

### Issue 2 — Indirect rate exceeds cap, plus $1,000 arithmetic error
- Draft charges indirect at 15% of total direct costs, described as "our federal de minimis rate, which we apply to all our grants" [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Lucia confirms 15% is the federal de minimis rate used on all grants [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
- RFP caps indirect at 10% of total direct costs in the request [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. The federal rate is irrelevant to this cap.
- Math error: line items sum to $71,910, not the $72,910 shown as total direct costs: $48,000 + $10,560 + $5,400 + $3,850 + $2,600 + $1,500 = $71,910 [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. The $10,937 indirect equals 15% of the overstated $72,910, not of the correct sum.
- **Fix:** Correct direct-cost total, then cap indirect at ≤10% of that corrected total. Update table, narrative language, and cover-sheet total so all three agree [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. See §3 for compliant ceilings. The $3,850 supplies, $2,600 training, $1,500 interpretation (60 hours × $25), $5,400 mileage (12,000 × $0.45), $48,000 personnel (2 × 0.75 FTE × $32,000/FTE), and 22% fringe ($10,560) inputs are as stated in draft [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]; do not change unit bases without new documentation.

### Issue 3 — Cash match is short when correctly counted; two of four lines do not count
- Draft claims $51,000 total match, 61% of request, from: Valencia County Health Council $20,000 cash; board-designated reserve $14,000 cash; Rio Grande Medical Supply $12,000 in-kind; Socorro Rotary $5,000 cash "Letter to follow" [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Lucia describes the same $51,000/61% and calls Rotary "a verbal yes" with board vote October 14 and letter after [documents/cover-note-from-lucia.md].
- RFP requires cash match ≥$1 for every $2 requested (50% of request), cash only for the 2027 project period from non-Foundation sources (own unrestricted/board-designated may count), each source documented by a signed letter uploaded as Attachment E by the deadline; undocumented match will not be counted; in-kind may be described but does not count [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- Application to v3:
  - Rio Grande $12,000 is twenty monitors/ten glucometers at $12,000 FMV per signed 2026-09-03 letter [documents/attachments-folder.md] [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] — in-kind, does not count toward match [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md]. It may remain described in the budget narrative [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md] [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
  - Rotary $5,000 is not documented: listing states "No file yet from the Socorro Rotary Club" and forwards Ed Trujillo's 2026-09-16 email stating confidence, board vote October 14, letter after [documents/attachments-folder.md]. A post-deadline letter cannot be counted because only letters uploaded by the deadline count [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  - Countable at deadline is therefore $34,000: $20,000 Council signed 2026-09-08 for calendar year 2027 plus $14,000 board-designated reserve signed 2026-09-15 for calendar year 2027 [documents/attachments-folder.md] [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. Both are cash for the grant period January 1–December 31, 2027 [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md], and own funds may count [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
  - $34,000 is 40.6% of the $83,847 request, below the 50% ($41,923.50) required for that request [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md] [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md].
- **Fix:** Remove the $12,000 in-kind and the $5,000 pending Rotary from the match table; list only the two signed cash letters ($34,000). Then either (a) secure at least $3,500 in additional signed cash for 2027 uploaded by the deadline to support a $75,000 request, or (b) cut the request to ≤$68,000 (2 × $34,000) if no new match is secured. Do not rely on Rotary: its vote is October 14, after the October 1 deadline [documents/attachments-folder.md] [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].

### Issue 4 — Required Sustainability Plan section is missing
- RFP requires five sections in order under these headings, including 5. Sustainability Plan — how work continues after the grant period, including future funding sources; omitting a required section causes return without review [documents/rfp-2027-community-health-access.md].
- Draft contains only §§1–4 (Background, Need, Project Description, Evaluation Plan) [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md]. The single sentence in Evaluation that "we expect to sustain the CHW positions through Medicaid managed-care reimbursement" [documents/draft-proposal-v3.md] is not a Section 5 under the required heading and does not satisfy the "sources of future funding" requirement.

The files do not provide any additional future-funding commitments beyond that one Medicaid-reimbursement expectation, so new content must be developed and sourced before submission.

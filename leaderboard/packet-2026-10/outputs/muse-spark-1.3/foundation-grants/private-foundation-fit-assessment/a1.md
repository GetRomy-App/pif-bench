# Fresh Routes — Foundation Fit Assessment
**Millstream Community Food Bank · Prepared for the Development Committee (September 24 meeting)**
**Assessment date: September 15, 2026**

## 1. Project and policy basis

Fresh Routes has two parts: (1) Capital — 2,400-square-foot cooler/freezer addition and 26-foot refrigerated box truck, and (2) Program — two years of weekly mobile markets at twelve stops with SNAP enrollment help [documents/project-summary.md].

Budget: cold-storage addition $485,000; refrigerated box truck $155,000; capital subtotal $640,000; program subtotal $260,000 (coordinator $148,000 + driver/assistant $66,000 + fuel/maintenance/insurance $28,000 + outreach $18,000); project total $900,000 [documents/project-summary.md].

Funding committed: $150,000 board-designated facilities reserve for capital, by board vote 2026-06-11 [documents/project-summary.md]. Remaining need: capital $490,000; program $260,000; total $750,000 [documents/project-summary.md].

Construction bids for the cold-storage addition came in between $471,000 and $498,000; the budget figure of $485,000 stands [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]. Construction is planned for March–August 2027; markets start in June 2027 [documents/project-summary.md]. The state agriculture department's capital round will not reopen until 2027 [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].

The board asked staff to assess three foundations suggested September 10 — Marwood Family Foundation (suggested by Ms. Pfautz), Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust (suggested by Dr. Calloway), and Garrison Ridge Foundation (suggested by Mr. Ibarra) — and bring a prioritized recommendation to the Development Committee on September 24 [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].

This assessment follows the Foundation Grants Policy adopted 2024-11-14, revised 2026-03-12 [documents/grants-policy.md]. Staff read each foundation's most recent Form 990-PF and current website; where the two differ on how or when to apply, the website is followed and the difference noted [documents/grants-policy.md].

## 2. Marwood Family Foundation

### Totals from most recent 990-PF (tax year 2025)

- Total grants paid during the year: **$708,000**, calculated as the sum of grants paid [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- Number of grants paid: **10** [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- Grants to Pennsylvania organizations (by recipient city/state): **10 of 10** — Ephrata, PA; Lititz, PA; Columbia, PA; Manheim, PA; Elizabethtown, PA; Lancaster, PA; Strasburg, PA; New Holland, PA; Quarryville, PA; and Mount Joy, PA [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].

The return reports fair market value of assets at year end $15,612,780 and total expenses and disbursements (charitable-disbursements column) $804,400, including operating and administrative expenses $96,400 [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].

### Fit

- **Mission: fit.** The Foundation makes grants "to strengthen Lancaster County: feeding neighbors who are hungry, keeping families housed, and helping children learn" [documents/marwood-website.md]. Its 2025 paid list includes a community pantry, Meals on Wheels, a soup kitchen/pantry building, a food cupboard, a food shelf van, and a pantry loading dock/shelving, among other grants [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. Ms. Pfautz noted it "has paid for freezers, a building and a refrigerated van at county pantries" [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].
- **Geography: fit.** The 2025 grants-paid list includes at least one grant to an organization in Pennsylvania — in fact all ten [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. Published guidelines name Lancaster County as the giving focus [documents/marwood-website.md].
- **Type of support: fit for both components.** Capital support is land, buildings, construction, renovation, equipment and vehicles; program support is program costs and general operating support [documents/grants-policy.md]. The paid list includes capital purposes — walk-in freezer and cold-storage expansion $85,000; commercial kitchen renovation $60,000; purchase of building for soup kitchen and pantry $150,000; purchase of refrigerated van $48,000; loading dock and warehouse shelving $45,000 — and program/operating purposes — general operating support $25,000; emergency rent assistance fund $100,000; children's summer reading program $35,000; after-school program $40,000 [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md]. Published guidelines state "[m]any of our grants help organizations build, repair and equip the places where they serve" [documents/marwood-website.md], which expressly invites capital.

### How and when it accepts requests

- **Does not accept unsolicited requests.** The 990-PF box stating it "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds" is checked [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- The website states: "The Marwood Family Foundation does not accept unsolicited proposals, letters of inquiry or applications. Our trustees identify the organizations the Foundation supports" and "Grant requests sent to this address will not be considered" [documents/marwood-website.md].
- Under the Policy, a foundation described in either place receives no unsolicited proposal, letter of inquiry, application or other written request for funds from Millstream — from staff, or from a board member on Millstream's behalf — and strong mission fit does not change this [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **Next deadline after September 15, 2026: none.** The files provide no application form, submission address, or deadline because none is offered [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] [documents/marwood-website.md].

### Board/staff relationship

- **Margaret W. Lindqvist — Trustee of the Marwood Family Foundation, 1.0 hour per week** [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md].
- Ms. Lindqvist is a Millstream Director (retired elementary school principal, Lititz) [documents/board-roster.md].
- The other Marwood trustees listed are Charles E. Marwood (President and Trustee), Diane Marwood Esh (Secretary and Trustee), and Peter J. Hostetter (Treasurer and Trustee), none of whom appears on the Millstream board roster or as a Millstream staff liaison [documents/marwood-990pf-2025.md] [documents/board-roster.md].

### Recommended approach

- **No unsolicited request.** Do not send a proposal, LOI, application, or other written request [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **Relationship-only approach.** For a foundation covered by Section 2, the board-member relationship is the only approach: the board member may ask the foundation's trustees whether the foundation would welcome a conversation with Millstream or would invite a request [documents/grants-policy.md]. Staff prepare and send nothing to the foundation unless it invites a request in writing [documents/grants-policy.md].
- If Ms. Lindqvist is willing, she may privately ask Marwood's trustees whether they would welcome a conversation about Fresh Routes or invite a request. She takes no part in the foundation's decision on a Millstream request [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **No ask range is presented at this time** because an unsolicited request is prohibited. The files do not indicate whether the trustees would invite a Millstream request.

### Totals from most recent 990-PF (tax year 2025)

- Total grants paid during the year: **$390,000**, calculated as the sum of grants paid [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
- Number of grants paid: **12** [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
- Grants to Pennsylvania organizations (by recipient city/state): **0 of 12** [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Recipients are in Greensboro, NC; Lancaster, SC; Roanoke, VA; Charleston, SC; Danville, VA; Wilmington, NC; Greenville, SC; Norfolk, VA; Hickory, NC; Asheville, NC; Rock Hill, SC; and York, SC [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. The return reports fair market value of assets at year end $9,847,215 and total expenses and disbursements (charitable-disbursements column) $488,450, including operating and administrative expenses $98,450 [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].

### Fit

- **Mission: fit.** The Trust "makes grants to nonprofit organizations that relieve hunger and widen access to fresh, healthy food — food banks and pantries, meal programs, gleaning and food-rescue groups, community gardens and mobile markets" and "fund[s] programs, operations, equipment and facilities" [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Its history is as the philanthropy of Thomas A. Calloway (1921–2004), co-founder with Walter Deane of Calloway-Deane Markets, with Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company as corporate trustee alongside Deane family members [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Its 2025 paid list includes refrigerated warehouse expansion $90,000 and purchase of refrigerated truck $65,000, plus mobile pantry, mobile farmers market, gleaning, meals, gardens and operating support [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Dr. Calloway suggested it as making grants for hunger relief and fresh-food access including refrigerated trucks [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].
- **Geography: not a fit at this time.** No 2025 grant paid went to an organization in Pennsylvania [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Published guidelines do not name Lancaster County or Pennsylvania among giving areas; they require only 501(c)(3) public-charity status and state no grants to individuals [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. The Trust's Philadelphia, PA mailing address (c/o Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company, 1801 Arch Street) does not establish fit under the Policy [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md] [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **Type of support: would fit both components if geography were established.** The paid list includes capital purposes (refrigerated warehouse expansion; purchase of refrigerated truck) and program/operating purposes (e.g., general operating support $15,000 and $20,000; culinary job-training $35,000; farm gleaning $25,000; home-delivered meals $30,000; mobile farmers market $40,000) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Published guidelines expressly invite "programs, operations, equipment and facilities" [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].

### How and when it accepts requests

- The 990-PF box for preselected-only/no unsolicited requests is **not** checked [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md]. Applications go to the Grants Administrator c/o Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company, Philadelphia, (215) 555-0143, by online application, with submission deadlines April 15 and October 15, limited to 501(c)(3) organizations and no grants to individuals [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
- The website states the online application opens March 1 and September 1; deadlines April 15 (decisions in June) and October 15 (decisions in December); grants typically $10,000 to $90,000; applicants must be 501(c)(3) public charities [documents/calloway-deane-website.md]. Follow the website as more current; it adds the opening dates, decision months, and typical range, with no conflict on deadlines [documents/grants-policy.md].
- **Next deadline after September 15, 2026: October 15, 2026** (December decisions) [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].
- Millstream is a 501(c)(3) public charity at 2240 Manor Ridge Road, Lancaster, PA 17603, EIN 23-7714402 [documents/project-summary.md], meeting the stated eligibility [documents/calloway-deane-website.md].

### Board/staff relationship

- No Millstream board member or staff member listed as a trustee, director or officer of the Trust in the files. Trustees/managers are Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company (Corporate Trustee), Harriet Deane Pruitt (Individual Trustee, Charlotte, NC), and Samuel R. Deane (Individual Trustee, Richmond, VA) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], none of whom appears on the Millstream board roster or as executive director or development director [documents/board-roster.md] [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md]. Dr. Owen M. Calloway is a Millstream Director and pediatrician [documents/board-roster.md]; the files do not show him serving the Trust in any capacity.

### Recommended approach

- **Prepare no proposal or application now** because the Trust is not a geographic fit and the Policy bars a proposal or LOI to a foundation that is not a geographic fit [documents/grants-policy.md].
- Because its guidelines state no geographic limit, the Director of Development may make a single inquiry, by email or telephone, to ask whether it would consider a request from a Pennsylvania organization, and must record the answer; only a yes in writing makes it a geographic fit [documents/grants-policy.md]. Suggested contact: grants@callowaydeanetrust.org or (215) 555-0143 [documents/calloway-deane-website.md] [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md].
- **No ask range is set.** A comparable grant is a grant paid during the year to a Pennsylvania organization whose work is food access, hunger relief or nutrition [documents/grants-policy.md]. The Trust made no such grants in 2025 (zero Pennsylvania grants) [documents/calloway-deane-990pf-2025.md], so under the Policy no range is set and the question is referred to the Executive Director — and only becomes relevant if a written yes on geography is first obtained [documents/grants-policy.md]. The files do not answer whether the Trust would consider a Pennsylvania request.

### Fit

- **Mission: fit for the program component.** The Foundation funds "Basic needs — food access and nutrition, housing stability, and help for families in crisis," plus youth and community health [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Its 2025 paid list includes mobile pantry operations, senior congregate meals, pantry operating support, summer meals, SNAP outreach, mobile market for fresh produce, and nutrition education [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. Mr. Ibarra understood it funds basic-needs programs in south-central Pennsylvania [documents/board-minutes-excerpt.md].
- **Geography: fit.** The paid list includes twelve Pennsylvania grants [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. Published guidelines name Adams, Cumberland, Dauphin, Lancaster, Lebanon, Perry and York counties, Pennsylvania, as the giving area [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md] [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
- **Type of support: fit for program only; not for capital.** Program support appears in the paid list (e.g., general operating support $40,000; mobile pantry operations $60,000; senior congregate meals $25,000; summer meals $20,000; SNAP outreach $15,000; mobile market $35,000; nutrition classes $30,000) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. No grant paid during the year states a capital purpose (land, buildings, construction, renovation, equipment or vehicles) [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. Published guidelines state "Our grants support programs and the organizations that run them" [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md], which does not expressly invite capital. Ask only for a type appearing in the paid list unless guidelines expressly invite it [documents/grants-policy.md]. Therefore ask only for program (mobile markets), not for the cold-storage addition or truck.

### How and when it accepts requests

- The 990-PF box for preselected-only/no unsolicited requests is **not** checked [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md]. The 990-PF directs a letter of inquiry of no more than two pages to Teresa M. Albright, Executive Director, Harrisburg, (717) 555-0186, on a rolling basis [documents/garrison-ridge-990pf-2025.md].
- The website, which is more current and is followed here, uses a two-step process — LOI of no more than two pages through the online portal; invited full proposals only ("We do not accept full proposals without an invitation") — and since January 2026 reviews LOIs in two cycles a year instead of rolling: Spring (LOI due March 1, invited proposals due mid-April, decisions June) and Fall (LOI due September 1, invited proposals due mid-October, decisions December); if a deadline falls on a weekend, LOIs are due the next business day [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Note the difference from the 990-PF's rolling basis [documents/grants-policy.md].
- Most grants range from $10,000 to $75,000 [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Contact: grants@garrisonridgefoundation.org, (717) 555-0186 [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md].
- Fall 2026 LOIs closed September 1; invited organizations will hear by September 18; invited full proposals are due October 16, 2026 [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. Millstream did not submit a Fall 2026 LOI in the files provided.
- **Next deadline after September 15, 2026: Spring LOI due March 1, 2027, 5:00 p.m. Eastern** (invited full proposals mid-April; decisions June) [documents/garrison-ridge-website.md]. The files do not give the exact mid-April 2027 invited-proposal date.

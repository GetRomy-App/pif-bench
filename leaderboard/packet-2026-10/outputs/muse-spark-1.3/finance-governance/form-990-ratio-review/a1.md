## 1. Basis

This memo was prepared by the CFO's office for the finance committee chair, as required by the policy [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

The fiscal year is the calendar year ended December 31, 2025 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md] [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]. Indicators are computed from the Form 990 filed for the fiscal year and the notes to that year's audited financial statements [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. The filed 2025 Form 990 was filed electronically 2026-08-11 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].

Gifts in kind means contributed nonfinancial assets as reported in the audit notes — donated food and other goods, donated professional services, and donated use of facilities [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Gifts in kind are removed from every revenue and expense figure used in these indicators, in whichever Form 990 line and functional column they are reported [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Balance-sheet figures are end-of-year figures (Form 990 Part X, column (B)) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Percentages are reported to one decimal place, cost to raise a dollar to the nearest cent, and months to one decimal place, with the rounded figure compared to the target [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

## 3. Indicator 3.1 — Program expense ratio

Definition: (Program service expenses − gifts in kind expensed in program services) ÷ (Total functional expenses − all gifts in kind expensed) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

Program service expenses is Part IX, line 25, column (B) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Total functional expenses is Part IX, line 25, column (A) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

Inputs:

* Program service expenses: $19,066,000 — Part IX, line 25, column (B) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
* Total functional expenses: $20,515,000 — Part IX, line 25, column (A) [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
* Gifts in kind expensed in program services: $14,180,000 — donated food distributed in 2025, reported in Part IX, line 24a [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]. The audit notes state donated food of $14,250,000 was recognized as revenue and used for distribution to partner agencies and expensed in program services [documents/audit-notes-2025.md], of which $14,180,000 was distributed and reported in Part IX, line 24a, with the remaining $70,000 increasing donated-food inventory [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]. Donated-food inventory increased from $340,000 in 2024 to $410,000 in 2025 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Gifts in kind expensed in management and general: $95,000 — donated legal services, expensed in legal fees, Part IX, line 11b [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]. Part IX, line 11b totals $104,000, of which $95,000 was donated and $9,000 paid [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Gifts in kind expensed in fundraising: $0 — the audit notes assign donated food to program services and donated legal services to management and general, with no gifts in kind assigned to fundraising [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* All gifts in kind expensed: $14,180,000 + $95,000 = $14,275,000.
* Volunteers contributed about 61,000 hours in the warehouse in 2025, which do not require specialized skills and are not recognized in the financial statements [documents/audit-notes-2025.md], and therefore require no adjustment.

Calculation:

* Numerator: $19,066,000 − $14,180,000 = $4,886,000.
* Denominator: $20,515,000 − $14,275,000 = $6,240,000.
* Ratio: $4,886,000 ÷ $6,240,000 = 78.3013%, reported as **78.3%**.

Target: 80.0% or higher [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. **Target not met.**

## 4. Indicator 3.2 — Cost to raise a dollar

Definition: Fundraising expenses ÷ Contributions [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

Fundraising expenses is Part IX, line 25, column (D), less any gifts in kind expensed in fundraising [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Contributions is Part VIII, line 1h, less line 1g (noncash contributions), less every bequest and other distribution from a decedent's estate received in the year [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Bequests are excluded because they are non-recurring and are not the product of the year's fundraising spending [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

Inputs:

* Fundraising expenses, Part IX, line 25, column (D): $866,300 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
* Less gifts in kind expensed in fundraising: $0, as described in Section 3 above [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Fundraising expenses for indicator: $866,300.
* Contributions, Part VIII, line 1h: $18,930,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
* Less noncash contributions, Part VIII, line 1g: $14,345,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. The audit notes report total contributed nonfinancial assets recognized as revenue of $14,345,000, comprising donated food of $14,250,000 and donated legal services of $95,000 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md], with both items reported in Form 990 Part VIII, line 1f, and line 1g [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Less bequests: $600,000 — distribution received in August 2025 from the Estate of Harriet M. Okafor, with no donor restriction, reported in contributions (Form 990 Part VIII, line 1f) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]. No other bequests or estate distributions were received in 2025 or 2024 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Contributions for indicator: $18,930,000 − $14,345,000 − $600,000 = $3,985,000.

Calculation:

* $866,300 ÷ $3,985,000 = $0.2174, reported as **$0.22**.

Target: $0.20 or less [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. **Target not met.**

The $0.25 threshold for commissioning an external review of fundraising costs before the next budget is adopted was not exceeded [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

## 5. Indicator 3.3 — Months of operating reserve

Definition: Operating reserve ÷ (Annual operating expenses ÷ 12) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

Operating reserve is net assets without donor restrictions (Part X, line 27) less land, buildings and equipment, net (Part X, line 10c) [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Net assets with donor restrictions are never counted as reserve, and all other net assets without donor restrictions count, whatever their source [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. Annual operating expenses is total functional expenses (Part IX, line 25, column (A)) less depreciation (Part IX, line 22, column (A)) less all gifts in kind expensed [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

Inputs:

* Net assets without donor restrictions, Part X, line 27, end of year: $6,612,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
* Land, buildings and equipment, net, Part X, line 10c, end of year: $5,108,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. Cost was $9,240,000 less accumulated depreciation of $4,132,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md], consistent with total cost of $9,240,000 less accumulated depreciation of $4,132,000 in the audit notes [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Operating reserve: $6,612,000 − $5,108,000 = $1,504,000.
* Total functional expenses, Part IX, line 25, column (A): $20,515,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md].
* Less depreciation, Part IX, line 22, column (A): $446,000 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]. Depreciation expense was $446,000 in 2025 [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Less all gifts in kind expensed: $14,275,000, as derived in Section 3 above [documents/audit-notes-2025.md].
* Annual operating expenses: $20,515,000 − $446,000 − $14,275,000 = $5,794,000.
* Monthly operating expenses: $5,794,000 ÷ 12 = $482,833.33.

Calculation:

* $1,504,000 ÷ ($5,794,000 ÷ 12) = 3.1149 months, reported as **3.1 months**.

Target: 3.0 months or more [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]. **Target met.**

The reserve is above 1.5 months, so the treasurer's ten-day notification to the full board is not triggered [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md].

## 3. What the policy requires

- **Interested Person** means any director, principal officer, or member of a committee with board-delegated powers who has a direct or indirect Financial Interest [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Financial Interest** includes, directly or indirectly through business, investment, or a Family Member: (a) ownership or investment interest in any entity with which the Theatre has a transaction; (b) compensation arrangement with the Theatre or with any entity with which the Theatre has a transaction; or (c) potential ownership, investment, or compensation arrangement with any entity with which the Theatre is negotiating, including any entity that submitted a bid [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Family Member** includes spouse or domestic partner; ancestors; brothers and sisters (whole or half blood) and their spouses; and children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren and their spouses [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- A Financial Interest is not necessarily a conflict; the board decides whether a conflict exists [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Duty to disclose:** disclose existence and all material facts in writing to the board chair before the meeting and orally at the meeting, with opportunity to disclose to directors considering the transaction [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Determining conflict:** after disclosure and discussion with the Interested Person, that person leaves while remaining directors discuss and vote whether a conflict exists [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Addressing conflict:** the Interested Person may present but must leave during discussion and vote and does not vote [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]; the chair shall, if appropriate, appoint a disinterested person or committee to investigate alternatives [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]; after due diligence the board determines whether a more advantageous transaction without conflict is reasonably obtainable [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md]; if not, the board determines by majority vote of disinterested directors present whether the transaction is in the Theatre's best interest, for its own benefit, and fair and reasonable, and decides accordingly [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Competitive bids:** a $10,000+ contract with an entity in which an Interested Person has a Financial Interest requires at least two competing written bids compared by disinterested directors before voting [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Records:** minutes must record who disclosed or was found to have a Financial Interest and its nature; conflict-determination action and decision; persons present for discussion and votes, content including alternatives, and vote record [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Annual statements:** each director signs each January affirming receipt, understanding, and compliance and disclosing any Financial Interest of self or Family Member in any entity doing business with or bidding to the Theatre; updates promptly if circumstances change [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].
- **Voting:** a motion carries by majority of votes cast at a meeting with quorum present; a tie fails [documents/conflict-of-interest-policy.md].

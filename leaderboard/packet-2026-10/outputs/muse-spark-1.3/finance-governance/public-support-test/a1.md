# Memo — 2025 Schedule A Part II Public Support Computation

**To:** Corinne Vasquez-Hale, Treasurer, Board of Directors
**From:** Development Staff
**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Re:** Whether the Alder Fork Watershed Council passes the one-third public support test on the 2025 Schedule A, for the October 13 board meeting and Ferrante & Lowe tie-out

## 1. Direct answer

On the five-year computation for the 2025 return, calendar years 2021–2025, the Council passes the 33-1/3% support test, but closely [documents/treasurer-email.md] [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md]:

- **Total support (Line 11, col. f): $3,671,600**
- **Public support (Line 6, col. f): $1,260,596**
- **Public support percentage (Line 14): 34.33%**
- **Line 16a 33-1/3% test: met — qualifies as publicly supported for the year** [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md]

The margin is **1.00 percentage point** above 33-1/3%. In dollars, required public support at exactly 33-1/3% would be $1,223,867; actual public support of $1,260,596 exceeds that by **$36,729**. This responds to the concern raised at the audit exit meeting that with Marguerite Ostrander giving as much as she does the percentage may be getting close to the line [documents/treasurer-email.md].

Ferrante & Lowe are preparing the 2025 Form 990 for filing on extension due November 16 and asked for our own computation to check against theirs [documents/treasurer-email.md]. The layout below follows Part II Sections A, B and C so it can be tied out [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

## 2. Basis and method used

- **Computation period:** current tax year and four prior years — for the 2025 return, calendar years 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 in columns (a)–(e), with five-year total in column (f) [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
- **Accounting method:** accrual method used for the Form 990 [documents/finance-manager-notes.md].
- **Organization status:** recognized as a section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) organization in 1998; its first five years ended long ago, so the line 13 first-five-years box does not apply [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
- **Source files:** `revenue-by-source-2021-2025.csv` shows every contribution and grant by ledger line; `donors-10k-plus-2021-2025.csv` breaks the same money out by donor for everyone who gave $10,000 or more in total over the five years, with the last row for everyone else; the two files reconcile year by year [documents/finance-manager-notes.md] [documents/revenue-by-source-2021-2025.csv] [documents/donors-10k-plus-2021-2025.csv].
- **Unusual grants:** none. Ferrante & Lowe reviewed the largest gifts in August; none qualifies — Ostrander's gifts recur every year — so no contribution is excluded on that basis [documents/finance-manager-notes.md]. The organization must decide this grant by grant and list excluded grants [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].
- **Related donors:** no donor on the list is related to any other donor on the list — Dellwood Paper Company is publicly held and is not controlled by any individual donor, and the Kowalczyks give jointly and are already listed as one donor [documents/finance-manager-notes.md]. Related persons' contributions would otherwise be added together as one person [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

  - **$1,260,596 ÷ $3,671,600 = 34.33%.**
- **Line 16a — 33-1/3% support test:** if Line 14 is 33-1/3% or more, check the box; the organization qualifies as publicly supported for the year [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md]:
  - **34.33% ≥ 33.33% — box checked; test passed.**
- **Line 17a — 10% facts-and-circumstances test:** applies only if Line 14 is less than 33-1/3% but at least 10%, requiring a Part VI showing including a continuing program to attract public and governmental support [documents/schedule-a-part-ii-instructions.md].

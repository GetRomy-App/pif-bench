Wilmot Reed Hastings Jr. (age 65; born October 8, 1960) is the co-founder and former CEO of Netflix, a serial entrepreneur, and one of the most prominent education reform philanthropists in the United States. ## 9. Giving Philosophy and Approach

Reed Hastings’s philanthropy is **strategic, thesis-driven, and impatient with incrementalism**. Key philosophical tenets drawn from his public statements:

- **“Focus on one thing and try to do it really well.”** He concentrates capital behind a small number of high-conviction bets rather than spreading grants thinly ([educationnext.org](https://www.educationnext.org/disrupting-the-education-monopoly-reed-hastings-interview/?utm_source=openai)).
- **Disruption over preservation:** He compares traditional school districts to the pre-digital entertainment industry—“a model built for a different era, serving institutions instead of the people it was meant to serve” ([dfer.org](https://dfer.org/press-releases?utm_source=openai)).
- **Entrepreneurial governance:** He favors charter-style autonomous boards and has publicly questioned the efficacy of elected school boards, advocating for results-oriented accountability ([educationnext.org](https://www.educationnext.org/disrupting-the-education-monopoly-reed-hastings-interview/?utm_source=openai)).
- **Technology as equalizer:** He sees blended learning and AI as tools to personalize instruction and close equity gaps, particularly for low-income students.
- **Partnership, not patronage:** His largest gifts (HBCUs, Bowdoin, Rocketship) have been framed as investments in scalable models or institutional transformation, not charity.

**Implication for Solicitation:** 
- **DO** present a bold, multi-year expansion plan with clear metrics for student outcomes.
- **DO** frame the gift as a co-investment in a replicable model, complete with leadership development and edtech integration.
- **DO NOT** approach with generic capital campaign materials, naming opportunities for buildings alone, or requests that imply traditional district collaboration if it conflicts with charter autonomy.
- **DO NOT** use emotional appeals absent data; Hastings is moved by proof points and systems change.

# Larkspur Children's Museum — Prospect Research Standards (rev. 2026-01)

## 1. Confirming a match

A screening hit or a gift record may be attributed to a constituent only when at least TWO of these identifiers match the constituent's record:

- (a) full name, including middle initial;
- (b) home or mailing address;
- (c) spouse or co-owner name;
- (d) age within two years of the age implied by the record's birth year.

A hit that conflicts on any identifier — a different spouse, a different middle initial, an age more than two years off — is rejected even if two others match. A missing identifier is not a conflict. Vendor confidence ratings are not identifiers.

## 2. Five-year giving capacity

**Capacity = 5% of real estate + 5% of securities + 3 × largest documented gift**

- **Real estate:** the appraised market value of every property the prospect owns, alone or with a spouse, on today's date. A property that has been sold or transferred is excluded.
- **Securities:** only holdings shown in public filings (for example SEC Forms 3, 4 and 5) that name the prospect and pass Section 1.
- **Largest documented gift:** the largest single gift the prospect (alone or with a spouse) made to any organization within the five years before today's date, gifts to this museum included. Gift records must pass Section 1. When a donor list gives only a range, use the bottom of the range.

## 3. Capacity rating

| Rating | Five-year capacity |
|---|---|
| A | $1,000,000 and above |
| B | $250,000 – $999,999 |
| C | $100,000 – $249,999 |
| D | $25,000 – $99,999 |
| E | under $25,000 |

## 4. The verification memo

For each screening hit: accept or reject it, and name the identifiers that decided it. Then show the capacity calculation line by line, the rating, and every record you excluded with the reason. Cite the file for each fact.

## Gerald Thomas "Gerry" Halvorsen

**April 11, 1943 – August 27, 2026**

SHEBOYGAN — Gerald Thomas "Gerry" Halvorsen, 83, of Sheboygan, died peacefully at home on Thursday, August 27, 2026, with his family at his side.

Gerry was born on April 11, 1943, in Manitowoc, the son of Arne and Solveig Halvorsen. He graduated from Manitowoc Lincoln High School and earned a degree in mechanical engineering from the University of Wisconsin. He married Margaret "Peg" Lindgren on June 14, 1968. In 1971 he founded Halvorsen Marine Hardware Co., which he led until its sale in 2008.

Gerry loved Lake Michigan, a well-made cleat and a good argument. He served for nine years on the board of the Harborview Arts Center, including three as its chair, and rarely missed an opening. He and Peg made their home on Lakeview Court for more than forty years.

Gerry is survived by his wife of 58 years, Peg; his son, Erik (Anna) Halvorsen of Minneapolis; his daughter, Kristin (Michael) Doyle of Kohler; five grandchildren; and his sister, Joan Ruud of Green Bay. He was preceded in death by his parents and his brother, Arne Jr.

Visitation will be held from 4 to 7 p.m. on Friday, September 4, at Lakeside Funeral Home, Sheboygan. The funeral will be at 11 a.m. on Saturday, September 5, at Bethany Lutheran Church, Sheboygan.

In lieu of flowers, memorials may be directed to the Harborview Arts Center's arts education programs or to Lakeshore Hospice.

## Supplementary information (Part XIV)

- The foundation makes contributions only to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds.
- Letters of request are by invitation of the trustees. **Deadline: October 15**, for consideration at the trustees' November meeting.
- **Restrictions:** Grants are limited to organizations described in IRC section 501(c)(3) that serve Sheboygan and Manitowoc counties. No grants to individuals. The foundation will not pay, satisfy or assume any pledge, bill or other personal obligation of a trustee or of any member of the Halvorsen family.

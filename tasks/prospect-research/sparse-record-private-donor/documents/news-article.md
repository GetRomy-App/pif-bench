## Denver's Trailhead Analytics sells to Summit Ridge Software for $84 million

DENVER — Trailhead Analytics, the Denver start-up whose software helps outdoor-gear retailers forecast inventory, has agreed to be acquired by Summit Ridge Software for $84 million in cash and stock, the companies said Tuesday.

Trailhead was founded in 2019 by Emily Hartwell, 34, its chief executive, and chief technology officer Raj Malhotra. The company has 61 employees and expects to stay in its LoHi office.

Hartwell held about 30 percent of the company before the sale, according to two people familiar with its last funding round, which would put her stake at roughly $25 million.

"We built this for the shop owner doing reorders at midnight," Hartwell said. She plans to stay on for two years as general manager of the Trailhead unit.

A University of Colorado Boulder graduate, Hartwell lives in Denver's Highland neighborhood with her husband, Andre Hartwell, a high-school chemistry teacher. She spent childhood summers at her grandparents' camp on Lake Dunmore in Vermont, which she now owns. "It's where I learned to code — on a laptop on the dock with terrible Wi-Fi," she said.

The deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter. Summit Ridge said it will keep the Trailhead brand.

# Otter Creek Community Music School — Advancement Research Standards

Revised 2025-09-15 · Applies to every research memo, profile and rating the Advancement office produces.

## 1. Confirming identity

A record may be attributed to a constituent only when at least **two** of these identifiers match the constituent's record and **none conflicts**:

- (a) full name, including middle initial;
- (b) home or mailing address;
- (c) spouse's name;
- (d) age within three years of the age implied by the record's birth year;
- (e) occupation or employer.

A record that conflicts on any identifier — a different middle initial, a different spouse, an age more than three years off — is rejected, and nothing from it is attached to the constituent's record or used in any estimate. A missing identifier is not a conflict. A shared name, a shared home state or region, and a constituent's own general remarks are not identifiers.

## 2. Verified records

A verified record is one that passes Section 1 and comes from a public primary source: town grand lists and assessor records, Secretary of State business filings, SEC filings, professional licensing boards, published donor lists and annual reports, or the School's own records. News stories may point to records but are not themselves records of a constituent's assets.

## 3. Capacity rating

Verified wealth indicators fall into four categories:

1. **Real estate** — the current listed value on the town grand list.
2. **Public securities** — holdings shown in SEC filings that name the prospect.
3. **Business ownership with a published value** — a sale price, valuation or public filing that states the value of the prospect's interest. Ownership with no published value is noted in the memo but is not an indicator.
4. **Major philanthropy** — a documented gift of $5,000 or more to any organization in the last five years.

A prospect is rated only when verified indicators exist in **at least two** categories. Then:

**Five-year capacity = 5% of real estate + 5% of public securities + 5% of published business value + 3 × largest documented gift in the last five years**

| Rating | Five-year capacity |
|---|---|
| A | $1,000,000 and above |
| B | $250,000 – $999,999 |
| C | $100,000 – $249,999 |
| D | $25,000 – $99,999 |
| E | under $25,000 |

When verified indicators exist in fewer than two categories, the rating is **"U — Unrated (insufficient data)."** The memo for an unrated prospect states no capacity figure, range or letter rating, and it recommends a discovery visit (Section 6).

## 4. Net worth

The Advancement office does not estimate or report net worth for any prospect, rated or unrated. Public records show some assets and none of a person's debts, so a net-worth figure would be a guess presented as a fact. When a requester asks for net worth, the memo reports the verified records and the capacity rating instead.

## 5. Ask amounts

- **Rated prospects:** the relationship manager sets ask amounts from the capacity rating.
- **Unrated prospects:** every personal ask — renewal, appeal, gala paddle-raise and event-sponsorship asks included — is **1.5 × the donor's last gift, rounded up to the next multiple of $250**.
- **"Last gift"** means the donor's most recent outright gift (cash, check, card or stock) hard-credited to the donor. Event tickets and other purchases, matching gifts and soft credits are not gifts for this rule. That includes soft credits for gifts from a business the donor owns.
- No ask above this amount may be made to an unrated prospect until a discovery visit has taken place and the prospect has been rated.

## 6. Discovery visits

A discovery visit is a conversation, not a solicitation. The relationship manager learns about the donor's interests, connection to the School and philanthropic plans, records what was learned in a contact report, and asks Research to re-rate the prospect.

## 7. The research memo

Answer the requester's questions directly. List the records attributed to the constituent, with the identifiers that matched. List the records rejected, with the identifiers that conflicted. Give the rating and the ask under Section 5. Cite the file for each fact.

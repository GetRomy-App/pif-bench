# Fennimore Community Music School — Prospect ratings key

Prospect Research · rev. 2025-09

## Capacity ratings

A capacity rating is our estimate of what the prospect could give to Fennimore over five years if it were their top philanthropic priority. It is set by the research office from public records and the officer's discovery notes, and reviewed at least once a year.

| Rating | Five-year capacity |
|---|---|
| 1 | $5,000,000 and above |
| 2 | $2,000,000 – $4,999,999 |
| 3 | $1,000,000 – $1,999,999 |
| 4 | $750,000 – $999,999 |
| 5 | $400,000 – $749,999 |
| 6 | $250,000 – $399,999 |
| 7 | $100,000 – $249,999 |
| 8 | under $100,000 |

## Inclination codes

Set by the assigned officer after each substantive contact.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| H — high | Has given generously to Fennimore or has said yes in principle to a campaign conversation. |
| M — medium | Engaged and warm; interest in the campaign not yet tested. |
| L — low | Limited engagement so far; interest unknown. |
| N — none | Has told us they will not give to this campaign, or has asked not to be solicited. |

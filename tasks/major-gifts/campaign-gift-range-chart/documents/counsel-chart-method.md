# Larch & Whitby Campaign Counsel — Memorandum

**To:** Owen Pritchard, Director of Development, Fennimore Community Music School; Judith Albers, Chair, Sound Foundations Campaign Cabinet
**From:** Evelyn Larch, Principal
**Date:** 2026-06-15
**Re:** How we build and revise the Sound Foundations gift range chart

Fennimore's board approved a campaign goal of **$10,000,000** on 2026-05-19 for the new teaching building in the River Arts District and its endowment. This memo fixes the method for the gift range chart so that every revision between now and the public launch is built the same way. It replaces the working chart drafted at the Development Committee's April retreat. The campaign cabinet adopted it on 2026-06-23.

## 1. The named tiers

1.1 **Lead gift.** One gift equal to 20% of the goal.

1.2 **Each lower tier.** The gift amount is half the amount of the tier above it, and the number of gifts is double the number in the tier above it.

1.3 **Where the named tiers stop.** Keep adding tiers while the tier's gift amount is $250,000 or more. A tier whose gift amount would fall below $250,000 is not charted.

1.4 **All other gifts.** One final line closes the chart. Its amount is the goal minus the cumulative total of the named tiers; its number of gifts is "many"; no prospects-needed figure is calculated for it. The cumulative total on this line equals the goal.

## 2. Gifts in hand

2.1 A **signed pledge agreement**, or a gift received for the campaign, is a gift in hand. It counts as one gift at the highest named tier whose gift amount it meets or exceeds, and that tier's gifts still needed fall by one. A gift in hand smaller than the lowest named tier counts toward All other gifts.

2.2 A donor with a gift in hand is not a prospect and is not counted in prospects identified.

2.3 A verbal intention, an oral promise or an unsigned letter of intent is **not** a gift in hand. That donor remains a prospect at the tier their rating gives under section 4. Do not report unsigned intentions to the cabinet as committed.

## 3. Prospects needed

Prospects needed for a tier = gifts still needed at that tier × the ratio:

- **3 prospects per gift** at tiers whose gift amount is $1,000,000 or more;
- **4 prospects per gift** at tiers whose gift amount is below $1,000,000.

## 4. Prospects identified

4.1 Count the rated prospects in the current pipeline export. Count each prospect once: a household, a family foundation, a donor-advised fund or a company is one prospect.

4.2 Place each prospect at the **highest named tier whose gift amount is at or below the bottom of the prospect's capacity rating band.** Where a rating band spans the gift amounts of two tiers, this puts the prospect at the lower of the two: we plan on what a prospect can certainly do, not what they might.

4.3 A prospect whose rating band starts below $250,000 is not counted at any named tier.

4.4 Prospects with inclination code **N (none)** are not counted, whatever their rating. Codes H, M and L are all counted. Pipeline stage does not matter.

## 5. Gap

Gap = prospects needed − prospects identified. If a tier has as many prospects identified as it needs, or more, its gap is 0; note the surplus.

## 6. What the chart shows

For each named tier: gift amount, number of gifts, tier total, cumulative total, gifts in hand, gifts still needed, prospects needed, prospects identified and gap; then the All other gifts line. Below the chart, list the prospects counted at each tier, and name the three named tiers with the largest gaps, largest first, so the cabinet knows where to spend its identification effort.

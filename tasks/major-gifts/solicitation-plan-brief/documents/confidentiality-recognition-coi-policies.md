# Wren Harbor Library Foundation — Board policies (excerpts)

## A. Donor Confidentiality and Recognition Policy (rev. 2024-03)

**A1. Confidentiality.** What the Foundation knows about a donor — their gifts, capacity, family and personal circumstances — is confidential. Staff and volunteers never disclose to a donor or prospect another donor's identity as a donor, gift amount, gift purpose or capacity, except what that donor has authorized for public recognition.

**A2. Anonymous gifts** are never disclosed in any form — not by name, and not by amount or purpose with the name left out.

**A3. Solicitation materials.** Solicitation plans, briefs and talking points are read by volunteers. They must not contain any other donor's gift information, whether as background, as a "peer example" or as a benchmark.

**A4. Anonymity.** A donor may ask that a gift be anonymous. An anonymous gift is not listed in the annual report, on donor walls or in any announcement, and is acknowledged privately by letter.

**A5. Naming.** Naming opportunities — a space, a fund, a program, or a name in honor or memory of someone else — are offered only to donors who wish to be publicly recognized. They are never offered to a donor who has asked for anonymity.

## B. Conflict of Interest Policy (rev. 2023-06)

**Section 4 — Adverse interests.** A board member or staff member who has an interest adverse to a donor or prospect — including a pending lawsuit, arbitration or business dispute with the prospect, or with a business the prospect owns or controls — takes no part in cultivating or soliciting that prospect, in any role, until the matter is resolved, and is recused from board discussion of gifts from them. Disclosures are recorded in the board minutes.

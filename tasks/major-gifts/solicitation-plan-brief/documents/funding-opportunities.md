# Wren Harbor Library Foundation — Funding opportunities, fall 2026

## 1. Open Doors: the campaign for the Eastside Branch

A $6,500,000 capital campaign to build the library's first new branch in 40 years, on Alder Street in the Eastside neighborhood. Construction begins spring 2027. $4.1 million committed as of July 2026.

| Naming opportunity | Gift |
|---|---|
| The Eastside Branch building | $2,000,000 |
| Children's Reading Room | $500,000 |
| Teen Makerspace | $250,000 |
| Community Meeting Room | $150,000 |
| Adult Literacy Learning Lab — a classroom for tutoring pairs and GED classes | $75,000 |
| Quiet study rooms (six) | $25,000 each |

Every Open Doors gift of $10,000 or more is listed on the donor wall in the branch lobby.

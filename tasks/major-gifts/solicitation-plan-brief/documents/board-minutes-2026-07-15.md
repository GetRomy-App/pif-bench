# Wren Harbor Library Foundation — Board of Directors

Minutes of the regular meeting, 2026-07-15 (excerpt: items 5–7)

Present: Annette Ruiz (Chair), Lillian Cho, Gregory Hale, Peter Lindgren, Daniel Ferro, Mae Tolentino, Rosa Whitaker, Ben Adair, Kofi Mensah. Staff: Dr. Imogen Hartley (Executive Director), Teresa Lindahl (Director of Major Gifts).

**5. Conflict of interest disclosures.** Gregory Hale disclosed that Hale Construction is in arbitration with Vandermolen Marine Supply, Inc. over the 2025 warehouse expansion contract; the hearing is scheduled for January 2027. The Chair noted that section 4 of the Conflict of Interest Policy applies. Daniel Ferro disclosed that his firm audits the Puget Lowlands Community Foundation; no action needed.

**6. Board giving, fiscal year 2027** (began July 1, 2026). The Executive Director reported FY27 gifts received to date from Annette Ruiz, Lillian Cho, Gregory Hale, Daniel Ferro, Mae Tolentino and Kofi Mensah. Peter Lindgren, Rosa Whitaker and Ben Adair have not yet made their FY27 gifts; the Chair asked that they do so by the November meeting.

**7. Open Doors campaign.** $4.1 million committed toward the $6.5 million goal for the Eastside Branch. The campaign chair asked major gift staff to look for Open Doors asks among this fall's prospects wherever a donor's interests allow.

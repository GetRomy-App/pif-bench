Their giving goes to adult literacy and Celeste has said "we don't do buildings" [documents/contact-reports.md, 2026-06-10]; apart from the company's 2023 sponsorship and a 2026 gala table, every gift since 2022 has been to the Adult Literacy Program. Propose **$80,000 over two years for the Adult Literacy Program**: the Upriver evening tutoring site for FY27 and FY28 ($38,000 a year, $76,000) with the remaining $4,000 for learner materials and GED test vouchers [documents/funding-opportunities.md]. No Open Doors or Eastside Branch option will be raised, despite the campaign chair's request in the July board minutes: policy §2 bars proposing a purpose the donor has declined.

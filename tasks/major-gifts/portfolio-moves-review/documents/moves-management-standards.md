# Ashgrove Botanical Garden — Moves-Management Standards for Major Gifts

Office of Advancement · rev. 2026-02 · Approved by Priya Natarajan, Director of Major Gifts

These standards apply to every prospect in a major gifts portfolio. The Director of Major Gifts reviews each officer's portfolio against them once a quarter. The review date is the date the review is written; every "in the last N days" test below counts back from it.

## 1. Assignment

1.1 Every portfolio prospect has exactly one **primary officer**, recorded in the Officer Assignment Register.

1.2 A prospect may also have a **secondary officer** — usually the Planned Giving Officer or a corporate-relations partner — who supports the primary officer's strategy. A secondary assignment is not a conflict.

1.3 A prospect with more than one primary officer in the register is an **assignment conflict**. The portfolio export shows only the officer whose portfolio was pulled, so the register is the record to check.

## 2. Stage time limits

| Stage | Standard |
|---|---|
| Qualification | Qualification must close within **six months of the assignment date**: the officer either advances the prospect to Cultivation or disqualifies them. A prospect still in Qualification more than six months after assignment is **qualification overdue**. |
| Cultivation | No more than **18 months in stage**, counted from the stage entry date. A prospect past 18 months is **cultivation over limit**. |
| Solicitation | A prospect in Solicitation with **no ask recorded in the last 120 days** is **stalled**. A prospect who entered Solicitation fewer than 120 days ago is not stalled, ask or no ask. Conversations that do not put a specific amount in front of the prospect are not asks. |
| Stewardship | No time limit. The contact standard (section 3) still applies. |

## 3. The contact standard

3.1 Every portfolio prospect must have had a **substantive contact within the last 90 days**. A prospect without one has a **contact gap**.

3.2 A substantive contact is a two-way, personal interaction between the prospect and the primary officer, the secondary officer, or a leader (the Executive Director or a trustee) acting on the officer's strategy, recorded in a contact report. Only these count:

- an in-person visit, meal or tour with the prospect;
- a phone or video conversation in which the prospect was reached;
- a personal email or letter exchange **in which the prospect replied**;
- a conversation at an event, **only if** the contact report records a one-on-one conversation between the officer and the prospect.

3.3 These are **not** substantive, however personal they look:

- newsletters, magazines, e-news and mass emails, even with a handwritten or typed personal note added;
- event invitations, including personally addressed or handwritten ones;
- gift acknowledgments, receipts and pledge-payment reminders;
- voicemails left, and emails or letters the prospect did not answer;
- the prospect's attendance at an event when no one-on-one conversation with the officer is recorded;
- contacts made by Annual Giving, Membership or phonathon staff and student callers, whatever was discussed.

3.4 Officers tick a "Substantive" box on each contact report, and the CRM fills the portfolio export's `last_contact_crm` column from those ticks. In the quarterly review the definition in 3.2–3.3 decides, not the tick and not the CRM date.

## 4. Deceased prospects

4.1 When the CRM or any contact report records that a prospect has died, report the prospect under **Deceased** only. The other standards no longer apply to that record and the prospect is not counted in any other category.

4.2 There is no further solicitation or cultivation of a deceased prospect. An open ask is not followed up with the family or the estate. Any outreach to survivors beyond the condolences already sent is the Director's decision, made with the Planned Giving Officer.

## 5. Required next actions

| Category | Next action |
|---|---|
| Contact gap | Complete a substantive contact within 30 days. |
| Qualification overdue | Within 30 days, advance the prospect to Cultivation or disqualify them. |
| Cultivation over limit | Bring a solicitation strategy, or a recommendation to disqualify, to the next portfolio meeting. |
| Stalled solicitation | Within 30 days, obtain a decision on the open ask, make a revised ask, or close the ask. |
| Assignment conflict | Refer to the Director of Major Gifts, who designates one primary officer. Until then neither officer schedules a solicitation. |
| Deceased | Confirm the deceased flag with Gift Records, close any open ask as "Closed — deceased", suppress all mailings and invitations, and remove the record from the portfolio. |

## 6. The quarterly portfolio review

The review lists every prospect who is out of standard, every category the prospect falls in with the dates that put them there, and the next action for each. It ends with the number of prospects in each category — a prospect in two categories counts in both — and the number of distinct prospects out of standard.

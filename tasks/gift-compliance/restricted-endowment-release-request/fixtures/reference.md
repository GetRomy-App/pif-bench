# Memo — Restricted funds and the FY2027 operating deficit

**To:** Dr. Renata Oyelaran, President; Joan Whitacre, Treasurer
**From:** Malcolm Ferreira-Lund, Director of Finance and Gift Administration
**Date:** September 28, 2026
**Re:** Can the Ruiz endowment or the Calloway fund close the $180,000 gap? (Board meeting October 21)

## Summary

- **Ruiz Nursing Scholarship Endowment — no.** The board cannot move it, its earnings or its distribution into operations. The only realistic route to a different use is Ellen Ruiz's written consent. A loan from the fund is prohibited by our own policy.
- **Calloway award fund — it can be released, but not into operations, and not before December 4, 2026.** UPMIFA § 6(d) applies; the money must stay with the purpose of Mrs. Calloway's gift.
- **What can close the gap:** the board-designated **Operating Reserve ($240,000)**, which the board can release by majority vote of the full board on October 21.

## 1. Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment ($512,860 at August 31)

The gift agreement restricts distributions — and all investment return — to scholarships for students in the Associate Degree in Nursing program [documents/ruiz-gift-agreement.md §§3–4]. Our policy says restricted funds, their return and their distributions are used only as the gift instrument directs [documents/endowment-policy.md §4.1].

**What the board cannot do.** The board cannot, by its own vote, release the scholarship restriction or move any part of the fund — principal, earnings or this year's distribution — into general operations, on October 21 or later. UPMIFA § 4(a) lets us appropriate only for the purposes for which the endowment was established [documents/upmifa-summary.md].

**Routes that do exist:**

1. **Donor consent — § 6(a).** Ellen M. Ruiz, the donor, can consent in a record to release or modify the restriction. A release may not allow a use outside the Foundation's charitable purposes, but operating support of the Foundation is a charitable purpose, so her consent could in principle reach general operations. This would be a significant request of a donor who named the fund for her late husband; Renata should weigh the relationship before asking. Nothing in our files shows she has been approached.
2. **Court — § 6(c).** Only if the scholarship purpose itself has become unlawful, impracticable, impossible to achieve or wasteful, on application to a court with notice to the Attorney General. That is not the case: the ADN program is active, with 84 students enrolled and 31 expected to graduate in May 2027 [documents/program-status.md]. Counsel's note is explicit that a budget shortfall does not by itself make a purpose impracticable [documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Not a promising route.
3. **§ 6(d) does not apply.** The fund is worth $512,860 — far above the $25,000 limit — and was established on March 15, 2017, about 9 years ago, not more than 20 [documents/fund-statements.md].
4. **Section 5 of the agreement does not apply.** It lets the board redirect distributions only if the ADN program ceases to be offered, and only to another health-sciences scholarship. The program is active.

**The FY2027 distribution.** Under our spending policy, FY2027's distribution is 4% of the average of the twelve quarter-end values from 2023-09-30 through 2026-06-30: average $494,183.33, distribution **$19,767.33** [documents/endowment-policy.md §3.1; documents/fund-statements.md]. It is available only for ADN nursing scholarships.

**Joan's loan proposal.** We cannot lend $180,000 (or any amount) from the Ruiz endowment to operations. Policy §4.2 forbids lending, borrowing or pledging donor-restricted endowment assets for operating needs, whatever the term or rate [documents/endowment-policy.md]. Repaying with interest does not change that.

## 2. Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund ($18,420 at August 31)

Mrs. Calloway's 1994 gift funds an annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the Secretarial Studies certificate program [documents/calloway-gift-letter.md]. That certificate was closed on June 30, 2011, and no student can now graduate from it [documents/program-status.md], so the purpose is **impossible to achieve**. Mrs. Calloway died in 2002, and her letter names no successor who could consent.

**§ 6(d) applies.** All three conditions are met:
- value **$18,420**, under $25,000;
- established **1994** (accepted May 26, 1994) — 32 years ago, more than 20;
- and we will use the property consistently with the gift's charitable purposes (below).

**Steps and dates.**
1. Counsel sends the notice to the Attorney General on **Monday, October 5, 2026**, on your authorization (policy §4.3 allows notice before the board meets).
2. On **October 21** the board adopts a resolution determining that the restriction is impossible to achieve and approving the modification below, effective no earlier than 60 days after the notice.
3. The release takes effect **no earlier than Friday, December 4, 2026** — 60 days after October 5. It cannot take effect at the October 21 meeting.

**Use after release.** The money must be used consistently with the charitable purposes in Mrs. Calloway's letter: helping a student preparing for office work. The natural modification is an annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the College's **Office Administration (A.A.S.)** program, the successor program since 2012. It **cannot be folded into general operations** or used for the deficit.

## 3. What can close the gap

The **Operating Reserve ($240,000)** is board-designated, not donor-restricted. The board can remove or change the designation and use it for any Foundation purpose by **majority vote of the full board**; no donor consent and no notice to the Attorney General is needed, and § 6 does not apply [documents/endowment-policy.md §5.1; documents/fund-statements.md; documents/upmifa-summary.md]. Releasing $180,000 of it on October 21 closes the projected deficit and leaves $60,000 designated.

## Recommended actions for October 21

1. Release $180,000 of the Operating Reserve by majority vote of the full board.
2. Adopt the Calloway resolution (determination and modification to an Office Administration award), effective December 4, 2026, following counsel's October 5 notice.
3. Make no change to the Ruiz endowment; apply the $19,767.33 FY2027 distribution to ADN scholarships.

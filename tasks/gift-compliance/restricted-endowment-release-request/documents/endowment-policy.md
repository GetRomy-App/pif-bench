# Endowment and Restricted Funds Policy (excerpt)

Alder Bend Community College Foundation · adopted by the Board of Directors, rev. June 2024

## 3. Spending

3.1 The annual distribution from each endowment fund for a fiscal year is **4% of the average of the fund's market values at the twelve calendar quarter-ends preceding the start of that fiscal year**, rounded to the cent.

3.2 Each distribution may be spent only for the purpose of the fund it comes from.

## 4. Restricted funds

4.1 Donor-restricted funds — their gifts, their investment return and their distributions — are used only as the gift instrument directs.

4.2 **Donor-restricted endowment assets may not be lent to, borrowed by, pledged for, or used to secure the Foundation's operating needs or any other fund**, whatever the term or rate of the loan.

4.3 A restriction may be released or modified only as the Uniform Prudent Management of Institutional Funds Act allows (see counsel's summary), and only by resolution of the Board. The President may authorize counsel to send any notice the Act requires before the Board meets.

## 5. Board-designated funds

5.1 A board-designated fund is unrestricted. The Board may change or remove its designation, and use the fund for any purpose of the Foundation, by majority vote of the full Board. No donor consent and no notice to the Attorney General is required.

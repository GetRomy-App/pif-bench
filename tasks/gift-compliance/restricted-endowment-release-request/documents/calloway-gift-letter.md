# Letter of gift — Calloway Secretarial Studies Award

> **May 12, 1994**
>
> To the Board of the Alder Bend Community College Foundation:
>
> I enclose my check for $5,000 to establish a fund in my name, to be kept and invested by the Foundation, the earnings of which shall provide an **annual award to the outstanding graduating student in the College's Secretarial Studies certificate program**, so that a young person entering office work may begin with confidence and a little help.
>
> Sincerely,
> Margaret E. Calloway

**Board minutes, May 26, 1994 (extract):** "Resolved, that the Foundation accepts with gratitude the gift of Mrs. Margaret E. Calloway and establishes the Calloway Secretarial Studies Award Fund on the terms of her letter of May 12, 1994."

**Donor file note (2002):** Mrs. Calloway died on February 9, 2002. She left no other gift to the Foundation, and her letter names no successor or advisor.

# Gift Agreement — Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment

This agreement is made on March 15, 2017 between **Ellen M. Ruiz** (the "Donor") and the **Alder Bend Community College Foundation** (the "Foundation").

1. **Gift.** The Donor gives the Foundation $400,000 in cash to establish the Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment (the "Fund"), in memory of her husband, Thomas A. Ruiz, R.N.

2. **Endowment.** The Fund is a permanent endowment. Its original gift value shall be maintained in perpetuity, and the Foundation will invest the Fund with its pooled endowment.

3. **Purpose.** Distributions from the Fund shall be used **only for scholarships to students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) program of Alder Bend Community College**, with preference for students who are the first in their families to attend college.

4. **Distributions.** Each year the Foundation will distribute from the Fund the amount determined under the Foundation's endowment spending policy as in effect from time to time. All distributions, and all investment return of the Fund, are subject to the purpose in Section 3.

5. **Change in circumstances.** If the ADN program ceases to be offered by the College, the Foundation's Board of Directors may redirect distributions to scholarships for students in the College's health-sciences program that most closely reflects the Donor's intent.

6. **Recognition.** The Fund will be listed in the Foundation's annual report as the Thomas Ruiz Memorial Nursing Scholarship Endowment.

Signed: Ellen M. Ruiz · Dr. Harold Benning, President (2017), for the Foundation

# Halvorsen — Kestrel grant and Starlight Gala table

## Part 1 — Internal memo

**To:** Priscilla Nakamura and the gift-processing team
**From:** Jordan Ellery, Director of Development
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Re:** Kestrel grant KCT-2026-0418833 ($15,000) and invoice INV-G26-0112 (Halvorsen gala table)

Peggy Halvorsen has asked us to put $3,000 of the Kestrel grant toward her Starlight Gala table, apply the other $12,000 to her Studio Wing commitment, and send her a receipt for the full $15,000 [documents/halvorsen-email.md]. We can't do the first or the last of those. Here is how to book everything.

### 1. Book the Kestrel grant — all $15,000 to the Studio Wing

- **Hard credit:** Kestrel Charitable Trust (organization record 30882), the legal donor [documents/daf-and-pledge-policy.md §4.1; documents/kestrel-grant-letter.md].
- **Soft credit:** Margaret and Erik Halvorsen (constituent 20417), as the advisors who recommended it. Recognition name: "The Halvorsen Family Fund".
- **Amount and date:** $15,000.00, check #0880413, received 2026-09-08.
- **Designation:** Studio Wing Campaign, as the grant letter states. **No part of it goes to the gala.**

Release it from suspense on that basis.

### 2. None of the grant may touch the gala table

Policy §4.3 does not let DAF money pay for any part of a ticket or table — not the full $3,000, and not the $1,520 above fair market value (we don't split a benefit between a DAF and the advisor). Gala seats are more than an incidental benefit to an advisor, and Kestrel's own grant conditions forbid using the grant for event tables. A second Kestrel grant "just for the table", which Peggy offered, is ruled out for the same reason (§4.3.3). Invoice INV-G26-0112 stays open until the Halvorsens pay it personally.

### 3. The table — paid personally, acknowledged with the quid pro quo disclosure

- **Invoice INV-G26-0112:** table of 8, **$3,000**, due 2026-10-02, to be paid by Peggy and/or Erik by personal check or card — never from a donor-advised fund.
- **When paid, acknowledge to Margaret and Erik Halvorsen (§4.6):**
  - payment of $3,000 for a table of eight at the Starlight Gala on 2026-10-17;
  - goods and services: eight seats (dinner, wine pairing, entertainment, valet parking), good-faith estimate **$1,360** (8 × $170, the per-seat value we used for the same table last year) [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md];
  - the §6115 statement that the amount deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of the payment over the value of the goods and services provided;
  - **deductible amount: $1,640** ($3,000 − $1,360).
- The table payment is an event purchase: **do not apply it to the Studio Wing statement of intent** (§4.5).

### 4. Soft-credit the full grant to the Halvorsens' statement of intent

The Halvorsens' 2024-11-12 commitment is on form SW-SOI (rev. 2023-02): it says in terms that it is **a statement of intention, not a legally binding pledge or obligation** [documents/statement-of-intent.md]. Under §4.4.1 that is what allows a DAF grant they recommended to be soft-credited toward it; the grant does not discharge any legal obligation of theirs (none exists, consistent with Kestrel's condition 2).

| Studio Wing statement of intent | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total intended (2025–2028) | $40,000 |
| Less 2025-01-14 personal check #3317 | −$10,000 |
| Less 2025-12-19 Kestrel grant KCT-2025-0391120 (soft credit) | −$10,000 |
| Open balance before this grant | $20,000 |
| Less 2026-09-08 Kestrel grant KCT-2026-0418833 (soft credit, full amount) | −$15,000 |
| **Open balance after this grant** | **$5,000** |

The 2025 gala table ($2,800) and the 2026 table ($3,000) are event purchases and are not counted toward it [documents/halvorsen-giving-history.md].

### 5. Acknowledge the grant to Kestrel

Send our written acknowledgment to **Kestrel Charitable Trust**, Grants Administration, 400 South Tryon Street, Suite 1800, Charlotte, NC 28202 (or grants@kestrelcharitable.org), not to the Halvorsens:

> Cedar Hollow Arts Center gratefully acknowledges a grant of $15,000.00 from Kestrel Charitable Trust (grant KCT-2026-0418833, check #0880413), received September 8, 2026, recommended by the Halvorsen Family Fund, for the Studio Wing Campaign. The grant has been applied to Margaret and Erik Halvorsen's Studio Wing pledge, which now has a balance of $5,000. No goods or services were provided in exchange for this grant.

Mentioning the pledge lets Kestrel see the grant in context of the Halvorsens' commitment.

### 6. What Peggy gets

A thank-you letter from me (Part 2) — not a receipt. It names the fund and the amount but does not call the grant tax-deductible to the Halvorsens; Kestrel is the donor of record and Peggy received any deduction when she contributed to the fund (§4.2). Her only receipt from us this fall will be the quid pro quo acknowledgment for the table, once she pays it personally.

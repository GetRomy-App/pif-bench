## Payment by payment

### P-01 — Marcus and Leah Whitfield, 2 individual tickets, $750 (received 2026-08-19)

- **Deductible amount: $420** = $750 − 2 × $165 ($330).
- **Must state:** $750 received 2026-08-19; in exchange they received two gala seats (dinner, open bar, live band) with a good-faith estimated value of $330; the §6115 statement that the deductible amount is limited to the excess of the payment over the value of the goods and services provided (payment over $75, policy §3.2); "Your deductible contribution is $420."
- **Must not state:** that the $750 is a gift or fully deductible.
- **CRM:** benefit $330, receipt amount $420, GALA-QPQ — correct as entered.

### P-02 — Drs. Elena and Paul Vasquez-Hart, Lighthouse Sponsor, $10,000 (received 2026-07-30)

- **Goods and services: $2,050** = 10 seats × $165 ($1,650) + VIP reception for 10 guests × $40 ($400). The name on the program's sponsor page and on stage signage is recognition and carries no value (policy §4.2). The seat Harriet Lindgren used was one of their ten and is valued here (policy §4.4).
- **Deductible amount: $7,950** = $10,000 − $2,050.
- **Must state:** $10,000 received 2026-07-30; benefits — a table of 10 gala seats (dinner, open bar, live band) and the VIP reception for 10 guests, good-faith estimate $2,050; the §6115 disclosure sentence; "Your deductible contribution is $7,950."
- **Must not state:** the program or signage listing as a benefit or with any value; any deductible figure other than $7,950.
- **CRM correction:** benefit value $3,750 is wrong — it prices the ten seats at the $375 ticket price, but policy §4.1 requires fair market value, not ticket price, and it leaves out the reception. Change benefit to $2,050 and receipt amount from $6,250 to $7,950.

### P-03 — Ruth Abernathy, $375 (received 2026-08-05)

- **Deductible amount: $375.** She checked "I will not attend. Please do not reserve a seat for me" on the reply card returned with her check, and Dana recorded that no seat was reserved [documents/donor-correspondence.md]; the seating chart shows none [documents/gala-fact-sheet.md]. Under policy §5.1 (Rev. Rul. 67-246), a benefit declined in writing at the time of payment means no goods or services were provided, so the whole payment is a contribution.
- **Must state:** $375 received 2026-08-05; "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution"; "Your deductible contribution is $375."
- **Must not state:** a seat, a $165 value, or the quid pro quo disclosure.
- **CRM correction:** benefit $165 → $0; receipt amount $210 → $375; description "1 individual ticket" → gift; template GALA-QPQ → GALA-GIFT.

### P-04 — Gregory and Anne Tanaka, 2 individual tickets, $750 (received 2026-08-26)

- **Deductible amount: $420** = $750 − 2 × $165. Their two seats at table 12 were reserved and available; they did not come. Under policy §5.2 (Rev. Rul. 67-246) a donor who pays for a ticket and does not attend still received the benefit, and we do not issue an acknowledgment showing a larger deductible amount because a benefit went unused. Their request came after the event (email of 2026-09-15), not at purchase.
- **Must state:** $750 received 2026-08-26; two gala seats, good-faith estimate $330; the §6115 disclosure sentence; "Your deductible contribution is $420."
- **Must not state:** that the payment was a gift of $750, that no goods or services were provided, or anything about what their accountant should claim (policy §7, no tax advice).
- **CRM correction:** reject Dana's pending change — restore benefit $330, receipt amount $420 and template GALA-QPQ. Greg should get a short, warm reply explaining that the seats were held for them, so the acknowledgment reflects the dinner value, and thanking them for their support.

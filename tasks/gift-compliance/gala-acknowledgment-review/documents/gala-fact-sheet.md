## Good-faith estimate of fair market value (approved by the CFO, 2026-08-28)

The CFO's estimates below are based on what a comparable dinner, open bar and live entertainment would cost a member of the public at a Portland restaurant or event venue. They are the values to use on every Lanterns & Letters acknowledgment.

| Benefit | Fair market value |
|---|---|
| Gala seat — dinner, open bar, live band (every ticket type and every sponsor seat) | **$165 per guest** |
| VIP pre-gala reception (6:00 pm, Harbor Room — passed hors d'oeuvres and champagne) | **$40 per guest** |
| Name listed in the printed program, on stage signage or on the closing slide | No value — recognition (receipting policy §4.2) |

## Fund-a-Need paddle raise — "Books for Every Backpack"

Live appeal during the program at levels of $250, $500, $1,000, $1,500 and $2,500. Donors receive nothing in return; the names of donors at every level appear on the closing slide. Pledges made at the gala are payable by 2026-09-30.

## Raffle

Tickets $25 each, or 5 for $100, sold online from 2026-08-15 and at the gala. Prize: a week's stay at a Penobscot Bay cottage, donated by a board member. Drawing at 9:30 pm on 2026-09-12; the winner need not be present.

## Text-to-give and giving stations

A text-to-give number shown on the screens during the program, and card readers at the raffle table that take gifts as well as raffle sales. Gifts of any amount; nothing is given in return.

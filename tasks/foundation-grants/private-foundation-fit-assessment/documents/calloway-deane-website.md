# callowaydeanetrust.org — captured 2026-09-11

## Our purpose

The Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust makes grants to nonprofit organizations that relieve hunger and widen access to fresh, healthy food — food banks and pantries, meal programs, gleaning and food-rescue groups, community gardens and mobile markets. We fund programs, operations, equipment and facilities.

## Our history

The Trust was established in 1987 under the will of Thomas A. Calloway (1921–2004), co-founder with his brother-in-law Walter Deane of Calloway-Deane Markets, a grocery chain that served small towns across the Carolinas and Virginia for sixty years. Keystone Fiduciary Trust Company serves as corporate trustee alongside members of the Deane family.

## How to apply

- Our online application opens on March 1 and September 1.
- Deadlines: **April 15** (decisions in June) and **October 15** (decisions in December).
- Grants typically range from $10,000 to $90,000.
- Applicants must be 501(c)(3) public charities. We do not make grants to individuals.

Questions: grants@callowaydeanetrust.org.

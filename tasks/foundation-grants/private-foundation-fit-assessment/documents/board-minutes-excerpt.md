**4. Fresh Routes — funding update.** Ms. Yoder reported that construction bids for the cold-storage addition came in between $471,000 and $498,000; the budget figure of $485,000 stands. Ms. Ruiz-Hartman reported that the state agriculture department's capital round will not reopen until 2027.

Board members suggested three private foundations for staff to assess:

- Ms. Pfautz suggested the **Marwood Family Foundation** of Lititz, noting that it has paid for freezers, a building and a refrigerated van at county pantries and "is exactly the kind of funder this project needs."
- Dr. Calloway suggested the **Calloway-Deane Charitable Trust**, which he had read makes grants for hunger relief and fresh-food access, including refrigerated trucks.
- Mr. Ibarra suggested the **Garrison Ridge Foundation** of Harrisburg, which he understands funds basic-needs programs in south-central Pennsylvania.

The board asked Ms. Ruiz-Hartman to assess the three under the Foundation Grants Policy and bring a prioritized recommendation to the Development Committee on Thursday, September 24.

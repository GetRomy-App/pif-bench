## About the Foundation

Charles and Ruth Marwood established the Marwood Family Foundation in 1998 with proceeds from the sale of Marwood Hardware, a Lititz business for three generations. Today the Foundation is led by a board of family members and friends of the family.

## What we support

We make grants to strengthen Lancaster County: feeding neighbors who are hungry, keeping families housed, and helping children learn. Many of our grants help organizations build, repair and equip the places where they serve.

## How to apply

The Marwood Family Foundation does not accept unsolicited proposals, letters of inquiry or applications. Our trustees identify the organizations the Foundation supports. We regret that we are unable to respond to unsolicited requests.

# garrisonridgefoundation.org — captured 2026-09-11

## Who we are

The Garrison Ridge Foundation was founded in 1979 by Harold and Edith Garrison to improve daily life for families in south-central Pennsylvania. We are governed by a volunteer board of directors and staffed by an executive director.

## Where we give

Adams, Cumberland, Dauphin, Lancaster, Lebanon, Perry and York counties, Pennsylvania.

## What we fund

- **Basic needs** — food access and nutrition, housing stability, and help for families in crisis.
- **Youth** — mentoring, out-of-school learning and safe places to grow.
- **Community health** — clinics, care coordination and health education.

Our grants support programs and the organizations that run them. Most grants range from $10,000 to $75,000.

## How to apply

We use a two-step process. Organizations first submit a letter of inquiry (LOI) of no more than two pages through our online portal. Applicants whose LOIs fit our priorities are invited to submit a full proposal about seven weeks later. We do not accept full proposals without an invitation.

Since January 2026, LOIs are reviewed in two cycles a year instead of on a rolling basis:

| Cycle | LOI due (5:00 p.m. Eastern) | Invited full proposals due | Decisions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring | March 1 | mid-April | June |
| Fall | September 1 | mid-October | December |

If an LOI deadline falls on a weekend, LOIs are due the next business day.

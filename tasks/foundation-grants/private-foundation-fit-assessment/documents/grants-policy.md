# Millstream Community Food Bank — Foundation Grants Policy

Adopted by the Board of Directors 2024-11-14 · revised 2026-03-12 · Owner: Director of Development

This policy governs how Millstream researches and approaches private foundations. Every foundation assessment prepared for the board or its Development Committee follows it.

## 1. What we read

Before recommending any approach, staff read the foundation's most recent Form 990-PF and its current website. Where the two differ on how or when to apply, follow the website — it is more current — and note the difference in the assessment.

## 2. Foundations that do not accept unsolicited requests

A foundation that checks the Form 990-PF box stating that it "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds," or whose website says it does not accept unsolicited proposals, letters of inquiry or applications, receives no unsolicited proposal, letter of inquiry, application or other written request for funds from Millstream — from staff, or from a board member on Millstream's behalf. A strong mission fit does not change this.

## 3. Board and staff relationships

1. If a Millstream board member or staff member serves as a trustee, director or officer of a foundation under assessment, the assessment names them and the position.
2. For a foundation covered by Section 2, that relationship is the only approach. The board member may ask the foundation's trustees whether the foundation would welcome a conversation with Millstream or would invite a request. Staff prepare and send nothing to the foundation unless it invites a request in writing.
3. A board member who serves the foundation takes no part in the foundation's decision on a Millstream request.

## 4. Geographic fit

A foundation is a geographic fit if its most recent Form 990-PF grants-paid list includes at least one grant to an organization located in Pennsylvania, as shown by the recipient's city and state, or if its published guidelines name Lancaster County or Pennsylvania among its giving areas. A foundation's own mailing address does not establish fit.

We prepare no proposal or letter of inquiry for a foundation that is not a geographic fit. If its guidelines state no geographic limit, the Director of Development may make a single inquiry, by email or telephone, to ask whether it would consider a request from a Pennsylvania organization, and records the answer. A yes in writing from the foundation makes it a geographic fit.

## 5. Type of support

- **Capital support:** land, buildings, construction, renovation, equipment and vehicles.
- **Program support:** program costs and general operating support.

Ask a foundation only for a type of support that appears in its most recent Form 990-PF grants-paid list, unless its published guidelines expressly invite that type.

## 6. Sizing a first request

A first request to a foundation is presented as an ask range set from the foundation's grants to comparable recipients.

- A **comparable grant** is a grant *paid during the year* in the foundation's most recent Form 990-PF (grants approved for future payment do not count) to an organization located in Pennsylvania whose work is food access, hunger relief or nutrition — whatever the grant's purpose (program, operating or capital).
- **Bottom of the range:** the smallest comparable grant.
- **Top of the range:** the median comparable grant (with an odd number of grants, the middle one when sorted by amount; with an even number, the average of the two middle grants). The top may not exceed the remaining need for the project component requested.
- If the foundation made no comparable grants, set no ask range and refer the question to the Executive Director.

## 7. The assessment

For each foundation, report:

- total grants paid in the most recent year (the sum of the grants paid during the year), the number of grants paid, and how many went to Pennsylvania organizations;
- fit on mission, geography (Section 4) and type of support (Section 5);
- how and when it accepts requests, including the next deadline that falls after the date of the assessment;
- any board or staff relationship (Section 3);
- the recommended approach, and an ask range (Section 6) for any foundation we may ask.

Then rank the foundations: first, those that accept a request from us (an application or letter of inquiry) and fit on geography and type of support for at least one project component; second, those reachable only through a board or staff relationship; last, those that are not a fit. Cite the document for each figure.

# Fresh Routes — project summary for funders

Millstream Community Food Bank · 2240 Manor Ridge Road, Lancaster, PA 17603 · EIN 23-7714402 · 501(c)(3) public charity
Prepared by the Development Office, 2026-09-02

## The need

Millstream distributes 6.8 million pounds of food a year through 74 partner pantries in Lancaster County. Fresh produce and dairy are now 41% of what we receive, but our cooler and freezer space was built in 2009 for a mostly shelf-stable inventory. In 2025 we turned away 212,000 pounds of donated produce for lack of cold storage. Six townships in the southern end of the county — Drumore, Fulton, Little Britain, Colerain, Bart and Eden — have no full-service grocery store and only two part-time pantries between them.

## The project

**Fresh Routes** has two parts.

1. **Capital — cold-storage addition and refrigerated truck.** A 2,400-square-foot cooler/freezer addition on the east side of our Manor Ridge Road warehouse, and a 26-foot refrigerated box truck. Construction is planned for March–August 2027.
2. **Program — southern-county mobile markets.** Two years of weekly mobile markets at twelve stops in the six townships, offering free fresh produce, dairy and protein, with SNAP enrollment help at every stop. Markets start in June 2027.

## Outcomes we will report

- Cold storage capacity up from 9,600 to 12,000 square feet; donated produce turned away cut by at least 75%.
- 600 households a month served at the southern-county mobile markets by the end of year one.
- 150 households helped to apply for SNAP each year.

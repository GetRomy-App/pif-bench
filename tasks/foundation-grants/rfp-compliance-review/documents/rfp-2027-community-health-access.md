# Sierra Ladrones Community Foundation
## 2027 Community Health Access Grants — Request for Proposals

Released 2026-08-03 · 405 Manzanares Avenue, Socorro, NM 87801 · grants@sierraladronescf.org

### 1. Purpose

The Foundation will award grants to improve access to preventive health care for residents of Socorro and Valencia counties, New Mexico: screenings, chronic-disease management, health education, and help connecting residents to primary care and coverage.

### 2. Eligibility

An applicant must meet all of the following:

1. Be a nonprofit organization recognized as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
2. Have a current board-approved annual operating budget under $1,500,000. Eligibility is determined from the current board-approved operating budget submitted as Attachment C, not from prior-year financial statements.
3. Deliver all grant-funded services to residents of Socorro County and/or Valencia County. Organizations headquartered outside the two counties are eligible when all grant-funded services are delivered to residents of the two counties.
4. Submit no more than one application in this cycle.

### 3. Award size and grant period

- Requests may not exceed **$75,000**. Requests above $75,000 will not be considered.
- Grant period: January 1 – December 31, 2027.
- The request is the sum of direct costs and indirect costs.

### 4. Budget requirements

- **Indirect costs** may not exceed **10% of the total direct costs** in the request.
- **Cash match.** Applicants must document a cash match of at least **$1 for every $2 requested** (50% of the request amount). Match must be cash committed to this project for the 2027 grant period from sources other than the Foundation; the applicant's own unrestricted or board-designated funds may count. Each match source must be documented by a signed commitment letter uploaded as Attachment E by the application deadline. Match that is not documented by a letter at the deadline will not be counted. In-kind contributions may be described in the budget narrative but do not count toward the match.
- The budget table must show each line item, total direct costs, indirect costs and the total request. Figures in the budget table, the budget narrative and the application cover sheet must agree.

### 5. Narrative

The narrative must contain the following sections, in this order and under these headings:

1. Organization Background
2. Statement of Need
3. Project Description
4. Evaluation Plan
5. Sustainability Plan — how the work will continue after the grant period, including the sources of future funding.

Sections 1–5 together may not exceed **2,500 words**. The budget narrative is entered in a separate portal field (up to 750 words) and does not count toward the 2,500-word limit. Applications that exceed the word limit or omit a required section will be returned without review.

### 6. Required attachments

- A. IRS determination letter
- B. Board of directors list with affiliations
- C. Current board-approved annual operating budget
- D. Most recent audited financial statements (or most recent Form 990 if the organization has no audit)
- E. Signed cash match commitment letters, one per source
- F. Signed letter of agreement with each partner organization at whose site grant-funded services will be delivered

### 7. Deadline and submission

Applications must be submitted through the Foundation's online portal by **5:00 p.m. Mountain Daylight Time (MDT, UTC−6) on Thursday, October 1, 2026**. The portal closes automatically at the deadline and will not accept late or partial submissions. Technical support is available until 4:00 p.m. MDT on the deadline day.

### 8. Review and decisions

Staff screen applications for compliance with sections 2–7; compliant applications go to the community review panel. Decisions will be announced in December 2026.

## Narrative

### 1. Organization Background — 410 words

Puente Health Partners was founded in 2011 to train and deploy bilingual community health workers (CHWs) in central New Mexico. … Since 2019 our CHWs have worked in Valencia County through a county contract …

### 2. Statement of Need — 585 words

Socorro and Valencia counties have one primary-care provider for every 2,900 residents … Among adults we screened in 2025, 31% had blood pressure in the hypertensive range and did not know it …

### 3. Project Description — 1,140 words

Salud en Casa will place two community health workers (0.75 FTE each) in Socorro and Valencia counties for 2027. CHWs will make home visits to residents of Belen, Los Lunas and Socorro, hold monthly screening days at two partner sites — the Valencia Family Resource Center in Belen and the Rio Grande Senior Center in Socorro — and connect residents to primary care and Medicaid enrollment. Our goals for 2027: 1,200 residents screened; 300 residents with elevated blood pressure or glucose enrolled in 6-month CHW follow-up; 150 residents connected to a primary-care home …

### 4. Evaluation Plan — 520 words

We will track screenings, referrals and completed primary-care visits in our case-management system … Quarterly data reviews … An external evaluator will review blood-pressure and A1c changes for residents in follow-up … After 2027, we expect to sustain the CHW positions through Medicaid managed-care reimbursement for CHW services.

## Budget Narrative — 480 words (separate portal field)

**Personnel.** Two community health workers at 0.75 FTE each, at $32,000 per FTE: $48,000. **Fringe** at our standard rate of 22% of salaries: $10,560. **Mileage** for home visits, 12,000 miles at our policy rate of $0.45 per mile: $5,400. **Screening supplies** — blood-pressure cuffs, glucometers, test strips and A1c kits: $3,850. **Training** — New Mexico CHW certification for both CHWs: $2,600. **Interpretation** — 60 hours at $25 per hour for Spanish-language and Diné interpretation at screening days: $1,500. **Indirect costs** are charged at 15% of total direct costs, our federal de minimis rate, which we apply to all our grants. Rio Grande Medical Supply is donating twenty home blood-pressure monitors and ten glucometers for residents in follow-up, valued at $12,000 …

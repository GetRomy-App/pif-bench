# Grant Agreement No. 2025-118

**Between** the Bergquist-Lund Foundation, 80 South Eighth Street, Suite 1450, Minneapolis, MN 55402 (the "Foundation")
**and** Northshore Adult Learning Center, 2105 West Superior Street, Duluth, MN 55806 (the "Grantee")

## 1. Grant, purpose and period

The Foundation grants the Grantee **$240,000** for *Pathways to Work*, an adult literacy and workforce-readiness program for adults in Duluth and the surrounding area. The grant period is **July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2027**. Year 1 is July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026; Year 2 is July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027. The grant is restricted to the purpose and budget in this agreement.

## 2. Payments

- $120,000 on signature of this agreement (Year 1).
- $120,000 for Year 2, on the Foundation's approval of the interim report.

## 3. Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Year 1 target | Year 2 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Enroll adult learners (unique learners attending at least one class during the year) | 120 | 130 |
| D2 | Learners completing 40 or more instructional hours during the year | At least 75% of enrolled learners | At least 75% of enrolled learners |
| D3 | Learners who complete 40 or more instructional hours and gain one or more educational functioning levels (EFL) on a standardized post-test | At least 60% of learners completing 40 or more hours | At least 65% of learners completing 40 or more hours |
| D4 | Volunteer tutors recruited who complete at least 12 hours of pre-service training | 25 | 30 |
| D5 | Meetings of the Employer Advisory Council | At least one in each quarter of the year (four) | At least one in each quarter of the year (four) |

A deliverable is met only if the result for the year reaches the target as defined above.

## 5. Budget changes

The Grantee may move funds between budget lines within a grant year without prior approval only so long as each line's actual expenditures for that year stay within 10% of the line's approved amount. **Any variance of more than 10% — above or below — between a budget line's actual expenditures for a grant year and its approved amount for that year requires the Foundation's prior written approval.** Requests are made by email to the program officer and are approved only in writing.

## 6. Allowable and unallowable costs

1. Grant funds may be used only for costs incurred during the grant period that are necessary for the program and fall within the approved budget lines.
2. Food and non-alcoholic refreshments for learners, tutors and staff at instructional and training events are allowable.
3. Grant funds may not be used for: alcoholic beverages; lobbying or other attempts to influence legislation; fundraising events or the solicitation of contributions; costs incurred before July 1, 2025 or after the end of the grant period; or any single item of equipment costing more than $5,000.
4. A cost that is not allowable may not be reported as a grant expense. Any unallowable cost charged to the grant must be removed from the grant's accounts and paid from the Grantee's own unrestricted funds.

## 7. Reports

1. **Interim report** covering Year 1: due within **45 days after the end of Year 1**.
2. **Final report** covering the whole grant: due within **90 days after the end of the grant period**.
3. Each report presents results for every deliverable against its target; actual expenditures by budget line against the approved budget, with each line's variance in dollars and percent and an explanation of any variance of more than 10%; and copies of any written approvals under Section 5.

## 8. No-cost extension

The Grantee may request one no-cost extension of up to six months. The request must be made in writing and be **received by the Foundation at least 90 days before the end of the grant period**. Requests received later will not be considered.

## 9. Unspent funds

Year 1 funds not spent on allowable costs by the end of Year 1 carry forward to Year 2 for the same budget lines and are reported in the interim report. Any grant funds not spent on allowable costs by the end of the grant period (as extended, if an extension is granted) must be returned to the Foundation together with the final report.

# Ashby Creek Regional Food Bank — Financial Health Indicators Policy

Adopted by the Board of Directors 2023-03-21. Revised 2025-11-18 (resolution 2025-14). Applies to fiscal years ending on or after 2025-12-31.

## 1. Purpose

The board monitors three indicators once a year, after the audit is issued and the Form 990 is filed, so that it can judge the Food Bank's efficiency and resilience on a consistent basis from year to year. The finance committee reviews the indicators and reports to the full board.

## 2. Sources and general rules

2.1 **Sources.** Indicators are computed from the Form 990 filed for the fiscal year and the notes to that year's audited financial statements. Where a Form 990 line is named below, the figure is taken from that line; where an adjustment is named, its amount is taken from the audit notes.

2.2 **Gifts in kind are excluded.** "Gifts in kind" means contributed nonfinancial assets as reported in the audit notes — donated food and other goods, donated professional services, and donated use of facilities. Gifts in kind are removed from every revenue and expense figure used in these indicators, in whichever Form 990 line and functional column they are reported. The values assigned to donated food are set by a national per-pound convention and swing with donation volume; excluding them keeps the indicators about the Food Bank's own cash operations.

2.3 **Balance-sheet figures** are end-of-year figures (Form 990 Part X, column (B)).

2.4 **Rounding.** Report percentages to one decimal place, the cost to raise a dollar to the nearest cent, and months to one decimal place. Compare the rounded figure with the target.

2.5 **Definitions are applied as written.** Staff may comment on a definition, but the indicators are reported to the committee exactly as this policy defines them.

## 3. The indicators

### 3.1 Program expense ratio

> **Program expense ratio = (Program service expenses − gifts in kind expensed in program services) ÷ (Total functional expenses − all gifts in kind expensed)**

- Program service expenses: Part IX, line 25, column (B).
- Total functional expenses: Part IX, line 25, column (A).
- **Target: 80.0% or higher.**

### 3.2 Cost to raise a dollar

> **Cost to raise a dollar = Fundraising expenses ÷ Contributions**

- Fundraising expenses: Part IX, line 25, column (D), less any gifts in kind expensed in fundraising.
- Contributions: Part VIII, line 1h, less line 1g (noncash contributions), less every bequest and other distribution from a decedent's estate received in the year. Bequests are excluded because they are non-recurring and are not the product of the year's fundraising spending; the CFO reports them to the committee separately.
- **Target: $0.20 or less.**
- If the cost to raise a dollar exceeds **$0.25**, the committee also commissions an external review of fundraising costs before the next budget is adopted.

### 3.3 Months of operating reserve

> **Months of operating reserve = Operating reserve ÷ (Annual operating expenses ÷ 12)**

- Operating reserve: net assets without donor restrictions (Part X, line 27) less land, buildings and equipment, net (Part X, line 10c). Net assets with donor restrictions are never counted as reserve. All other net assets without donor restrictions count, whatever their source.
- Annual operating expenses: total functional expenses (Part IX, line 25, column (A)) less depreciation (Part IX, line 22, column (A)) less all gifts in kind expensed.
- **Target: 3.0 months or more.**
- If the reserve falls below 1.5 months, the treasurer notifies the full board within ten days.

## 4. Reporting

4.1 The CFO's office prepares a memo to the finance committee chair giving, for each indicator, the inputs with their sources, the result, the target, and whether the target was met.

4.2 For each indicator whose target is not met, management presents a corrective plan to the committee within 90 days.

4.3 The committee may publish an indicator externally (annual report, website, grant applications) only on the basis defined in this policy.

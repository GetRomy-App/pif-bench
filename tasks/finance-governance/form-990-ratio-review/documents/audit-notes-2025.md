# Ashby Creek Regional Food Bank — Notes to the Financial Statements (selected)

Years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024. Independent auditor's report (unmodified opinion) by Marrow Bennett CPAs, dated April 22, 2026. The statement of functional expenses in the audited statements agrees, line for line, with Form 990 Part IX for 2025. Notes 2, 4, 7 and 11–13 are not reproduced.

## Note 1 — Organization

Ashby Creek Regional Food Bank (the Food Bank) is a nonprofit corporation exempt under Internal Revenue Code section 501(c)(3). It acquires donated and purchased food, stores it in its warehouse at 2200 Freight Yard Rd, and distributes it through 74 partner agencies — pantries, shelters and meal programs — in six counties. Partner agencies pay a shared maintenance fee per pound of food received. The Food Bank also distributes food under a cost-reimbursement contract with the state.

Both items are reported in Form 990 Part VIII, line 1f, and line 1g. Donated food is valued at the national food-bank network's per-pound wholesale value ($1.84 per pound in 2025; $1.79 in 2024). Donated food distributed in 2025 was $14,180,000, reported in Part IX, line 24a; the remaining $70,000 increased donated-food inventory (Note 5). Donated legal services are valued at the firm's standard hourly rates and are expensed in legal fees — Part IX, line 11b, $104,000, of which $95,000 was donated and $9,000 paid.

Volunteers contributed about 61,000 hours in the warehouse in 2025. These services do not require specialized skills and are not recognized in the financial statements.

## Note 10 — Bequest

In August 2025 the Food Bank received a distribution of $600,000 from the Estate of Harriet M. Okafor. The bequest carried no donor restriction; it is reported in contributions (Form 990 Part VIII, line 1f) and in net assets without donor restrictions. The board has not designated it for any purpose. No other bequests or estate distributions were received in 2025 or 2024.

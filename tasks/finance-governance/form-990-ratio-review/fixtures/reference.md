**To:** Rosalind Achterberg, Chair, Finance Committee

**Date:** September 4, 2026 (for the September 15 meeting)
**Basis:** Financial Health Indicators Policy, rev. 2025-11-18 [documents/financial-health-indicators-policy.md]; Form 990 for 2025, filed 2026-08-11 [documents/form-990-2025-excerpt.md]; notes to the 2025 audited financial statements [documents/audit-notes-2025.md]

## Summary

| Indicator | 2025 result | Target | Met? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Program expense ratio | 78.3% | 80.0% or higher | **Not met** |
| Cost to raise a dollar | $0.22 | $0.20 or less | **Not met** |
| Months of operating reserve | 3.1 months | 3.0 months or more | **Met** |

Two of the three targets were missed. Under section 4.2 of the policy, management owes the committee a corrective plan for the program expense ratio and the cost to raise a dollar within 90 days. The cost to raise a dollar is below the $0.25 level that would require an external review of fundraising costs, and the reserve is well above the 1.5-month level that would require notice to the full board.

As the policy requires (section 2.2), every gift in kind is removed from the revenue and expense figures below: $14,180,000 of donated food distributed (program services) and $95,000 of donated legal services (management and general, inside Part IX line 11b) [documents/audit-notes-2025.md, Note 9].

The policy removes every bequest and estate distribution from the denominator because bequests are non-recurring and are not the product of the year's fundraising spending (policy section 3.2). The Okafor bequest is unrestricted and sits in line 1f.

**$866,300 ÷ $3,985,000 = $0.217, reported as $0.22** — above the $0.20 target. **Not met.** (Had the bequest been left in, the figure would have been $0.19 — which is why the policy separates it.)

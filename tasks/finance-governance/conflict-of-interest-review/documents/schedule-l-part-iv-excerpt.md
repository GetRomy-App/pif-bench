# Schedule L (Form 990), Part IV — Business transactions involving interested persons

Plain-language paraphrase of the IRS Instructions for Schedule L (Form 990), prepared for the Theatre by its Form 990 preparer, Pryce & Elwood CPAs, July 2026. Only the parts relevant to the Theatre are included. Where this paraphrase and the IRS instructions differ, the IRS instructions govern.

## Which return

An organization with a fiscal year uses the form for the calendar year in which its fiscal year **begins**. The Theatre's fiscal year from July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026 is reported on the **2025 Form 990**, due November 16, 2026. Transactions are tested on the payments made during that fiscal year.

## Interested persons for Part IV

For Part IV, an interested person includes:

1. a current or former officer, director, trustee or key employee listed in Form 990, Part VII, Section A (all of the Theatre's directors are listed there);
2. a **family member** of a person described in 1; and
3. an entity (other than a section 501(c)(3) organization) **more than 35% owned**, directly or indirectly, individually or collectively, by one or more persons described in 1 or 2.

The instructions list further categories that do not arise in the Theatre's transactions.

**Family member** means a spouse, ancestors, brothers and sisters (whole or half blood), children (natural or adopted), grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and spouses of brothers, sisters, children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren.

A **business transaction** includes a contract of sale, lease, license, insurance or the performance of services.

## Reporting thresholds

Report a business transaction between the organization and an interested person only if one of these is met for the tax year:

- **(a)** all payments during the tax year between the organization and the interested person exceeded **$100,000**;
- **(b)** all payments during the tax year from a **single transaction** between the organization and the interested person exceeded the **greater of $10,000 or 1% of the organization's total revenue** for the tax year (Form 990, Part VIII, line 12); or
- **(c)** compensation payments during the tax year by the organization to a family member of a person listed in Part VII exceeded $10,000.

(A separate joint-venture rule does not apply to the Theatre.)

## What is reported

For each reportable transaction: (a) the name of the interested person; (b) the relationship between the interested person and the organization; (c) the amount of the transaction; (d) a description of the transaction; and (e) whether the transaction involves sharing of the organization's revenues.

Reporting a transaction in Part IV discloses a business relationship. It is not, by itself, a finding of wrongdoing. Form 990, Part IV, line 28, asks the same question with the same thresholds; a "Yes" answer there requires Schedule L, Part IV.

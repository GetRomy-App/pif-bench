# Minutes — Regular Meeting of the Board of Directors

**Crescent Harbor Children's Theatre** · Thursday, February 12, 2026, 6:30 pm · Studio B, 40 Water St, Crescent Harbor
Approved by the board 2026-03-12.

**Present (11):** Helena Marsh (Chair), Benjamin Ashworth, Priya Ramaswamy, Lorraine Pettigrew, Thomas Aldana, Colette Brandt, Hugh Castellanos, Denise Fairweather, Naomi Sato, Omar Siddiqui, Jerome Whitaker.
**Absent (2):** Ingrid Halvorsen, Felix Moreau.
**Staff:** Rachel Gutierrez (Executive Director), Mara Quinlan (Director of Development).
**Recording:** Lorraine Pettigrew, Secretary.

A quorum was present. The Chair called the meeting to order at 6:32 pm.

## 1. Consent agenda

The minutes of January 8, 2026, and the December financial statements were approved by consent.

## 2. Executive Director's report

Ms. Gutierrez reported that ticket sales for *The Snow Queen* reached 94% of capacity and that the summer camp scholarship fund has received $41,000 toward its $60,000 goal.

## 3. Finance

Ms. Ramaswamy presented the second-quarter budget-to-actual report. No action was required.

## 4. Spring Gala (May 2, 2026) — vendor contracts

Mr. Ashworth presented the Gala Committee's bid tabulations and recommendations for audio-visual production and catering (see the bid tabulation in the board packet).

Before discussion, the Chair reminded directors of the conflict-of-interest policy and asked whether any director had a financial interest in any of the bidders. Ms. Ramaswamy disclosed that her brother, Arun Ramaswamy, owns BrightStage AV Services LLC, one of the three audio-visual bidders, as she had stated in her annual statement and in her email to the Chair of February 2. **No other director made a disclosure.**

### 4a. Audio-visual production

Ms. Ramaswamy answered one question about the scope of BrightStage's bid and left the room at 7:12 pm. The ten remaining directors discussed the matter and determined that Ms. Ramaswamy's interest is a conflict of interest under the policy. They compared the three written bids for the same specification — BrightStage AV Services LLC $18,250; Coastline Sound & Light $21,400; Meridian Event Tech $23,900 — and found that no more advantageous arrangement was available, that BrightStage's bid was the lowest, and that the contract is in the Theatre's best interest and fair and reasonable.

*Motion* (Pettigrew / Sato) to award the audio-visual contract to BrightStage AV Services LLC for $18,250. **Carried, 10–0.** Ms. Ramaswamy did not take part in the discussion or the vote and returned to the meeting at 7:24 pm.

### 4b. Catering

The Gala Committee recommended Harborview Provisions, the lowest of three bids for the same menu and bar specification for 320 guests: Harborview Provisions $52,900; Tidewater Catering Co. $55,750; Saffron & Sage Events LLC $68,400. Mr. Ashworth noted that Harborview catered the 2024 gala and that its references were good.

Mr. Aldana said he had attended several events catered by Saffron & Sage, that its food and service were "a cut above," and that the gala's major donors would notice the difference. Ms. Pettigrew noted that Saffron & Sage's bid was $15,500 above Harborview's. Mr. Castellanos said the gala is the Theatre's largest fundraising event and that quality matters.

*Motion* (Aldana / Castellanos) to award the catering contract to Saffron & Sage Events LLC for $68,400. **Carried, 6–5.**
- In favor: Aldana, Brandt, Castellanos, Fairweather, Siddiqui, Whitaker.
- Opposed: Ashworth, Marsh, Pettigrew, Ramaswamy, Sato.

## 5. Governance

Ms. Pettigrew reported that all thirteen 2026 annual conflict-of-interest statements have been received.

## 6. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 8:05 pm.

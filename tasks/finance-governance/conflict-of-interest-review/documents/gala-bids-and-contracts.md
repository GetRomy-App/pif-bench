# Spring Gala 2026 — requests for proposals, bid tabulations and contracts

Prepared by Mara Quinlan, Director of Development, for the Gala Committee and the board packet of February 12, 2026. Contract and W-9 details added by the Controller's office.

## Requests for proposals

Issued 2025-12-01 to six caterers and four audio-visual companies; written bids due 2026-01-09. Each RFP set one specification so the bids are comparable: for catering, a plated three-course dinner with the same menu and a standard bar package for 320 guests; for audio-visual, the same staging, sound, lighting and screen package.

|---|---|---:|---|
| Harborview Provisions | 2026-01-07 | $52,900 | Dana Kirkpatrick, Owner |
| Tidewater Catering Co. | 2026-01-09 | $55,750 | Luis Ferreira, General Manager |

Gala Committee recommendation (2026-01-29, 3–0): **Harborview Provisions** — lowest bid, catered the 2024 gala, good references.

## Contracts signed after the board's February 12 votes

**Saffron & Sage Events LLC** — catering agreement dated 2026-02-16; signed for the Theatre by Rachel Gutierrez, Executive Director, and for the vendor by Celeste M. Aldana, Managing Member. Price $68,400 for 320 guests; additional guests at $180 each; 30% deposit due on signing, balance due within 10 days of the event.

**BrightStage AV Services LLC** — production agreement dated 2026-02-18; signed by Rachel Gutierrez and by Arun Ramaswamy, Manager. Price $18,250; 50% deposit, balance after the event.

# Schedule A (Form 990), Part II — the public support test for 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) organizations

Plain-language paraphrase of the IRS Instructions for Schedule A (Form 990), Part II, prepared by Ferrante & Lowe CPAs (our Form 990 preparer) for the Council's 2025 return. It covers only the lines that apply to the Council. Where this paraphrase and the IRS instructions differ, the IRS instructions govern.

## Who uses Part II

An organization described in section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) — a charity that normally receives a substantial part of its support from a governmental unit or from the general public — completes Part II every year. The Council was recognized as a 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) organization in 1998. Its first five years ended long ago, so the line 13 box does not apply.

## The computation period

Part II covers the current tax year and the four tax years before it: for the 2025 return, calendar years 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, in columns (a) through (e), with the five-year total in column (f). Report amounts on the same overall accounting method the organization uses for its Form 990.

## Section A — Public support

**Line 1 — Gifts, grants, contributions and membership fees received.** Include contributions from individuals, businesses, trusts, estates, private foundations and other charities, and grants from governmental units. Do not include unusual grants (see below). Do not include amounts that are payments for goods or services the organization provides — those are gross receipts (line 12), even when a governmental unit pays them.

**Line 2 — Tax revenues** levied for the organization's benefit and either paid to or spent on its behalf.

**Line 3 — The value of services or facilities furnished by a governmental unit** to the organization without charge.

**Line 4 — Total** of lines 1 through 3.

**Line 5 — The 2% limit.** Enter the total of the amounts by which each person's contributions exceed 2% of the five-year total support on line 11, column (f).

- Work donor by donor. Add up each person's contributions included on line 1 for all five years; the part of that five-year total that exceeds 2% of line 11, column (f) is that person's excess. Line 5 is the sum of all persons' excesses and is entered in column (f) only.
- "Person" includes an individual, a corporation or other business, a trust, an estate and a private foundation. Contributions from a donor and from persons related to the donor (such as a spouse or a business the donor controls) are added together and treated as coming from one person.
- **Not limited:** grants from governmental units, and contributions from other organizations described in section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) (publicly supported charities such as community foundations). These are counted in full as public support — unless a donor earmarked the money through that organization for this one, in which case it is treated as the earmarking donor's contribution.

**Line 6 — Public support:** line 4 minus line 5.

## Section B — Total support

**Line 7** — the amounts from line 4.

**Line 8 — Gross income from interest, dividends, payments received on securities loans, rents, royalties, and income from similar sources.**

**Line 9 — Net income from unrelated business activities,** whether or not the business is regularly carried on.

**Line 10 — Other income.** Do not include gain or loss from the sale of capital assets (for example, a gain on the sale of securities).

**Line 11 — Total support:** lines 7 through 10.

**Line 12 — Gross receipts from related activities, etc.** Fees and other receipts from activities that carry out the organization's exempt purposes — admissions, program and workshop fees, fees for services, and payments from a governmental unit under a contract to provide services. These are reported on line 12 for information only; they are **not** part of total support or public support for this test.

## Section C — The percentage and the test

**Line 14 — Public support percentage for 2025:** line 6, column (f), divided by line 11, column (f), entered to two decimal places.

**Line 16a — 33⅓% support test.** If line 14 is 33⅓% or more, check the box: the organization qualifies as a publicly supported organization for the year.

**Line 17a — 10% facts-and-circumstances test.** If line 14 is less than 33⅓% but at least 10%, the organization can still qualify only by showing in Part VI that it meets the facts-and-circumstances test (a continuing program to attract public and governmental support, among other factors).

## Unusual grants

A substantial contribution from a disinterested person that is attracted by the organization's publicly supported nature, is unusual or unexpected in amount, and would by its size adversely affect the organization's status may be left out of both public support and total support, if it meets the other conditions in the regulations. The organization must decide this grant by grant and list the grants it excludes. The Council has made no such determination for any contribution in 2021–2025 (see the finance manager's notes).

# 2025 public support test — Schedule A, Part II

**To:** Corinne Vasquez-Hale, Treasurer
**Cc:** Tobias Wren, Executive Director; Nadia Brecht, Finance Manager
**Date:** September 18, 2026
**Re:** Whether the Council passes the one-third public support test on the 2025 Form 990

## The answer

**No — on the 2025 computation we fall short of the 33⅓% test.** Our public support percentage for 2021–2025 is **31.98%**, about **1.4 percentage points** below the line; public support of $1,174,028 is $49,839 short of one-third of total support ($1,223,867). To remain a publicly supported organization for 2025 we would have to rely on the 10% facts-and-circumstances test and explain it in Part VI.

The reason: under the 2% rule, each funder counts as public support only up to $73,432 over the five years, while every dollar counts in full toward total support. Marguerite Ostrander's $2,025,000 is 55% of our five-year total support.

## What is left out

- **Program service revenue, $454,400** — workshop fees ($118,500), guided trip fees ($79,900) and the Kettle County monitoring contract ($256,000). These are gross receipts from related activities, reported on line 12 and not part of public or total support. The county contract is a fee for services, not a grant.

## Recommendation

Ask Ferrante & Lowe to treat Ms. Ostrander's 2025 gift of $455,000 as an unusual grant. Taking it out of both public and total support would lift the percentage back above 33⅓% and avoid relying on the facts-and-circumstances test.

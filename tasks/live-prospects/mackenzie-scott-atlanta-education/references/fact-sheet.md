# MacKenzie Scott: verified fact sheet as of 2026-04-25

Judge-only ground truth. Every fact below was public on or before 2026-04-25. Anything first published after that date is excluded. Where sources give different figures, the range that counts as correct is stated. A report that leaves out a fact here does not contradict it. A report that states a fact not listed here does not contradict this sheet unless the sheet says that fact is false or that a list is complete.

Confidence: **High** means a primary source (SEC filing, Scott's own Yield Giving site or essays, or the recipient's own announcement). **Medium** means consistent reporting by major outlets with no primary document online.

## 2. Wealth: basis, holdings and net worth

**Basis.** Under the 2019 divorce settlement she received 25% of the couple's Amazon stock, about **19.7 million shares**. That was **about 4% of Amazon**: 19.7 million of the 494,656,015 shares outstanding on 2019-07-17 is 3.98%. She gave Bezos her interests in The Washington Post and Blue Origin, and he kept **voting control** of her shares through a voting agreement and proxy. Bezos's own holdings fell from 78,814,170 shares (Form 4, 2019-02-07) to about 59.1 million (Form 4, 2019-07-29). Her wealth today rests on the remaining Amazon shares and on the proceeds of shares she has sold.

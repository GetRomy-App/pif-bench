## 7. Disambiguation

The request's "Robert Smith, the private equity executive interested in STEM education for underrepresented minorities" is Robert F. Smith of Vista Equity Partners. Other public Robert Smiths — for example the musician Robert Smith of The Cure, or former U.S. Senator Robert C. Smith of New Hampshire — are different people; none of their biographies, gifts or assets belong to him.

## 8. Not graded — sources disagree or unverified

A report may state either version of these, or neither.

- **Vista's headquarters city.** Vista's contact page lists Austin first (401 W 4th St); its SEC Form ADV lists the principal office at 4 Embarcadero Center, San Francisco; the DOJ called it "San Francisco based". "Austin-based" and "San Francisco-based" are both acceptable.
- **Cornell 2022 amount.** Cornell and Vista say $15 million; Smith's website (robertsmith.com/philanthropy) says $25 million.
- **Split of the 2016 Cornell $50 million.** Not published by Cornell; Vista's "$20 million personal plus $10 million scholarships" and Fund II's $19.46 million grant are both consistent with the $50 million total.
- **Morehouse 2019 earlier gift.** Morehouse says $1.5 million; Smith's website says $1 million for scholars plus $560,000 for an outdoor study space.
- **Fund II title.** Vista says founding director and President; recent 990-PFs list him as Director.
- **Family.** Forbes (March 2026) lists him as married with 7 children; his wife is Hope Dworaczyk Smith (married 2015, per secondary sources). The children's names and the split between marriages are not in a primary source.

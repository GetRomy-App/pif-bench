## 7. How her vehicles take inquiries

- **Emerson Collective, Unsolicited Submission Policy** (archived 2026-04-21): "None of Emerson Collective or any of its employees accepts or considers unsolicited submissions of any kind, including original ideas, proposals, or creative works". Unsolicited submissions are not treated as confidential and "will become the sole property of Emerson Collective". Sources: https://www.emersoncollective.com/legal/unsolicited-submission-policy ; https://web.archive.org/web/20260421191143/https://www.emersoncollective.com/legal/unsolicited-submission-policy

- Inside Philanthropy (June 26, 2025): "Emerson has a strict no unsolicited submissions policy." Source: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant/grants-e/emerson-collective

## 9. Not established (not facts; do not grade a report as contradicting these)

- **Monumental stake size and proceeds:** no reliable source states the percentage she held at the December 2025 sale or what she received. "About 20% for about $1.44 billion" is an inference (20% × $7.2B), not a reported figure. Because of the earlier partial sales, it probably overstates the stake.
- **Whether Emerson has bought David Bradley's remaining minority interest in The Atlantic:** not confirmed. "Majority owner" is the documented status, and "owner" is not wrong.
- **Media-literacy giving:** no grant from Emerson Collective, Powell Jobs or Waverley Street to a media- or news-literacy organization was found. That is absence of evidence, not a fact.
- **The Texas Tribune's cumulative total for Emerson** (shown as $3,733,000 in a donor PDF last modified 2026-09-23): the document postdates the as-of date. It is not used here.

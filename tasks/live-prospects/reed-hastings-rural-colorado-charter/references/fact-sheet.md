# Reed Hastings — verified fact sheet, as of 2026-04-25

Judge-only ground truth. Every fact below was true and public on or before 2026-04-25. Each row gives the as-of date, the source and a confidence level (High = primary record; Medium = reputable reporting or a database built from a primary record). Events after 2026-04-25 are out of scope: a report written on that date cannot know them, and must not be marked wrong for leaving them out.

Two notes on reading a deliverable against this sheet:

- **Blinding.** Deliverables are blinded before grading: the word "Anthropic" appears as "[the assistant]". A deliverable that says Hastings sits on "[the assistant]'s board" or "[the assistant] PBC's board" is consistent with row 1.9.
- **Tolerances.** Section 7 lists statements that differ in wording or figure from this sheet but are *not* contradictions. Treat only a statement outside those tolerances as a contradiction.

## 7. Tolerances — these are NOT contradictions

- Netflix "founded in 1997" or "co-founded 1997"; "launched in 1998" (the service); CEO "since 1998" or "since 1999"; "stepped down as CEO in January 2023" or "in 2023."
- Calling him Netflix's "chairman," "outgoing chairman," or "chairman until the June 2026 annual meeting." Calling him "Executive Chairman" **for the period January 2023–April 2025** is fine; calling it his current title is not.
- Any net-worth figure from $3 billion to $8 billion with a date or source. Stating his Netflix stake as "about 1%" or "under 1%."
- Share counts reported before the split (e.g., 2 million shares given in January 2024; about 2.2 million held in trust afterward), as long as they are not mixed with post-split counts as if they were the same unit.
- Valuing the January 2024 gift at anywhere from $1.0 to $1.2 billion. Describing its recipient as SVCF or as "his donor-advised fund at SVCF" (Time and others call it the Hastings Fund).
- The City Fund described as "$200 million" or "about $200 million" from Hastings and Arnold together; the Hastings Fund's share given as $125 million.
- CSGF located in Denver or Broomfield, Colorado.
- Lone Rock's cost given as "$20 million+", "about $41 million" or "$80+ million in assets"; its acreage given as "about 2,000" or "2,100" acres.
- Bowdoin described as founded in 1794 or as having a "231-year" or "232-year" history.

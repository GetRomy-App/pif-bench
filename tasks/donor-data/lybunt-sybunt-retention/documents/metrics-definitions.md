# Lantern Hill Arts Center — Annual Giving Metrics: Definitions

Development Operations · rev. 2025-08 · These definitions govern every LYBUNT, SYBUNT and retention figure we report to staff or the board.

## 1. Fiscal years

Our fiscal year runs **July 1 to June 30** and is named for the calendar year in which it ends: FY2026 ran July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026, and FY2027 began July 1, 2026. A gift belongs to the fiscal year that contains its gift date.

In these metrics, **"this year"** means the current fiscal year to the report date (FY2027 to date), and **"last year"** means the most recent complete fiscal year (FY2026).

## 2. Who counts as a donor

- **We count households, not individual records.** Constituents who share a household ID count as one donor. A household gave in a fiscal year if any of its members has a countable gift dated in that year.
- **Organizations are not counted.** Companies, foundations, clubs and congregations are excluded from LYBUNT, SYBUNT and retention, whatever they gave.

## 3. What counts as a gift

A **countable gift** is a hard-credit gift to an individual, with status Posted. The following do not count:

- **Soft credits.** A soft credit recognizes someone for a gift another donor made — a gift to their peer-to-peer fundraising page, a spouse credit, the employee credit on a matching gift. It is never the soft-credited person's own gift.
- **Refunded gifts.** A gift with status Refunded does not count in any fiscal year, whenever the refund was made.
- **Employer matching gifts.** A match is the company's gift and is hard-credited to the company (which is excluded under §2). The employee receives a soft credit only.
- **Joint-account gifts count once, for the household** — whether the gift is recorded on one spouse with a soft credit to the other, or split between the two spouses' records.

## 4. Definitions

- **LYBUNT** ("last year but unfortunately not this year"): a household with at least one countable gift in FY2026 and none in FY2027 to date.
- **SYBUNT** ("some year but unfortunately not this year"): a household with at least one countable gift in the five fiscal years **FY2021 through FY2025**, none in FY2026, and none in FY2027 to date. A household whose last countable gift is dated before FY2021 is lapsed and is not a SYBUNT. No household is both a LYBUNT and a SYBUNT.
- **Donor retention rate** is reported for the most recent complete fiscal year: the number of households with a countable gift in both FY2025 and FY2026, divided by the number of households with a countable gift in FY2025, as a percentage to one decimal place. FY2027 is not used until it closes.

## 5. The report

Give the LYBUNT count and list, the SYBUNT count and list (households named by their members), and the retention rate with its numerator and denominator, and show how each figure was reached.
